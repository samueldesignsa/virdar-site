## One Insight

Keller is different from the DFW mid-cities corridor in one important way: the businesses here opened recently because the population grew recently. The owner of that IV clinic or yoga studio isn't a 20-year veteran running a mature book of business — they're 3–5 years in, still figuring out what their ceiling is, and their systems are the ones they started with. That means two things. First, they're more open to change — they haven't been doing it the same way for 15 years. Second, they don't know what they're missing yet. The pitch in Keller isn't "you're leaking revenue." It's "you're growing and you're about to hit a wall — here's what that wall looks like, and here's how you get ahead of it."
