# Walk-In Research — Tuesday, September 29, 2026
## Territory: Frisco / Prosper / Little Elm

Fresh territory. This corridor is affluent, fast-growing, and full of owner-operated businesses that depend on seasonal revenue. Q4 urgency is real here — dental insurance windows, HVAC pre-winter prep, year-end FSA/HSA spend, fall estimate reactivation. Businesses in this area are busy but often running without a follow-up system.

Route: Start in Frisco (Main Street / Legacy corridor), move north to Prosper, end in Little Elm. About 35 miles total.

---

## 1. Frisco Family Dentistry
**Address:** 2810 Eldorado Pkwy, Frisco, TX 75033
**What they do:** General dentistry, family practice, high patient volume area near Eldorado and DNT.
**What I noticed:** No online booking on their website — contact form only. Google profile shows they respond to reviews occasionally but not consistently (some 1-star reviews with no response in 60+ days). Dental insurance year-end window is October–December — this is exactly when patient recall matters most.
**Best hook:** "Most dental practices lose 20–30 patients a month who had benefits left and just never got a recall call in time. We help you automatically reach those patients in October and November before they lose their benefits. Takes about 2 weeks to set up."

## 2. Legacy Heating and Air

## 4. Premier Chiropractic of Frisco
**Address:** 5530 Warren Pkwy, Frisco, TX 75034
**What they do:** Chiropractic care, sports injuries, family wellness. Good location near major office parks.
