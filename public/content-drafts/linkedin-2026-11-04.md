Sunday at 6pm.

A couple in Southlake decides they want a private chef for Thanksgiving. It's the first weekend of November. They have 22 people coming, they're exhausted thinking about cooking for them, and they're ready to hire someone.

They find three personal chefs through Google. They fill out inquiry forms for all three.

Monday morning:
- Chef A: Automated reply at 6:04pm Sunday. Scheduling link included. Consultation booked for Monday at 2pm.
- Chef B: Reply Tuesday at 11am. "Thanks for reaching out! I'll send some info this week."
- Chef C: Thursday email, 400 words about their background.

The couple confirmed with Chef A before they even opened Chef B's reply.

Chef A didn't have better reviews. They had a system that responded while the client was still ready to say yes.

This is how the Thanksgiving season fills. Not through advertising — through whoever responded first while the decision was live.

If you run a personal chef service, catering company, or any appointment business — your window closes in the next 10 days.
