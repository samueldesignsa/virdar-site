## One Insight

The Irving/Las Colinas territory is worth making a recurring stop — probably once every 3–4 weeks. It's a different market character than the outer suburban ring. The businesses here are used to vendor conversations, the decision-makers are available, and the patient/client base has unusually high employer-plan insurance saturation. That means the FSA/HSA/year-end benefits hooks land with more precision here than in markets where coverage is patchier. A Coppell dentist or optometrist doesn't have to guess whether their patients have employer plans — they almost certainly do. The pitch becomes "your patients have this money expiring, here's how to reach them" instead of "some of your patients may have benefits." That specificity closes faster.

One category to watch in this territory: commercial cleaning companies. Irving has a dense concentration of office parks, light industrial, and medical offices. Janitorial contracts in that market are worth $1,500–$4,000 per month per building. The blog post published today gives you a reference point if you run into a commercial cleaning company owner in the Irving or Las Colinas corridors. The hook: "October is when commercial cleaning contracts up for Q4 renewal — office managers and property managers are making decisions right now. Are you reaching those prospects before they sign with someone else?"
