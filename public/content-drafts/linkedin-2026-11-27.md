Today is Black Friday.

For every independent business that had a great day today — full parking lot, long lines, customers you've never seen before — there's a simple question worth sitting with tonight:

Of the people who walked in today, how many will come back in January?

Not the regulars. The new ones. The people who chose you over the chain because someone recommended you, or because they were specifically looking for local.

Most of them won't come back unless someone reaches out. Not because the experience wasn't good — it was. Because the routine didn't form. Life moved on. The holiday energy faded. Nobody reminded them you exist.

The shops that convert a great Black Friday into a full January are the ones with a system: the email captured at checkout, the follow-up that goes out in three weeks, the gift card that brings the recipient in after Christmas.

If you don't have that system yet, the window to build it for January is roughly the next two weeks.

After that, you're doing it for February.
