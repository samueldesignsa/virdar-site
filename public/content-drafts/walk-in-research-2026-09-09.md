Mid-cities north of Fort Worth, between the Alliance corridor and the HEB area. Keller skews affluent — owner-operated businesses with strong customer bases and minimal vendor competition. NRH has density. Watauga fills the gap between them. None of these have been in the recent walk-in cycle.

## Target Businesses

### 1. Keller Orthodontics or any independent ortho practice — Keller Pkwy corridor
**What they do:** Orthodontic treatment — braces, Invisalign, retainers. Keller has several independent practices serving the fast-growing suburban families between Southlake and Alliance.
**What to notice:** Ortho practices have two natural reactivation windows: the student who finished treatment and needs a retainer check 6 months out, and the sibling who's now old enough for an evaluation. If the practice has no active outreach to former patients about either, that's the hook. Check Google reviews — under 60 is an easy target.
**Hook:** "Your finished patients are your best referral source, and their siblings are your next patient pipeline. Is anything reaching out to former patients at the retainer-check interval, or does the relationship end when the braces come off?"

---

### 2. Independent Med Spa or Aesthetics Clinic — Keller or NRH
**What they do:** Botox, fillers, laser, body contouring. Keller has a growing aesthetic market — affluent families, younger demographic than Southlake but similar income profile.
**What to notice:** Check Instagram. If the account has 500–2,500 followers but spotty posting and no consistent booking CTA, that's a business that's found an audience it hasn't converted into recurring appointments. Injectable clients need a touchup every 3–4 months. Ask how many are being proactively contacted at that interval.
**Hook:** "Your Botox clients are due back in 3 to 4 months. Are they getting a message at that interval, or is reappointment mostly inbound when they remember to call?"

### 3. Independent Family Dental Practice — NRH Boulevard 26 or Rufe Snow Dr
**What they do:** General dentistry, maybe cosmetic add-ons. NRH has several owner-operated family practices between the commercial corridors.
**What to notice:** December 31 dental benefit expiration is the cleanest Q4 hook in dentistry. 113 days away as of today. If the practice has patients who haven't been in for 12 months with benefits still on the table, that's a warm list nobody is working.
**Hook:** "You've got patients with benefits that expire December 31 — that's 113 days. How many of those patients is someone actively contacting right now? If the answer is 'we send postcards,' I can show you what a text-based recall sequence gets you versus that."

### 4. Independent Physical Therapy Clinic — NRH or Keller
**What they do:** Orthopedic rehab and sports medicine. Keller/NRH has a high athletic youth population — tournament sports kids aging out of club programs plus the adult weekend warrior crowd.
**What to notice:** September is the start of the fall sports injury season — Friday Night Lights, cross country, volleyball, and club soccer all running simultaneously. If the clinic has staff and availability, the fall patient surge is about to hit. The question is whether discharged patients from spring are being reached.
**Hook:** "Your discharged patients from spring sports injuries are back in fall sports right now. They're the most likely people in your market to need PT again — ACL rehab, shoulder impingement, shin splints. Is anything reaching out to those patients before they search Google for a clinic?"

### 5. Independent Martial Arts School — Keller or NRH
**What they do:** Karate, BJJ, taekwondo — mostly kids programs with adult tracks. Several independent schools in strip centers along Rufe Snow and Keller Pkwy.
**What to notice:** August-to-September is the trial class spike for martial arts. Families are thinking about after-school activities after summer. If the school ran any summer promotion or got walk-ins in August, those trial leads are the first target. Also ask about summer pauses — kids who took a break and haven't been contacted about coming back.
**Hook:** "How many trial classes did you have in August? And how many of those families got followed up with more than once? The gap between trial-to-enrollment is almost always a follow-up problem, not a pricing problem."

### 6. Independent Auto Repair Shop — Watauga or NRH commercial strip
**What they do:** General auto repair — oil changes, brakes, tires, engine work. Owner-operated shops on the Rufe Snow or Denton Hwy corridors serve a working-class to middle-class vehicle mix.
**What to notice:** Oil change customers are the highest-frequency repeat visit in auto repair. A customer who comes in for a service interval and doesn't get a follow-up when that interval elapses is a customer waiting for someone else to remind them. Also: any estimate for larger work (engine, transmission, suspension) that went cold is on the table.
**Hook:** "Your oil change customers are due back every 3 to 5 months. What's bringing them back in — is anything reminding them, or is it mostly they remember when the light comes on and they search for whoever's convenient?"

### 7. Independent Chiropractic Practice — Keller Pkwy or NRH
**What they do:** Spine, musculoskeletal care. Keller families with athletic kids and adult patients dealing with desk-job posture issues. Several independent practices operate between Keller and NRH.
**What to notice:** Fall is when chiropractic businesses start seeing new patients from fall sports and back-to-school stress patterns. If the practice has a list of lapsed patients from spring and summer who finished a care plan, those are the warm leads. December insurance resets also make Q4 a natural push for chiro — many patients have met deductibles and can come in for minimal cost.
**Hook:** "Your patients who finished a care plan in the spring have probably had a different injury or a different complaint by now. And they know you. Is there anything reaching out to your discharged patient list right now, or does reactivation just happen when they call?"

---

### 8. Independent Salon or Blow-Dry Bar — Keller Main St district
**What they do:** Hair services — cuts, color, blowouts. Keller's Main Street has a walkable small-business district with independent salon suites and full-service shops.
**What to notice:** Color clients are the highest-frequency ticket in salons — every 6 to 8 weeks. If the salon's rebooking is entirely manual (front desk books at checkout), ask what percentage of clients actually rebook before leaving. The ones who don't are the drift pool.
**Hook:** "Your color clients are due back every 6 to 8 weeks. What percentage of them rebook before they leave? And the ones who don't — is anything reaching out to them at the right interval, or do they come back when they get around to it?"

---

### 9. Insurance Agency — NRH or Keller
**What they do:** Personal lines and small business insurance. Several independent agencies operate in this corridor — one to three producers, owner-operated.
**What to notice:** Independent agencies have a powerful underutilized asset: a client book that trusts them. The annual renewal conversation is the bare minimum. A proactive referral system — timed asks to satisfied clients — can generate significant new business from relationships already built.
**Hook:** "Your clients who've been with you for 3 or more years are your most credible referral source. Is there anything that systematically asks them for a referral at the right moment, or does new business come mostly from people calling in cold?"

---

### 10. Window Cleaning or Residential Service Company — any visible van on the route
**What they do:** Residential and commercial window cleaning, or similar exterior cleaning service. These companies are rarely in offices — look for vans, fleet signage, or an address listed on Google as a home-based business.
**What to notice:** September is the front end of the fall booking season. Residential clients who had windows done in the spring are natural fall return bookings. If the company is running on word of mouth and inbound calls only, there's a working customer list sitting idle.
**Hook:** "Fall window cleaning season opens in October. Your spring customers are your warmest leads right now. Is anything reaching out to them, or does the return booking mostly happen when they remember to call?"
