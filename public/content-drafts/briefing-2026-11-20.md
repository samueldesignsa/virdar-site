Their entire business is built on the idea that having a system beats hoping things work out. When Beverly walks into an organizer's business and says "you help people build systems for their physical space — we do the same thing for the customer side of your business," the analogy lands. It's one of the cleaner pitches in the whole walk-in playbook.
