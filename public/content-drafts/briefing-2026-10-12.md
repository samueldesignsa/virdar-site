The Legacy West / Plano corridor is the highest-value walk-in territory in the DFW portfolio. The concentration of corporate campuses means the patient base at every practice in a 5-mile radius is employed by a major company, benefits-eligible, and benefit-aware. One good conversation with a dental or PT practice owner in this corridor — "your patients have $1,200 in FSA money expiring and they're in a decision window right now, and here's what proactive October outreach actually produces" — can close fast. This territory is worth returning to once a month through December.
