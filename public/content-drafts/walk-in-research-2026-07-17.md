## One Observation

Wylie and Sachse have a specific characteristic worth noting: rapid population growth outpaced by business development. The residential density is there — the commercial infrastructure is still catching up. What that means for Beverly: the independent business owners she'll meet here are often running practices they started 2–4 years ago into a customer base that's growing faster than their capacity to reach it. They're not struggling for patients; they're struggling to keep up with the ones they have while also finding the ones they're losing. The pitch that lands here isn't "let us help you grow" — it's "let us help you stop losing the ones that are already yours." The patient who visited in February and hasn't been back. The inquiry from last month that nobody followed up. The appointment that drifted and wasn't rescheduled. That's where the money is, and it's right there in their system.
