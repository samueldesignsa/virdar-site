## One Insight

Ellis County is a different market dynamic than the North Dallas corridors. In Frisco, Southlake, or McKinney, business owners have been sold to by every vendor in DFW — they're skeptical of the first two minutes of any sales conversation. In Waxahachie and Midlothian, the vendor saturation is lower. The owner who runs a 3-truck HVAC company or a four-operatory dental practice in Ellis County has probably never had an AI automation conversation before. That changes the pitch structure: don't lead with credibility ("we work with a lot of businesses like yours") — lead with the specific problem and the specific October timing. The year-end insurance and FSA hooks are genuinely time-sensitive, and in a market where the owner hasn't been worn down by constant vendor contact, a well-timed specific hook converts differently. Today's territory is low-competition, high-potential.
