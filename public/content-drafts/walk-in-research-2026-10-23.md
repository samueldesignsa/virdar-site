# Walk-In Research — Friday, October 23, 2026
## Territory: Plano (Central / Legacy) / Richardson / Garland

Plano is the corporate employment anchor for all of North Dallas. AT&T, Toyota Financial Services, JPMorgan Chase, Frito-Lay, and Dr Pepper Snapple Group have major campuses here, collectively employing tens of thousands of people with strong employer benefit packages — dental, vision, FSA, and high-deductible health plans. These employees live locally and use the independent practices along Spring Creek Parkway, Park Boulevard, and the Legacy corridor. The demographic is nearly identical to Frisco in income and benefit structure, but the territory is more densely concentrated and closer in.

Richardson's Telecom Corridor is a distinct submarket: Ericsson, Samsung Electronics America, Nokia, and Fujitsu have North American headquarters here, and the employee base is largely engineers and tech managers on HDHP plans. UT Dallas is a constant source of young tech workers filtering into the local patient population. The independent practices on Campbell Road and Belt Line Road serve this population directly.

Garland is high-value undervisited territory. Working-class and middle-income, with strong teacher and trade-union benefit coverage through Dallas ISD, Garland ISD, and employer plans from the logistics, hospitality, and retail employers in the area. Garland does not respond to soft pitches or finesse language. Lead with math.

**The October hooks apply here:**
- **FSA/HSA**: Corporate employees at AT&T, JPMorgan, and the Legacy corridor companies are benefits-sophisticated. Many have maxed their FSA and know the December 31 deadline. The optometry and dental practices serving this demographic can convert patients on a specific "here's what you have left and here's what we have open" message.
- **Year-end deductible**: Tech workers on HDHPs in the Richardson Telecom Corridor have real deductibles ($1,500–$3,000 individual, $3,000–$6,000 family). Anyone who had significant care earlier in 2026 has paid toward that deductible — and it resets January 1. October is the last month the math is a genuine incentive.
- **Holiday booking window**: Aesthetics and dental cosmetic work for Plano/Legacy professional women follows the same pattern as Frisco — corporate holiday events, school concerts, entertaining. The booking window for work that needs to settle before December is closing.

**Friday note:** Plano practices are fully staffed on Fridays. Legacy corridor businesses run 9am–5pm with owner-operators accessible before the noon rush. Garland independent businesses are often most accessible Friday morning before weekend-prep traffic. Arrive early in each submarket.

## 1. Independent Dental Practice — Central Plano
**Address:** Look for owner-operated general or cosmetic dental practices along Spring Creek Parkway between Coit Road and Preston Road in Plano TX 75023, or near the Parker Road and US-75 interchange in Plano TX 75074. The Spring Creek corridor runs through an established Plano residential neighborhood; the Parker Road corridor has a high density of independent medical and dental practices serving the east Plano population close to AT&T's campus.
**What they do:** Full-service general and cosmetic dentistry — exams, cleanings, Invisalign, crowns, implants, whitening — for a patient base with access to strong employer dental plans. AT&T employees carry Cigna PPO plans with $2,000 annual maximums; JPMorgan Chase provides Delta Dental at $1,500–$2,000 per member; Frito-Lay and Dr Pepper Snapple employees carry PepsiCo benefits with solid dental coverage. The average Plano dental practice in this corridor has a patient base where 30–40% are covered by one of these corporate plans.
**What I noticed:** Central Plano dental practices are running the same October window as Frisco — open treatment plans from earlier in 2026, end-of-year benefit expiration, holiday cosmetic timing — but the territory has seen less walk-in outreach and fewer practices have active campaigns running. A practice that contacts its open-estimate patients in October with "your Cigna or Delta benefit resets January 1 — here's what you have available and here's what we have open in November" is having a conversation that most practices in this corridor are not having yet. The AT&T and JPMorgan employees in this patient base know exactly what their benefits are and will act on a specific message.
**Best hook:** "Your patients here — AT&T, JPMorgan, Frito-Lay employees — have strong Cigna and Delta Dental plans with real annual maximums. There are probably patients in your system with open treatment estimates from earlier this year who haven't scheduled. The practices that reach those patients in October with 'your benefit expires December 31 — here's what you have available and here's what we have open in November' fill the schedule from their own list. Are you reaching your open-estimate patients right now with anything specific, or sending the standard year-end reminder in November when everyone else does the same thing?"

## 2. Chiropractic Practice — Richardson (Campbell Road or Belt Line corridor)
**Address:** Look for owner-operated chiropractic practices on Campbell Road between US-75 and Greenville Avenue in Richardson TX 75080, or on Belt Line Road near Coit Road in Richardson TX 75080. The Campbell Road corridor runs directly through the Telecom Corridor employment zone — Ericsson's North American headquarters is on Telecom Parkway, Samsung Electronics America's head office is on Greenville Avenue, and Nokia operates major offices in the same area. The employees who live in Richardson, Plano, and north Garland use the practices on these corridors.
**What they do:** Chiropractic care — adjustments, spinal decompression, soft tissue therapy, sports rehabilitation — for the tech-worker and corporate employee population of Richardson. A significant share of the patient base is desk workers (software engineers, network engineers, project managers, corporate finance) with repetitive-stress neck and back issues. The younger UT Dallas-adjacent demographic adds students and recent graduates in the 22–35 range.
**What I noticed:** Richardson chiropractic practices have a Q4 conversion opportunity that most are not working. Tech workers in the Telecom Corridor are predominantly on employer-provided HDHPs — high-deductible health plans — with family deductibles in the $3,000–$6,000 range. A patient who came in for 8 visits in Q1 for a neck injury has paid significant deductible that resets January 1. If that patient is managing residual tightness or a secondary issue they've been ignoring, October is the last month the "your deductible is already paid — now is the least expensive time to come back in" argument is a real incentive rather than a November panic message. The practices that reach lapsed patients in October with specific numbers convert at a higher rate than the ones who call in December after the schedule is compressed.
**Best hook:** "A lot of your patients here work at Ericsson, Samsung, Nokia — they're on high-deductible health plans and a lot of them have paid toward their family deductible this year. That resets January 1. October is when the math actually works as an argument for coming back in: 'You've already paid toward your deductible — getting that nagging issue addressed now costs less than it will in February.' Are you reaching your lapsed patients right now with that specific message, or mainly waiting for them to call when something hurts badly enough?"

## 3. Medical Spa or Aesthetics Clinic — Plano Legacy / Dallas North Tollway Corridor
**Address:** Look for owner-operated medical spas or aesthetic clinics along the Dallas North Tollway between Legacy Drive and Spring Creek Parkway in Plano TX 75024, or on Legacy Drive west of the Tollway in Plano TX 75024. The Legacy corridor is the commercial core of Plano's corporate campus district — Toyota Financial Services, JPMorgan Chase, Fidelity Investments, and Liberty Mutual have major presences here. The med spa client base in this corridor is the professional woman demographic working at or adjacent to these employers.
**What they do:** Medical aesthetics — Botox, filler, laser resurfacing, body contouring, HydraFacials, medical-grade peels, gift card retail — for the dual-income professional household in the Legacy area. The client base skews 28–55, with access to employer FSA plans, and a social calendar that includes corporate holiday events at the Legacy corridor companies. Toyota Financial, JPMorgan, and Fidelity all host large December holiday events; their employees are booking aesthetic appointments in October.
**What I noticed:** The Plano Legacy med spa market is the closest analogue to Frisco in the metro, but it's often treated as an extension of the broader "North Dallas" territory rather than a distinct market with its own drivers. The FSA triple — balances expiring December 31, Botox booking window closing before November, gift card season opening now — applies identically here. The JPMorgan Chase and Fidelity Investments employees in this patient base are financially organized, know their FSA deadline, and will act on a specific offer. A med spa with 350 active clients in this corridor doing no October outreach is leaving the same $35,000–$70,000 holiday window open for whoever reaches their clients first.
**Best hook:** "Your clients here work at JPMorgan, Toyota Financial, Fidelity — these are people who max their FSA and know the December 31 deadline. Right now they have FSA balances expiring, a holiday Botox window that closes around November 1, and they're starting to shop for gift cards for December. That's three specific reasons to reach your active list this month, and all three expire on different dates. Are you reaching them with any of it, or planning to send something in November when two of the three windows are already closed?"

## 4. Independent Optometry Practice — Garland (Buckingham Road corridor)
**Address:** Look for owner-operated optometry practices on Buckingham Road between Garland Road and Jupiter Road in Garland TX 75043, or near Forest Lane and Northwest Highway in Garland TX 75234. Garland's Buckingham Road corridor runs through an established working-class and middle-income residential neighborhood. The Garland ISD and Dallas ISD teacher population in this area carries VSP coverage through the district. Garland-area trade and service workers carry EyeMed through employer plans at retail, logistics, and hospitality companies.
**What they do:** Full-service optometry — eye exams, frames, contact lens fitting, pediatric vision — for the Garland family market. The patient base includes Dallas ISD and Garland ISD teachers with VSP plan coverage, working families with EyeMed through employer plans, and the Garland Hispanic community, which represents a large share of the residential population in the western Garland zip codes.
**What I noticed:** The independent optometry advantage in Garland is the same as in Allen or Plano, but the pitch needs to be calibrated differently. This demographic is price-conscious, not benefit-sophisticated — they understand "$150 exam benefit expiring December 31" more readily than "your VSP plan year ends." The independent OD in Garland who reaches their patient list in October with a specific, plain-language benefits message is competing with LensCrafters and Walmart Vision for the same patients — and the independent OD has the relationship advantage that chains don't have. The patients who haven't had their annual exam this year are leaving real dollar value on the table; a $350 combined exam-and-frame benefit is meaningful money for a Garland teacher or service worker household.
**Best hook:** "Your patients have vision benefits expiring December 31 — Dallas ISD and Garland ISD teachers on VSP, working families on EyeMed. A lot of them haven't had their annual exam this year, and they'll use that benefit somewhere before it expires. The independent ODs who reach their patient list in October with a specific 'your benefit expires December 31 — here's what it covers and here's what we have open' message fill November from their own regulars. The chains are already sending automated reminders. Are you reaching that list right now, or competing for those same patients in November?"

## 5. Personal Training Studio or Fitness Boutique — Richardson / Plano Border
**Address:** Look for owner-operated personal training studios or boutique fitness (HIIT, Pilates, barre, strength coaching) on Campbell Road near Alma Drive in Richardson TX 75080, or on Alma Road near Campbell Road in Plano TX 75025. The Richardson/Plano border on the Campbell Road corridor is a high-density residential area with a strong concentration of young tech and corporate professionals from the adjacent Telecom Corridor. The demographic is 28–45, physically active, motivated by performance and body composition.
**What they do:** Personal training and group fitness for the active professional population of Richardson and east Plano. The client mix typically includes tech workers from the Telecom Corridor, UT Dallas faculty and staff, and corporate professionals from the Plano employer base. Boutique fitness in this corridor competes primarily on coaching quality and convenience rather than price.
**What I noticed:** October is a distinct conversion window for boutique fitness that most studios underuse. New Year's resolution memberships peak in January — but the clients who start in January and stay through February are a fraction of the clients who start in October and build the habit before the holiday disruption. A client who establishes a twice-weekly training routine in October keeps it through November and December because it's already scheduled; a client who starts January 1 is fighting the holiday aftermath plus winter schedule friction. Studios that reach out to inquiries who never converted, past clients who dropped off after summer, and corporate referral contacts in October get better clients — higher retention, lower churn — than the ones who wait for the January rush.
**Best hook:** "The clients who stick with personal training are the ones who build the habit before the holiday season disrupts it. Someone who starts in October and establishes the routine by November keeps it through December — the ones who start January 1 hit their first missed week in February and many of them don't come back. Do you have a list of people who inquired but didn't start, or past clients who fell off after summer? That's the list worth reaching right now, before the New Year's crowd fills your schedule with clients who have a much lower retention rate."

## Route Notes

- **Start time:** 9am. Begin in the Plano Legacy/DNT corridor — med spa first, accessible from 9am. Take the Dallas North Tollway north from Dallas or exit Legacy Drive from the Tollway.
- **9:00–9:45am:** Med spa — Legacy/Tollway corridor. FSA + holiday prep triple hook; lead with numbers.
- **9:45–10:30am:** Dental — Spring Creek Parkway or Parker Road corridor. Practice managers accessible before mid-morning volume.
- **10:30–11:15am:** Veterinary clinic — Park Boulevard corridor. Mid-morning is accessible before the noon appointment block.
- **11:30am–12:15pm:** Drive north on US-75 to Richardson (20 minutes). Chiropractic on Campbell Road or Belt Line.
- **12:30–1:15pm:** Personal training studio — Campbell/Alma border. Studios often have a mid-afternoon lull between morning and evening classes.
- **1:30–2:30pm:** Drive south on US-75 or Garland Road to Garland (25–30 minutes). Optometry on Buckingham Road or Forest Lane.
- **Total drive:** Under 60 minutes for the full route. US-75 is the north-south spine connecting all three cities; the Tollway connects Legacy to Central Plano efficiently.

**Tone calibration:**
- **Plano Legacy/DNT corridor:** Polished, fast-moving. Lead with numbers, skip the setup. JPMorgan and Toyota Financial employees are financially organized and respond to specifics. "Your FSA clients have $740 to spend before December 31" is a better opener than any narrative.
- **Richardson Telecom Corridor:** Efficiency-minded tech workers and practice owners. Skip the soft sell. Lead with the deductible math — they understand it and will engage with the specifics.
- **Garland:** Plain and direct. No narrative warm-up. "Your patients have $350 in vision benefits expiring December 31" is the whole opener. This demographic does not have patience for a pitch. Give them the number first, earn the conversation second.
