# Walk-In Research — Thursday, October 1, 2026
## Territory: Richardson / North Plano / Murphy

Richardson and North Plano are mature, high-density suburbs with a strong mix of established owner-operated businesses — many of them in the 5–15 year range, enough history to have a real customer database, and enough competition to feel the gaps. Murphy is a smaller city just east of Plano on the Collin County line, growing fast, with less saturation on the walk-in side. Good Thursday territory: less foot traffic from sales reps, owners are more available mid-week.

Route: Start in North Plano (Spring Creek Pkwy / Legacy area), east on Campbell Rd through Richardson (US-75 corridor), end in Murphy/Wylie. About 30 miles total.
