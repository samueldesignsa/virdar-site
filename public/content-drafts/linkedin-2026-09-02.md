# LinkedIn Post — Wednesday, September 2, 2026

**Topic:** The list you already have is your best September marketing.

**Post:**

---

A dental practice in north Texas had 47 patients who came in for cleanings in January and February. None of them had been back. None of them had gotten a message.

Their September schedule had openings. Their front desk was calling new patients to fill them. Nobody was reaching the 47 people who were due right now, whose insurance benefits expire December 31, and who were waiting for someone to tell them it was time.

One message went out: "Your last cleaning was in January — you're due for your six-month visit, and your insurance benefits reset at year-end. We have September openings and wanted to reach you before the fall schedule fills."

31 responded. 22 booked.

That's a full week of hygiene appointments, from a list they already owned, in four days.

The businesses filling their September schedules right now aren't outspending their competitors. They built a system that reaches the right customer at the right moment — before that customer goes looking for someone else.

The list is not a marketing problem. It's a timing problem.
