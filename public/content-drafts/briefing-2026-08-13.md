Five revenue problems covered:

1. **Quote Response Speed:** Families contact 5–7 movers simultaneously during peak season, book with whoever responds first. After-hours requests (submitted 7–11pm weeknights) sit unanswered until the next morning. **45 requests/month × improved booking rate from automated response = $9,800 in additional peak season monthly revenue.**

2. **Confirmed Booking Dropout:** Verbal yes, no deposit, no follow-up sequence = 21% soft cancellation rate on peak-season bookings. Deposit confirmation and reminder sequence recovers booked slots before they're lost. **8 additional secured bookings per peak season × $1,400 = $11,200 recovered.**

3. **Post-Move Review Capture:** The 12–48 hour post-move window is the only time customers write reviews at high rates. Without a review request, 13% of customers leave a review (skewed negative). With automated next-day text and direct link: 35% response rate from satisfied customers. **78 additional reviews/year — meaningful ranking improvement in local search without advertising.**

4. **Waitlist and Cancellation Backfill:** A peak-season Saturday cancellation is the most expensive event in a mover's month. Automated waitlist notification fills slots the same day. **$2,800 in recovered peak season revenue from slots that would otherwise run empty.**

5. **Repeat Customer and Referral Recall:** The average American moves every 6–8 years. 300 completed moves = 300 households likely to move again in the next 5 years. Semi-annual outreach, 5% booking or referral rate. **$22,400 in annual revenue from customers who already trust the company — zero acquisition cost.**
