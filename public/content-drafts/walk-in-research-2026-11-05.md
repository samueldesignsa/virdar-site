# Walk-In Research — November 5, 2026

**Area: Lewisville / Flower Mound / Highland Village**

Mid-to-upper income Denton County corridor along FM 2499 and FM 407. Dense concentration of independent medical practices, dental offices, and professional services. Flower Mound in particular has a high per-capita concentration of independent practitioners — cardiologists, orthopedists, family physicians — who have been in business 10+ years and are the exact profile of an owner who understands ROI but hasn't been pitched by an AI automation vendor. This area hasn't been in recent rotation.

**Today is Thursday, November 5.** FSA/HSA deadline is 56 days away. This is the last full week before mid-November when urgency outreach starts to feel rushed. Lead with the year-end angle in every conversation — it's specific, timely, and gives the owner a concrete reason to move now rather than "think about it."

## 1. Flower Mound Family Dentistry

**What I noticed:** Dental practices in this corridor typically list "call to schedule" as their primary booking method — no online booking visible in most cases. The ones who've been putting off treatment are going to book somewhere in the next 6 weeks — the question is whether it's with you or whoever sent them a reminder. ## 3. Flower Mound Eye Care
**Address:** 2001 Morriss Rd Ste 100, Flower Mound, TX 75028 *(verify — independent optometry practices in this zip)*

## 4. Highland Village Physical Therapy
**Address:** 1000 Highland Village Rd Ste 125, Highland Village, TX 75077 *(confirm — several PT practices in the HV medical corridor)*
**What they do:** Outpatient physical therapy, independent practice or small group serving Highland Village and Flower Mound.
**What I noticed:** Physical therapy practices lose patients in two specific ways: (1) patients who finish their prescribed sessions but still have remaining FSA funds and would benefit from additional treatment never get asked, and (2) discharged patients with chronic conditions (back pain, shoulder instability, knee issues) who will need PT again next year never get a reactivation message.
**Best hook:** "Patients who've been discharged still have FSA money they can use for additional PT before December 31. The ones managing chronic conditions are going to need you again — the question is whether they book it this month or start over at a new practice in January. We build the follow-up system that keeps them coming back to you specifically."

## 5. Brazos Medical Group — Flower Mound
**Address:** 4455 Long Prairie Rd Ste 100, Flower Mound, TX 75022 *(verify — family medicine practices in this corridor)*
**What they do:** Independent family or internal medicine practice. Primary care group serving the Flower Mound/Argyle corridor.
**What I noticed:** Family medicine practices lose patients in a specific way in Q4: patients due for annual physicals who haven't scheduled. Most private practices have 200–400 patients who are overdue for a wellness visit that their insurance covers fully. Those patients aren't angry or leaving — they just haven't been reminded. This is a recoverable revenue conversation that the practice should own before December, when patient attention shifts entirely to holidays.
**Best hook:** "You have patients who are overdue for annual physicals that their insurance covers 100%. They're not leaving your practice — they just haven't been reminded. We send one outreach to your overdue list in November, before December makes it impossible to get attention. Practices similar to yours typically schedule 30–50 physicals in 3 weeks from one message."
