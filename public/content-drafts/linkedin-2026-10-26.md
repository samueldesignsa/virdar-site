**Topic:** Gift card campaign strategy for service businesses — why October is the window and what to actually say
**Target length:** ~175 words

Most service businesses treat gift cards as something to sell at the front desk.

The ones that do it right treat it as a campaign. There's a difference.

Here's the setup: gift card buyers start shopping in late October for December giving. The spa, dental office, or salon that sends a specific message — "give the gift of [X] this holiday season, order by November 30 and we'll include a complimentary [Y]" — reaches their own client base before the impulse gets spent somewhere else.

The math is simple. A med spa with 400 active clients where 8% buy gift cards at an average of $150: $4,800 in gift card revenue with a single targeted message. The same spa doing nothing earns $0 from that same pool.

October is when this gets started. November is when the window closes.

The service businesses that run a gift card campaign in October and November earn $4,000 to $15,000 they would have left on the table — from people who were already going to buy something for someone they know.

They just needed to know where to buy it.
