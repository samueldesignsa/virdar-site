## One Insight

The remodeling post today identified a gap that applies across nearly every contractor industry: **the dead proposal problem**. Most contractors give 15-20 estimates per month and close 3-5. The rest sit in a pile nobody follows up on. That number — 10 to 15 dead proposals per month at $20K+ average project size — is the easiest revenue framing in the whole sales conversation, because it's money the business already found and then lost. When you walk into any contractor business today, ask: "How many estimates did you give last month that haven't converted yet?" Whatever the number, multiply it by their average project size. That's the revenue sitting in follow-up sequences they don't have. It's a more visceral number than "improve your response time" and it closes faster.
