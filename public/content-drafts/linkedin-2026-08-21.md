**Topic:** The fall calendar fills in August, not September.

**Post:**

---

A dental practice owner told me last October: "We had a terrible September. Didn't see it coming."

I asked what her August looked like. She paused. "We didn't really do anything in August. We were waiting for school to start."

That's the pattern every year. The practices with full Septembers sent their recall messages in August. The ones with slow Septembers waited until they could see the empty slots and then wondered why the phone wasn't ringing.

Back-to-school is a planning mindset, not a season. For three weeks after school starts, parents are in list-checking mode. Dental checkup. Eye exam. Physicals. They're going to schedule those appointments — the question is whether they schedule at your practice or the one that showed up in their inbox while they were making the list.

Patients have four months of remaining insurance benefits. They have motivation right now they won't have in November. And they have six-month recalls sitting in your practice management software that nobody has turned into a message yet.

The September that fills in September is already lost. The one worth working is August.
