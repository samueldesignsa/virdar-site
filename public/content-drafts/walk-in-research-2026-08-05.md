## 9 Target Businesses

### 1. Independent Orthopedic or Sports Medicine Physician Practice — O'Connor Road, Irving
**What they do:** Orthopedic surgery or sports medicine practice near the Las Colinas medical office corridor. Likely sees a mix of sports injury referrals, workers' comp, and elective musculoskeletal cases.
**What to look for:** No automated after-hours inquiry response. New patient referrals that go to voicemail after 5pm. Ask: "When a parent calls on a Tuesday evening after a game injury, how does that inquiry get handled?" If it goes to voicemail and gets a callback the next morning, they're losing patients to whoever answers that evening.
**Hook:** "Irving High two-a-days started last week. The first ACL referral of the season went somewhere — was it here? The practice that confirms within 90 minutes of an evening call gets that patient. What does your after-hours inquiry process look like?"

## Key Hooks for Today

**Universal across every stop:**
- "Irving ISD starts August 12. That's next Tuesday. Every business with a customer or patient list should have a message going out this week. What does yours look like?"
- "Sports season opened last week. The first injury referral went somewhere. Was it here, or did it go to whoever answered faster?"

**Sports / medical angle (primary for this corridor):**
- Las Colinas has a significant concentration of independent physician practices, PT clinics, and chiropractic offices within a five-mile radius of major hospital systems. The independent practices that respond fastest keep the referrals. The ones that rely on established relationships without maintaining the pipeline lose patients to health system-owned practices.

**Corporate demographic advantage:**
- Las Colinas is uniquely dense with dual-income, high-education households. These are clients who will pay for quality and maintain relationships with businesses that stay in contact. The fitness studio, med spa, and insurance agency pitches all land harder here than in most other corridors.
