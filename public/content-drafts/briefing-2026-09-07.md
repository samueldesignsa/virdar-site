## One Insight

Pressure washing is an underserved vertical for Virdar outreach and worth targeting directly. Here's why:

The economics are clean: an independent two-truck operation with 150 past residential customers and 5–8 commercial relationships is sitting on $40,000–$60,000 in annual revenue that runs through unworked follow-up. The average pressure washer has no CRM, no automated outreach, and no review system. They are the textbook Virdar client.

The walk-in angle is strong: pressure washing owners are almost never in an office. They're on routes. But the owner of a two-truck operation in Rockwall or Heath does have a shop address or a home office where they store equipment. The better channel for this vertical may be direct outreach — LinkedIn, phone, or email — rather than a physical walk-in. If you find a pressure washing company on the walk today, the pitch is the same, but consider adding pressure washers to a direct outreach list for September. Their fall window closes in 6 weeks.
