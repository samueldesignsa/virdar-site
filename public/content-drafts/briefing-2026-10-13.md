## One Insight

Flower Mound and Highland Village are the highest household-income walk-in territory in the DFW portfolio — higher even than Legacy West, because the wealth there is residential rather than corporate. The implication for the pitch is different: at Legacy West, you're talking to a practice owner whose *patients* are benefit-loaded. In Flower Mound, you're talking to a practice owner whose *patients and the owner themselves* are both in that income bracket. Practice owners in this territory are financially sophisticated, used to working with vendors who know their market, and will respond well to a hook that's anchored to a specific dollar figure and a specific timing window. "Your patients have $1,000 in Delta Dental benefits expiring December 31 and October is when recall outreach converts in this market" is the right register. Generic AI pitches fall flat here. The more specific the hook, the shorter the path to a real conversation.
