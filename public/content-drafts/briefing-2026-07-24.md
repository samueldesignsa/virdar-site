**File:** `public/blog/ai-automation-for-pediatric-occupational-therapy.html`

Distinct from blog #53 (speech/OT combined) and blog #62 (pediatric PT). This post focuses on what is specific to pediatric OT: sensory processing, fine motor, ADHD/autism referral pipelines, IEP evaluation workflow, and home program compliance. Five problem areas with specific revenue math:

1. **Back-to-school intake surge (August 12-18 window):** Practices that respond to new inquiries within an hour during the surge week capture families who would otherwise book elsewhere. 3 additional evaluations per surge week × $750 = $2,250/week, recovering $10,400 over the 3-week surge including ongoing treatment conversion.

2. **IEP referral pipeline:** School districts refer to private OT for formal evaluations when district capacity is maxed — $600-900 per evaluation. Practices that acknowledge referrals within an hour (and close the loop with the school diagnostician) retain referral source relationships worth $18,000/year per active school contact.

3. **Home program compliance:** Weekly home program text prompts keep families doing the exercises between sessions, which drives faster outcomes and longer retention. Dropping early-discharge patients from 3 to 1 per month = $6,000/year. Secondary benefit: families whose children hit goals become the strongest referral sources.

4. **Lapsed patient reactivation:** 6-month check-ins to discharged families generate reactivation inquiries and sibling referrals. 96 discharged families at 15% response rate = 4-5 reactivations per year + 2 new patient referrals per quarter.

5. **Physician and teacher referral close loop:** Practices that send a brief acknowledgment when a physician's referral is scheduled — and a one-paragraph summary after evaluation — retain referral sources worth $45,450/year in sustained evaluation + treatment revenue.

The post ends with a clear "what it is not" section: not clinical decision-making, not a portal families have to log into, not independent of the therapeutic relationship.
