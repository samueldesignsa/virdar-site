# LinkedIn Post — Wednesday, July 30, 2026

---

A prospective client texts three personal trainers on a Sunday afternoon.

The first responds in 11 minutes.
The second responds Monday at 9am.
The third responds Tuesday.

The second trainer has better results, more reviews, and more experience.

Doesn't matter. The client booked with trainer one.

This is happening in every service business without an automated inquiry acknowledgment. Personal trainers. Chiropractors. Fitness studios. Hair salons. Dental offices. The person submitting a form at 7pm on a Sunday isn't loyal to any provider yet. They're loyal to whoever makes them feel acknowledged first.

You don't need to close the sale in 11 minutes. You need to keep the inquiry alive until you can personally respond.

"Thanks for reaching out — I'll follow up with you personally within the hour."

That's it. That single message holds the inquiry for 60–90 minutes while you finish your session, your appointment, your evening. The prospect stops contacting other providers because one already responded.

Most service businesses lose 30–40% of inquiries not to better competitors — to faster ones.

If you're losing people who contacted you first, check response time before you redesign your website.
