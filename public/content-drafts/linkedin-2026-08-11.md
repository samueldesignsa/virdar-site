School started today for most of North Texas.

Your customers' routines just reset.

Every habit they built over the summer — where they worked out, where they got their hair cut, which service provider they used because it was "convenient for the summer" — is up for re-evaluation right now. Not consciously. They're not sitting down to review their vendor list. But they're building a new fall schedule, and the businesses that show up in the next two weeks get slotted in. The ones that don't show up get dropped without a conversation.

This is the clearest window of the year for any business with repeat customers.

The boutique fitness studio whose summer members went sporadic — they need a "welcome back to routine" message today.

The dental practice whose patients have kids — those families are in scheduling mode right now, and overdue cleanings are on the mental to-do list.

The barber whose regulars visited every four weeks until July — they're overdue, and they'll rebook with whoever texts them first.

The window is this week. Two weeks from now, habits are set.

If you have a customer list and you haven't sent a message today, that's the thing to do.
