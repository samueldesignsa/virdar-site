# Walk-In Research — Friday, October 2, 2026
## Territory: Rockwall / Rowlett / Heath

Rockwall, Rowlett, and Heath are east of Dallas across Lake Ray Hubbard — a cluster of Rockwall County suburbs that are growing fast and have a strong mix of owner-operated businesses in the 3–10 year range. Rockwall has a developed commercial strip along Hwy 205 (S Goliad) and the I-30 frontage. Rowlett sits right on the Dallas county line with heavy residential density and a Lakeview Pkwy commercial corridor. Heath is newer, more affluent, and smaller — fewer walk-in targets but higher-average-revenue businesses. Last covered in this territory rotation: not recently. Good Friday territory — owners tend to be in on Fridays, slower foot traffic from other sales reps.

Route: Start in Rockwall (I-30 and Hwy 205 corridor), cut west through Heath on FM 740, end in Rowlett (Dalrock/Lakeview corridor). About 35 miles total, mostly I-30 and surface roads.

## 1. Rockwall Dental Group
**Address:** 1000 E Interstate 30, Rockwall, TX 75087
**What they do:** General dentistry — cleanings, restorations, cosmetics. Rockwall and Fate coverage.
**What I noticed:** October opens the strongest dental recall window of the year. Year-end benefits deadline is December 31, and patients who have been putting off appointments are now motivated. Practice profile shows scheduling capability but no visible indication of proactive outreach. Review pace has slowed — fewer than 5 new reviews in the last 90 days for what appears to be a multi-provider practice.
**Best hook:** "October is the start of the highest-leverage recall window dental practices see all year. Patients have benefits expiring December 31 that they'll lose if they don't use them — right now they're actually motivated to book. Are you doing anything proactive to reach patients who haven't been in since spring, or is it more inbound from here?"

## 2. Pro-Tech Heating & Air
**Address:** 205 S Goliad St, Rockwall, TX 75087
**What they do:** Residential HVAC — repairs, replacements, tune-ups. Rockwall and Heath area.
**What I noticed:** Fall is peak season for HVAC tune-up demand in North Texas. Homeowners who skipped the summer check-in are now getting their first cool nights and thinking about the furnace. After-hours inquiry capture is the most active gap right now — a homeowner who submits a contact form at 9pm is booking whoever responds first. Google profile appears to have solid ratings but a slowing review pace over the last few months.
**Best hook:** "October is your busiest inquiry window for tune-ups and early system replacements. How are you handling after-hours contact right now? If someone fills out your web form at 9pm because they turned the furnace on for the first time and something is off — do you have a process that gets back to them that night, or does it wait until morning?"

## 3. Lakeside Roofing & Construction
**Address:** 2605 Lakeview Pkwy, Rowlett, TX 75088
**What they do:** Residential roofing, gutters, and storm damage repair. Rowlett and Garland coverage.
**What I noticed:** October is the follow-up window for summer estimates. Roofing companies in this corridor gave dozens of quotes between June and August — homeowners who didn't pull the trigger immediately are now in the mindset to act before year-end. Also worth checking: Google profile review responses, which are often slow for smaller roofing companies in this area.
**Best hook:** "Quick question — how many open estimates do you have from this summer that haven't closed yet? October is when those homeowners actually decide. The roofing companies following up right now are closing jobs their competitors don't know are still available. If those estimates are sitting in a spreadsheet or your CRM, there's a straightforward way to reach all of them in one push."

## 4. Heath Aesthetics & Wellness
**Address:** 4000 FM 740, Heath, TX 75032
**What they do:** Med spa — injectables, skin treatments, body contouring. Heath and East Rockwall clientele.
**What I noticed:** Q4 is the most revenue-dense quarter for med spas. Patients want to look their best for the holidays, holiday parties, and family photos — and October is when they start booking. The challenge for smaller med spas is that their October surge is driven entirely by whoever they reach proactively. Patients who haven't been in since spring won't think to book unless something prompts them. No visible online booking on the website; contact form only.
**Best hook:** "October is when med spas that have their reactivation process running fill November and December. The ones that don't reactivate proactively watch their Q4 schedule fill much slower. You've got a database of past patients who already trust you — how are you reaching them right now ahead of the holiday season?"

## 5. Rockwall Eye Care Center
**Address:** 320 W Ralph Hall Pkwy, Rockwall, TX 75032
**What they do:** Optometry — exams, eyewear, contacts. Rockwall and Fate area.
**What I noticed:** Year-end vision benefits expiration is October 1 through December — right now is the window. Patients with FSA/HSA money to spend and vision benefits expiring are actively looking for a reason to book. Practice websites for optometry typically have scheduling built in, but the proactive recall process (reaching out to patients before they think to call) is usually missing for independent practices.
**Best hook:** "Vision benefits expire December 31 for most patients. The practices that proactively contact patients — 'you have benefits expiring, here's how to use them before year-end' — fill their schedule in October and November on autopilot. Are you doing that kind of outreach right now, or is it more organic from your end?"

## 6. Rowlett Family Chiropractic
**Address:** 4501 Dalrock Rd, Rowlett, TX 75089
**What they do:** Chiropractic care and sports injury treatment. Rowlett and Sachse area.
**What I noticed:** FSA/HSA year-end spend is active for chiropractic right now — patients with healthcare savings accounts are trying to use balances before December 31. Back-to-school sports injuries (fall football, soccer, cross-country) are also peaking. Rowlett is a large-population suburb with significant competition for local providers, making recall outreach more important than in smaller markets.
**Best hook:** "Two things are working in your favor right now: FSA accounts that patients need to spend before year-end, and fall sports injuries peaking across the area. The practices that reach lapsed patients in October — 'you've been in before, your FSA money expires soon' — see a real September-October spike. Are you reaching those patients or waiting for them to call?"

## 7. Crossroads Plumbing Services
**Address:** 3200 Rowlett Rd, Garland, TX 75043
**What they do:** Residential plumbing — repairs, water heaters, drain service. Rowlett, Garland, and Mesquite coverage.
**What I noticed:** October is the opening of pre-freeze plumbing season in North Texas. Water heater replacements, pipe insulation, and tankless installs pick up significantly before November. A plumbing company that proactively reaches its past customer list with a freeze-prep message now will fill the next 6–8 weeks. Most plumbing companies don't do this — they wait for the calls.
**Best hook:** "Plumbing has one of the best fall seasons in North Texas because of pre-freeze work, but most companies wait for the calls instead of reaching the customers who already trust them. If you sent a freeze-prep message to every customer you've done work for in the last 3 years — 'now is the time to check water heaters and pipe insulation before the first freeze' — how many of those do you think would book?"
