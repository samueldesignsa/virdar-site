**Topic:** The tour families who toured six weeks ago and nobody has called since.

**Post:**

---

A family tours an assisted living community in late July. They say "we need to think about it." The director says "absolutely, take your time."

Nobody calls them in August.

In week three of silence, the family books a second tour at a different community. That community follows up the next day. And again five days later. By the time the first community calls to check in — at the five-week mark — the family has already signed paperwork somewhere else.

This happens constantly in senior living. Not because the care is worse. Because the follow-up wasn't there.

Assisted living is a 14-to-21-day decision window from tour to signature. The communities winning the move-in are not waiting for families to come back. They're staying present — a personal call on day one, a relevant note on day four, a check-in on day nine — without making the family feel pressured.

That sequence doesn't require hiring another admissions coordinator. It requires a system that runs it automatically and flags when a family goes quiet.

The families are there. They toured. They just need someone to stay in the conversation.
