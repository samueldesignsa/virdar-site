# Walk-In Research — Thursday, July 23, 2026
## Territory: Prosper / Celina (US-380 Corridor / Preston Rd North), TX

**Why Prosper / Celina today:**
Prosper crossed 55,000 residents in 2025 and is still adding 400 to 600 households per month. Celina, immediately to the north on Preston Road, was 6,000 people in 2015 and is now approaching 40,000. These are the two fastest-developing cities in Collin County after Frisco — and unlike Frisco, they have commercial corridors that are less than 5 years old. Independent businesses that opened here in 2021, 2022, and 2023 built their patient or customer bases from zero in a new market and have never had systems — because growth was fast enough that the lack of systems didn't register as a problem yet.

**Prosper ISD starts August 12 — 20 days.** Celina ISD starts August 12 as well. These are both earlier-than-average starts for North Texas. Any medical practice, therapy practice, or children's service provider in this corridor has the same urgency window as Frisco — and an even smaller commercial ecosystem of people who've been walking in with the same pitch. Beverly is likely the first person to name the August 12 date in most of these conversations.

**Two-a-days start July 28 — 5 days.** Prosper ISD has two high schools (Prosper High, Rock Hill High) with large UIL programs. Celina ISD has Celina High School with a storied football program (multiple state titles). The athletic culture is intense. A physical therapy or sports medicine practice in this corridor has a higher proportion of competitive athletes in its patient base than practices farther south.

**DPC / concierge medicine angle introduced today.** Today's blog post covers direct primary care automation — the membership retention, utilization, and referral problems specific to that model. The Prosper/Celina demographic is precisely the DPC market: dual-income households earning $200K+, highly educated, frustrated with insurance-based primary care, willing to pay $100–$150/month for same-day access. If Beverly encounters a DPC or concierge physician today, this is the sharpest conversation she'll have this month.

## One Observation

Prosper and Celina are Frisco from five years ago — fast growth, few inherited systems, owners who built their businesses entirely through word-of-mouth and hustle. The difference today is that they're far enough along that the growth gap is starting to close. The Frisco practices Beverly has been walking into this week built their books during the boom and are only now facing a plateauing environment. The Prosper and Celina practices are still in the boom — and that means the owners are the most receptive to the concept that they should be building systems now, while the schedule is full, rather than scrambling to build them when it isn't.

The DPC angle is specific to this territory in a way it hasn't been to most other DFW stops. Prosper is the right income and demographic profile for DPC to penetrate — and the practices that are running a membership model here are likely 2 to 4 years in and hitting their first major retention challenge.
