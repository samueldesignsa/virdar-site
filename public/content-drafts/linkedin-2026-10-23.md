# LinkedIn Post — Friday, October 23, 2026

**Topic:** The cost of not having an outreach system during the holiday close — revenue that disappears while the owner is busy
**Target length:** ~160 words
**Post time:** 8–9am CT

---

The holiday close is the busiest six weeks of the year for most service businesses.

It's also when the follow-up stops.

October, November, December — the owner is managing installations, handling patient volume, running the floor. Nobody is working the lapsed client list. Nobody is following up the quote from three weeks ago. Nobody is sending the year-end benefit message.

And the revenue disappears. Quietly.

Here's the math: a dental practice with 300 patients who haven't been in this year has roughly $90,000 in unscheduled production — $300 per patient, all expiring December 31. The practice that reaches those patients in October fills November from its own list. The one that doesn't finds out in January that another practice sent the same message in October.

The outreach that builds Q4 revenue doesn't happen in Q4. It happens now — before the owner gets too busy to send it.

Busy is not a strategy. It's just a reason the revenue went somewhere else.
