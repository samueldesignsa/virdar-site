## One Insight

Celina is worth a longer look beyond today's route. The city is in an unusual growth phase: residential population is doubling approximately every four years, but the commercial infrastructure is only 3–5 years old. That means you'll find businesses that have all the hallmarks of a hot walk-in target — fast-growing revenue, owner still making all the decisions, not yet locked into enterprise software or long-term agency contracts — and almost none of the vendor saturation that makes Plano and Frisco harder to walk into. The chiropractic, dental, and HVAC categories in particular are seeing organic demand growth that their systems aren't keeping up with. An owner in Celina who hears "your patient recall process isn't keeping up with your new patient volume" will feel that immediately because it's true and they know it. The conversation isn't about convincing them the problem exists — it's about showing them what fixing it looks like.
