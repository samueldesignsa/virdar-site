## One Insight

Addison is underworked because it has a reputation for being saturated — it's a high-visibility commercial corridor and a lot of vendors assume someone has already been there. In practice, the independent medical, dental, and specialty retail businesses in Addison respond the same way businesses everywhere do: nobody has talked to them specifically about their year-end outreach, and they're running November the same way they ran September. The density of the corridor works in your favor for a walk-in day — 5–7 stops within 2 miles, no dead zones, and the businesses there tend to have higher average transaction values than typical strip-center businesses in the outer suburbs. Worth doing a full corridor sweep rather than cherry-picking.
