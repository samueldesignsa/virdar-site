### 5. Med Spa or Day Spa — Colleyville or Southlake

**Hook to use:** The maintenance interval timing. Botox is a 3-month treatment, filler is 6–12 months, most laser protocols have a defined re-treatment window. "Your records know when each client's next treatment window opens. The only thing that happens automatically right now is that the window opens and nobody tells them."
