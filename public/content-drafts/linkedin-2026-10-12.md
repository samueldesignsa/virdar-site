**Topic:** The Q4 exterior work window is closing — contractors who reactivate their estimate backlog now win the season
**Target length:** ~170 words

Mid-October in North Texas is the last reliable window for exterior work.

Roofing, painting, concrete, fencing, foundation repair — all of it gets harder to schedule as November approaches. Weather gets unpredictable. Homeowners mentally check out for the holidays. Contractor schedules fill with whatever jobs are already booked.

The contractors who fill Q4 aren't generating a flood of new leads right now. They're working their summer estimate backlog.

Every estimate you sent between May and August that never converted is sitting in an inbox. Some of those homeowners are still deciding. Some got busy and forgot. Some are ready to book but never got a follow-up. The ones who are ready will go with whoever reaches them first — not because they prefer that company, but because that company showed up at the right moment.

A 3-sentence follow-up on a $9,000 estimate costs nothing. Letting it sit another 60 days costs the job.

How many estimates from this summer are still open on your end?
