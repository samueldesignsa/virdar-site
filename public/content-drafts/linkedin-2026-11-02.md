# LinkedIn Post — Monday, November 2, 2026

**Topic:** FSA and HSA balances expire December 31. 59 days left. Medical and dental practices that haven't reached out to patients with unused benefits are leaving their own revenue on the table.
**Target length:** ~180 words
**Post time:** 8–9am CT

---

December 31 is 59 days away.

If you run a dental practice, optometry office, or chiropractic clinic, your patients with FSA or HSA accounts have money earmarked specifically for you. They have to spend it by December 31 or it's gone.

Most of them haven't thought about it since they set it up during open enrollment.

The practices that fill their November and December schedules don't do it by running ads. They do it by sending one specific message to patients who haven't been in since the spring: "You likely still have dental benefits — here's what that covers, and here's how to use it before it expires."

That message converts because it's genuinely useful. It's not a promotion. It's a reminder about money that belongs to the patient, that only your practice can help them spend.

A dental office that sends this message to 300 overdue patients typically fills 15–25 appointments in two weeks. Without it, those same patients spend January blaming themselves for not getting in.

The window is now. After November 15, you're fighting holiday schedules.
