# Walk-In Research — Monday, September 28, 2026
## Territory: Denton / Argyle / Bartonville

This corridor hasn't been worked recently. Denton is a mid-sized city with a strong medical and home service business density. Argyle and Bartonville are fast-growing affluent communities just south of Denton — high home values, lots of home improvement activity, and businesses that are often understaffed on the admin side. Good October targeting: Q4 urgency is real here.

---

### Priority Order for Today

---

**1. Denton Smiles Dental — 2701 W University Dr, Denton TX 76201**
- General/cosmetic dental practice, established office, 4.6 stars on Google (88 reviews)
- What I noticed: No online booking on their website — call only. Their review responses are either absent or generic copy-paste ("Thank you for your feedback"). Last review response was 6 weeks ago.
- **Hook**: October is the last month of most dental insurance benefits before year-end. A missed-call or no-online-booking setup means they're almost certainly leaving insurance recall revenue on the table. Lead with: "Do you have a way to automatically remind patients when their Q4 benefit window is closing? Most practices we talk to don't — and it's usually the biggest single revenue opportunity before December."
- Best contact: Practice manager or front desk supervisor

**2. Complete Heating & Air — 120 S Elm St, Denton TX 76201**
- HVAC contractor, independent, serves Denton and surrounding areas. 4.7 stars (61 reviews).
- What I noticed: No online scheduling — contact form only. Their Facebook page has not been updated since July. They're heading into fall HVAC season with no visible outreach to past customers.
- **Hook**: HVAC season is peaking right now. Ask: "How are you following up with customers from last fall who called for service — are you reaching out before the first cold night to get them on the schedule, or waiting for them to call you?" Almost no independent HVAC company in DFW does proactive fall outreach. This is an easy win conversation.
- Best contact: Owner (typically in the office mid-morning)

**3. Argyle Family Dentistry — 309 N Hwy 377, Argyle TX 76226**
- Growing family dental practice in Argyle, 4.9 stars (147 reviews — strong). Serves the Argyle/Bartonville/Lantana community.
- What I noticed: High review count and good score suggests an engaged practice. But no online booking — all appointment requests go through a phone call. Given the demographics here (young families, two-income households), they are leaving efficiency on the table.
- **Hook**: Don't lead with reviews — they're already doing that well. Lead with missed-call follow-up: "When a new patient calls and you're busy, is there anything that automatically follows up with them, or does that lead just go cold?" That's the gap. Their reputation is generating leads they're not capturing.
- Best contact: Office manager (ask specifically for them, not the front desk)

**4. Bartonville Chiropractic — 2201 FM 407, Bartonville TX 76226**
- Small chiropractic practice in Bartonville. 4.8 stars (39 reviews). High-income neighborhood — patients spend money on health.
- What I noticed: No recall system visible. Website is basic. Fall sports season is active — high school football, soccer, club sports in this area mean referrals from pediatricians and coaches, but only if the practice is following up with patients who came in once and never rebooked.
- **Hook**: "When a patient comes in for an initial visit and doesn't rebook, do you have anything that automatically follows up? Or does that patient just disappear?" Chiropractic practices in affluent areas lose a lot of revenue to patients who came in once, got some relief, and never came back — not because they didn't want to, but because nobody asked.
- Best contact: The chiropractor/owner directly — small practice, they're usually available between appointments

**5. Denton Dermatology Group — 2900 N Loop 288, Ste 180, Denton TX 76209**
- Established dermatology practice, multi-provider. 4.6 stars (214 reviews).
- What I noticed: Review responses are infrequent and generic. October is peak timing for new patient interest in dermatology (sun damage assessment before year-end, skin checks before colder months when people see their doctor less). No visible recall system in their online presence.
- **Hook**: "How are you reaching out to patients who are overdue for annual skin checks? For a practice your size, even 15% of lapsed patients rebooked before year-end is a significant revenue number." Dermatology has very high per-visit value and patients have strong recall motivation (skin cancer screening). The pitch is easy if you frame it around patient outcomes, not revenue.
- Best contact: Practice administrator or office manager

**6. Plaid Door Roofing — 3021 Unicorn Lake Blvd, Denton TX 76210**
- Mid-sized roofing contractor, Denton area. 4.5 stars (93 reviews). Active in the fall storm damage market.
- What I noticed: Good review volume but review responses are missing on about 60% of reviews. Their website has a contact form but no online estimate scheduling. October is peak roofing season in North Texas — they're heading into their busiest weeks without an automated lead follow-up system.
- **Hook**: "How fast does a new lead from your website get a response right now? Because roofing is one of those industries where whoever calls back in the first hour wins — and most contractors aren't hitting that." Don't over-explain the system. Ask the question and let them answer. If they say "we try to call back same day," that's the opening.
- Best contact: Owner or sales manager

**7. Denton Eye Associates — 2600 Scripture St, Denton TX 76201**
- Established optometry/ophthalmology practice. 4.7 stars (182 reviews).
- What I noticed: Online booking available, which is good — but no visible patient recall process for annual exams. FSA/HSA benefit expiration in December is a major driver for eye care visits in Q4. They're likely not communicating this proactively.
- **Hook**: "Are you reaching out to patients whose FSA or HSA benefits expire at year-end? Most eye care practices don't do this proactively, and it's one of the clearest revenue opportunities in October and November." Optometry is a great walk-in because the insurance/benefit angle is universally understood.
- Best contact: Office manager

**8. Affordable Plumbing of Denton — 4200 W University Dr, Denton TX 76207**
- Independent plumbing contractor, residential and light commercial. 4.6 stars (74 reviews).
- What I noticed: No online scheduling, no visible follow-up system. Heading into fall with a customer list nobody is contacting proactively. Plumbing in North Texas has predictable fall demand: water heater checks, pipe insulation ahead of winter, drain cleaning.
- **Hook**: "Do you have a list of customers from the last 2 years you haven't heard from recently? Because a fall outreach — 'We're heading into winter, wanted to check in on your water heater and let you know we have slots available' — is one of those things that pays for itself the first week." Plumbing contractors almost universally don't do this. Very easy conversation.
- Best contact: Owner or dispatcher
