A photographer I know opens fall mini-session registration every August.

Last year she posted her announcement at 7pm. By 9pm she had 40+ DMs. She answered them the next morning. Two spots remained open on October weekends she could have filled.

This year she set up an automated response: anyone who messages gets an instant reply with the pricing, the available dates, and a booking link. She posted Thursday night and woke up Friday with 14 confirmed sessions — while she was asleep.

Same demand. Same reputation. Different result because the response was immediate.

This applies to most service businesses. The inquiry doesn't arrive at a convenient time. The dentist is with a patient. The attorney is in a deposition. The contractor is on a job site. The window where a prospect decides to commit is short — usually a few hours, sometimes less.

The businesses that fill their calendars aren't always the best at what they do. They're the ones that respond first while the prospect is still deciding.

If your busiest season is September and October, the time to fix your inquiry response is now. Not after summer ends.
