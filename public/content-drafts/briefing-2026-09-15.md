## One Insight

**East Dallas County is the closest thing to an untapped market that still exists in DFW.**

The walk-in rotation has been thorough: north Dallas suburbs (Plano, Frisco, McKinney, Prosper), mid-cities (Hurst, Euless, Bedford, Keller, NRH), northeast (Rockwall, Rowlett), and south (Cedar Hill, DeSoto, Duncanville). What's missing is the east — Garland, Mesquite, Balch Springs, and ultimately Forney and Terrell further out.

Garland is genuinely one of the largest cities in Texas and it barely registers in the conversation about where businesses get vendor outreach. The owner-operated service businesses there — HVAC companies that have been in business since 1998, auto shops that have served the same families for two decades, family dental practices on Garland Road — are running on relationship, reputation, and word of mouth. None of them have a formal customer reactivation system. None of them have been pitched on AI by someone who understood their business well enough to be credible.

That's the market. The only competitive advantage the bigger vendors have is brand recognition — and brand recognition means nothing to a business owner in Garland who's never heard of any of them. Walk in with a specific, relevant hook and you have the conversation entirely to yourself.
