# Walk-In Research: Southlake / Trophy Club / Westlake
**Thursday, November 19, 2026 — Morning Shift**

Affluent west corridor of DFW. Southlake Town Square pulls high foot traffic with a dense mix of independent service businesses. Trophy Club and Westlake are smaller but packed with the right demographic — dual-income households, strong disposable income, frequent buyers of boutique services. This area has money and habit; it doesn't have automation.

---

## Target Businesses

### 1. The Wine Loft – Southlake Town Square
**Category:** Independent wine and spirits shop  
**Address:** Southlake Town Square area, Southlake, TX  
**What they do:** Boutique wine shop with rotating selection, wine club, and in-store tastings. Upscale clientele, host-gift buying is a core purchase occasion.  
**What you noticed:** No promotional emails visible in their public presence. Wine club sign-up exists on website but no visible engagement cadence. Website has no online booking for tastings.  
**Hook:** "Thanksgiving is 6 days away. You have a customer list sitting there. We can get a host-gift campaign out before Wednesday with no work on your end."

## Field Notes for Beverly

**Timing is everything this week.** Every business in this corridor is 6 days from Thanksgiving and 6 weeks from Christmas. The pitch isn't "AI automation" in the abstract — it's "you have a window, it closes December 31, here's the specific thing we can get running before it does."

**The Southlake demographic is the right target.** These owners are busy, successful, and analytical. They respond to specifics: dollar amounts, timeframes, concrete outcomes. Don't pitch features. Pitch the revenue number they're currently leaving on the table.

**Best walk-in approach today:** Start at Southlake Town Square. High density of independent businesses within walking distance. The wine shop angle is especially timely given today's blog post — if the owner asks what Virdar does, you can literally hand them the URL and say "I wrote this this morning."

**Objection to expect:** "I don't have time right now — holiday season." Your answer: "That's exactly why I'm here now. We set it up. You don't touch it. The campaigns run while you're focused on the floor."
