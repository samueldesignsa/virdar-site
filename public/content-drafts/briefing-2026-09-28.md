## One Insight

The LinkedIn post for today makes a specific point worth reinforcing in walk-in conversations: most small businesses are spending money on new advertising while sitting on an unworked list of past leads. The ROI argument is stronger when you frame it that way — "before we talk about anything new, let's look at what you're already not using." That reframe tends to land better than leading with AI or automation. It positions the problem as a waste problem, not a technology problem.
