Thanksgiving is 8 days away.

The dog boarding facility in your neighborhood that's been full since October didn't earn that by being a better kennel. They sent one email to their client list in mid-October. "Holiday spots filling fast — here's how to reserve yours."

"Loyal customer" is not a system. It's a feeling you have about a person who hasn't heard from you in four months.

If you have a list of people who've bought from you and been happy — and haven't heard from you lately — you have a campaign that pays for itself in a week.

The window is closing.
