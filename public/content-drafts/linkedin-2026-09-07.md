Most business owners I talk to have a customer list they haven't touched in six months.

Not because they forgot about it. Because there's always something more urgent — the next job, the next call, the next invoice.

September is the month that divides the businesses with a full Q4 from the ones wondering where October went.

The ones with full October schedules right now did one thing: they sent a message to the people who already know them. Not a campaign. Not a promotion. A message that said: "It's been a while — we have openings before the holidays if you want to get back on the schedule."

That's it. No discount required. No ad spend.

The customers who came in last fall and said "definitely again next year" — they still mean it. They just need someone to ask.

September is the ask. October is the answer.

If you have a contact list and it's been quiet since spring, this week is when you send the message.
