## Target Businesses

### 1. Garland Nail Salon (Belt Line Road / Northwest Highway area, Garland)
**What they do:** Independent nail salon — gel manicures, pedicures, acrylics. Likely owner-operated with 3–5 techs.
**What I noticed:** Independent nail salons in this corridor typically have minimal web presence beyond a Google Business listing. Booking is phone-only or walk-in. No pre-holiday outreach visible — no email, no text reminder. Google reviews are often sparse (20–50), limiting new client discovery.
**Revenue problem:** Today is July 3. If the salon is quieter than expected this week, it's because no message went out to regulars two weeks ago. The ones who are fully booked today sent that message on June 19th. The ones who didn't are finding out right now what that costs.
**Hook to use:** "How's your calendar looking today compared to what you expected? [Let them answer.] The salons I work with that are fully booked right now sent a message to their regulars 10 days ago — not today. Your clients want to come in before the holiday. They just didn't know you had open slots. What does your outreach to regulars look like before a holiday weekend?"
**Leave-behind:** Show virdar.co/blog/ai-automation-for-nail-salons.html — published this morning, July 3. First dedicated nail salon post in the Virdar library. This is the strongest leave-behind Beverly can use with a nail salon owner.

### 2. Garland Barbershop (Garland Road / First Street area, Garland)
**What they do:** Independent barbershop — haircuts, fades, lineups. Owner-operated or 2–3 barbers.
**What I noticed:** Barbershops in this corridor typically have Instagram activity but no automated booking or outreach. Clients walk in or call. Busy before holidays but often inconsistent mid-week. Review count is usually under 80 on Google.
**Revenue problem:** July 4 eve is the biggest single day of the year for barbershops. Clients want a fresh cut before parties and gatherings. The barbers who had a full book today reached their regulars. The ones who didn't are relying on walk-in traffic that's unpredictable.
**Hook to use:** "How's today looking — did you fill up the way you expected for the holiday? [Let them answer.] The shops I work with booked most of today by Thursday last week. Not a gimmick — just a message that went out to their regulars: 'We're running a full book before the holiday — grab a slot before we fill up.' Takes 5 minutes to set up. Runs automatically before every holiday after that."

### 3. Aladdin Auto Repair / Garland Auto Service (Garland Road area, Garland)
**What they do:** Independent auto repair — oil changes, brakes, tires, diagnostics.
**What I noticed:** Independent shops in this area have basic Google listings with limited review management. Several have declined service note issues — no visible follow-up system. July 4 road trips are happening today and this weekend.
**Revenue problem:** Customers who declined brake or tire work in the past 90 days are driving those cars right now for the holiday weekend. No follow-up system means that revenue stays declined. A shop that reached those customers this week captured $3,000–$5,000 in already-identified work.
**Hook to use:** "It's July 4 weekend. You have customers who said 'not right now' to brakes or tires in April and May. They're driving those same cars on the highway today. The shops that have a declined-service follow-up system sent those customers a message this week. What does your follow-up look like on declined recommendations?"

### 4. Richardson Family Dental (Campbell Road / Greenville Avenue area, Richardson)
**What they do:** General dentistry, family practice — cleanings, pediatric, ortho referrals.
**What I noticed:** Richardson has several independent dental practices that are well-established but running on paper/phone recall. RISD (Richardson ISD) starts August 17. The pediatric recall window for back-to-school cleanings is open right now.
**Revenue problem:** RISD starts August 17 — six weeks out. Parents who want their kids seen before school starts are starting to schedule now. A practice that sends a recall message today reaches those families in July. A practice that waits reaches them when August is already in crisis mode and morning slots are gone.
**Hook to use:** "RISD starts August 17. How many pediatric patients do you have who are overdue for a cleaning right now? [Let them answer.] The practices that fill August mornings with back-to-school checkups sent the recall message in early July, not August. If you're still on phone recall, you're competing for slots that are filling up right now."

### 5. Richardson Chiropractic (Campbell Road / Arapaho Road area, Richardson)
**What they do:** Independent chiropractic practice — adjustments, injury rehab, wellness care.
**What I noticed:** Richardson chiro practices have strong community reputations but tend to underwork their patient database. Mid-year unused insurance benefit is the single most actionable hook in July for any chiropractic practice.
**Revenue problem:** PPO chiropractic benefits reset December 31. A patient who had active care in Q1 and stopped in March has $500–$800 in unused benefit sitting there. The practice isn't telling them. Another practice might.
**Hook to use:** "Do you know how many of your Q1 patients still have unused chiropractic benefit through December? Most practices I work with have 40–60% of their active-care patients from the first half of the year sitting on unused coverage. That's not a marketing pitch — that's a patient service. Are you reaching those patients in July?"

### 6. East Garland Fitness / Garland CrossFit (Miller Road / Belt Line area, Garland)
**What they do:** Independent fitness studio — boutique classes, CrossFit, or personal training.
**What I noticed:** Fitness studios in this corridor typically have attendance tracking in their software but no automated re-engagement when a member goes quiet. July 4 week is the single highest-drift week of the year for recurring attendance businesses.
**Revenue problem:** Members who miss class July 4 week miss two or three times in a row. Without a check-in message at the two-week mark, those members quietly stop showing up. They haven't cancelled — they just broke the habit. Studios that don't catch them by week 2 are dealing with a cancellation call in week 8.
**Hook to use:** "What does your attendance look like this week compared to normal? [Let them answer.] The studios I work with have an automated check-in that goes out when a member hasn't been in for two weeks. It brings back about half the members who would have otherwise drifted to a cancellation. It's not a marketing message — it's a 'hey, everything okay?' at the exact moment it matters."

### 7. Richardson Optometry (Greenville Avenue / Belt Line area, Richardson)
**What they do:** Independent optometry — eye exams, glasses, contact lenses, pediatric vision.
**What I noticed:** Richardson ISD starts August 17. Pediatric vision recall for back-to-school exams is the single most time-sensitive revenue action for an optometry practice right now. Online booking exists at larger chains; independent practices often rely on phone.
**Revenue problem:** Back-to-school pediatric vision exams fill in July and August. The practices that fill August slots sent the recall message now. The ones that wait until August call overdue families who've already been seen elsewhere or decided to wait another year.
**Hook to use:** "RISD starts August 17. How many of your pediatric patients are overdue for an annual exam right now? If you haven't sent a recall message in the past three weeks, those families are scheduling with whoever reached them first. What does your recall process look like for overdue patients?"

### 8. Independent Pet Boarding / Garland Veterinary (Garland / Rowlett Road area, east Garland)
**What they do:** Pet boarding, veterinary clinic, or doggy daycare — either standalone boarding or vet-run boarding.
**What I noticed:** July 4 is the single biggest boarding weekend of the year. Pet owners traveling for the holiday need to board their dogs today or have already booked. Facilities that sent a message to past boarding clients two weeks ago are full. The ones that didn't have last-minute walk-ins or open kennels.
**Revenue problem:** July 4 boarding is booked by mid-June by the owners who plan ahead. The revenue left on the table is the last-minute panic bookings from clients who meant to call and forgot. A two-week-out message to past boarding clients captures the planner AND the procrastinator.
**Hook to use:** "How's your July 4 weekend looking — fully booked, or still space? [Let them answer.] The clinics that are fully booked reached their past boarding clients two weeks ago with a simple heads-up: 'July 4 is coming, we fill early — want to secure your pet's spot?' Did you send anything to your past boarding clients this year before the holiday?"
