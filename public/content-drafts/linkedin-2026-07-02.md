**Topic:** July 4 week as a planning test — and what it reveals about who has a system

---

Two types of business owners going into July 4 weekend.

The first type knows what next week looks like. Their July schedule is intact. They sent reminders last week, filled the gap slots before the holiday hit, and have a waitlist message ready if a cancellation comes in Thursday. They're not stressed about the four-day stretch.

The second type is managing it in real time. They have a few open slots, a couple of last-minute cancellations they found out about this morning, and a "let's see what happens" plan for next week.

The difference isn't effort. Both of them are working. The difference is whether they have a system that works when they're not looking at it — over the weekend, after 6pm, on the Friday before a holiday.

July 4 week is a small test. The bigger test is August 18, when Texas schools start and the families that meant to enroll for fall already picked someone else.

If July 4 week is stressful to manage, August will be too.
