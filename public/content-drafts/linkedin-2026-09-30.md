A homeowner in Southlake requested generator quotes from three companies on the same night.

First company called back at 9am the next morning.
Second company called two days later.
Third company sent an email a week after that.

She booked the first one.

Same thing happens in HVAC. Dental. Roofing. Plumbing. Any business where a customer makes a decision after a specific trigger — a cold snap, a broken appliance, an insurance deadline — and the window of intent is short.

The companies that win aren't always the best reviewed. They're not always the most experienced. They're the ones who showed up in the first 10 minutes.

If your business depends on inbound leads, run this test: submit a contact form on your own website right now, from a phone number you don't answer at the office.

See what happens. When does someone respond? What do they say?

Most business owners are surprised by what they find. And that gap — between when a customer is ready and when they finally hear back — is where most of your leads are going.
