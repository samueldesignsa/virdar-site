## One Insight

The Arlington / Grand Prairie territory is a different demographic than the Plano / Frisco / Southlake stops, and the pitch tone needs to shift accordingly. Grand Prairie business owners are practical and direct — they've heard plenty of pitches and they filter quickly. The hook that cuts through is specific and dollar-anchored, not relationship-oriented: "your patients have $1,200 in benefits expiring December 31" lands better than "October is a good time to think about recall." In Mansfield, the demographic skews higher-income and the pitch should be more results-oriented: "before Thanksgiving," "before the holiday rush," "your patients are in the buying window right now." Same urgency, different framing. The Grand Prairie owner is responding to a financial deadline. The Mansfield owner is responding to timing and positioning. Watch for that split as you move south through the route.
