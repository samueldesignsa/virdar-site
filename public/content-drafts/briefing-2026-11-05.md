## One Insight

Independent pharmacies are an underworked vertical for AI automation sales — not because they're unresponsive to the pitch, but because automation vendors don't walk into pharmacies. The mental model for most vendors is appointment-based businesses: dental, medical, spa. Pharmacies don't fit that frame, so they never hear the pitch.

But the revenue problem is identical: they have a database of patients who are drifting away because nobody is proactively communicating with them, and a seasonal window (flu season, FSA deadline) where outreach converts at unusually high rates. The case study numbers in today's blog post are specific and compelling. If Beverly walks past a Lewisville Pharmacy or any independent pharmacy in the corridor today and the owner is visible, it's worth a 90-second conversation.
