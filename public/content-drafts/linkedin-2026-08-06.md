# LinkedIn Post — Wednesday, August 6, 2026

---

Most dentists know which families are overdue for a cleaning.

They have the list. It's in the practice management software, sitting there — patients who were supposed to come in six months ago and never scheduled.

The ones with kids are thinking about it right now.

School starts next week across North Texas. The parent who has been putting off the pediatric checkup since spring just added it to their mental to-do list alongside school supplies and new shoes. They're going to schedule an appointment somewhere this week.

The dentist who sends a recall message today captures those families. The one who waits until after school starts is competing for appointment slots with parents who are suddenly less flexible and more stressed.

Back-to-school is the most predictable reactivation window in pediatric dentistry. Better than January. Better than spring break. Because parents are in scheduling mode right now — they're building the fall calendar — and a message that says "we have openings this week before school starts" lands at exactly the right moment.

The window is this week. Next Tuesday it's gone.
