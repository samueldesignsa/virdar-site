## Territory: Duncanville / Cedar Hill / DeSoto (South Dallas Suburbs)

**Why this territory today:** The last six weeks have covered north, northeast, east, and west DFW heavily — Denton County, Tarrant County's HEB corridor, Arlington/Mansfield, Irving, Plano, Rowlett/Rockwall. The south Dallas suburbs — Duncanville, Cedar Hill, DeSoto — haven't been hit in this cycle. These are established mid-size suburbs with independent business density that's strong for Beverly's model:

- **Duncanville** is a dense, established suburb (pop. ~40,000) with a commercial corridor along Camp Wisdom and Hwy 67 that's been in place for 20+ years. Independent medical, dental, and service businesses here have large, underworked patient and client lists. They're not growth-story businesses — they're steady-state operations that have been losing patients slowly without noticing.
- **Cedar Hill** sits adjacent to Joe Pool Lake, has a mix of upper-income residential and small independent professional businesses. Medical practices here serve a higher-LTV client base than the address would suggest — families who bought on the lake 10–15 years ago are well-established, have insurance, and expect proactive service.
- **DeSoto** (pop. ~55,000) has one of the more complete independent business ecosystems in the south suburbs — dental, chiropractic, optometry, veterinary, and fitness are all represented along Beltline Road and Pleasant Run Road. Many of these practices opened in the 2010–2015 window and are now running on systems that were designed for half their current patient load.

**Primary angle for today:** August mid-month is a "quiet before the storm" moment for south Dallas businesses. School just started, the summer's informal pace is ending, and the fall Q4 demand curve is about to hit. Beverly can walk in with a specific observation: "Most practices in this area are going to get a natural September-October pickup just from the school calendar restarting. The ones that prepared for it — sent messages to their patient lists, set up their recall campaigns — will have a much bigger fall than the ones waiting for the phone to ring."

**Secondary angle:** Moving season. August 13 is prime moving season in DFW — lots of families who closed on homes in July are settling into the south suburbs right now. Any new businesses Beverly spots in Cedar Hill, DeSoto, or Duncanville are worth noting — recently opened independents are often running entirely on word of mouth with no follow-up systems in place.
