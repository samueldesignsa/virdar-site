# Walk-In Research — Wednesday, November 25, 2026
**Area: Bishop Arts District / Oak Cliff / Davis Street corridor**

Today is Thanksgiving Eve. Many business owners will be open this morning but mentally in the holiday. That's an advantage — they're reflective, thinking about the year, thinking about what December and January look like. The usual "I'm too busy to talk" objection doesn't apply the same way today.

**Best timing: 9:30am–12:30pm**, before owners start closing early or handling last-minute prep. Avoid after 1pm — many will start winding down.

**Primary hooks today:**
- Holiday gift card window (Hanukkah starts Monday, Christmas is 4 weeks away)
- Thanksgiving Eve reflection: "What does your December calendar look like right now?"
- January bookings start NOW — the businesses that fill January do it in December

## Notes for Today

**Thanksgiving Eve framing:** Don't lead with the pitch. Lead with the moment. "Big week for you?" or "How's the holiday been treating the business?" — let them tell you what's on their mind. Most will say something that opens the door directly to what you're offering.

**The gift card angle is the fastest open today.** Every retail and hospitality business in this corridor is thinking about the holiday buying window. "When did you last tell your customers you sell gift cards?" lands in under 10 seconds.

**Leave by 12:30pm.** Bishop Arts business owners will start winding down for Thanksgiving after lunch. You'll get better conversations in the morning window.
