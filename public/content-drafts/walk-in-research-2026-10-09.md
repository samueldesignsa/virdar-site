# Walk-In Research — Friday, October 9, 2026
## Territory: Irving / Las Colinas / Coppell

Irving and Coppell sit in the DFW Metroplex's mid-corridor — between Dallas and Fort Worth, directly adjacent to DFW Airport. Las Colinas (the commercial district inside Irving's city limits) is one of the densest corporate office concentrations in North Texas: financial services companies, insurance offices, regional headquarters, and the medical complex near Las Colinas Medical Center. Coppell is a smaller, higher-income residential city with a well-established commercial spine along Sandy Lake Road and Denton Tap Road — independent practices, boutique service businesses, owner-operated shops.

This territory is meaningfully different from the suburban residential markets like Keller, Frisco, or McKinney. In Irving and Las Colinas, the patients and clients are mostly corporate employees — people with employer-sponsored insurance plans, FSA and HSA accounts set up through HR, and a habit of using professional services near their office rather than near their home. The FSA/HSA year-end hook lands especially well here because the benefits were set up by HR, the deadlines are standard, and the employees are generally more aware of them than residential patients in smaller markets.

In Coppell, the dynamic is closer to the suburban residential market — dual-income professional families, high homeownership, owner-operated practices that serve local residents. Sandy Lake Rd and Denton Tap Rd are the main commercial corridors.

**Friday note:** Businesses wind down earlier on Fridays. Aim to be in the first stop by 8:30am. Target 4–5 conversations rather than a full 7-stop route. Finish by noon.

Key October hooks: year-end FSA/HSA/dental/vision benefits, fall HVAC inquiry surge, Q4 contract season for commercial services, fall aesthetic booking season.

## 1. Irving Family Dental

**What I noticed:** Irving has a large working-age population with employer-sponsored Delta Dental, Cigna, and Aetna plans. **Best hook:** "October is when dental recall outreach actually works — your patients have employer plan benefits expiring December 31 and they're motivated to schedule before they lose them. Are you reaching the patients who haven't been in since spring, or waiting for them to call in on their own?"
