**Target length:** ~175 words

Thanksgiving is in 6 days.

Right now, most service businesses in DFW are as busy as they'll be all year. Full schedules, happy clients, holiday energy. It feels like the business is working.

The problem comes in January.

January is slow for most of these same businesses — not because demand disappears, but because they didn't capture anything from November. No rebookings scheduled. No follow-ups set. No campaign queued for when holiday clients go dark until February.

The businesses that have a full calendar on January 2 did one specific thing differently: they used November's busy stretch to book December and January at the same time. Not by working harder. By having a system that sent a rebook prompt, a "here's what's next" message, or a January-specific offer to every satisfied client before the holiday fog rolled in.

If November is full and you haven't done that yet, the window is now — not after Thanksgiving.

What are you doing this week to make January easier?
