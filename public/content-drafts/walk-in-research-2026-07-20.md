## One Observation

Richardson independent business owners tend to be more analytical than the owners Beverly has met in newer suburbs. Many have been in business long enough to have tried multiple systems that didn't stick — CRM software they paid for and didn't use, email platforms they set up but never learned. The pitch that lands here is not "here's a new system" — it's "here's a specific thing that's costing you money right now, and here's the exact mechanism that closes the gap." Lead with the dry cleaner's status call count, the dentist's unprompted August recall, the HVAC owner's invoice list from 2023. Concrete and immediate beats aspirational and general in an established business corridor.
