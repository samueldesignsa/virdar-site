Your customers are making holiday decisions this week.

Not December decisions. Right now. This week.

Which cleaning service gets called before Christmas dinner. Which dentist appointment to squeeze in before the year resets. Whether they'll stay loyal to their current gym through December or quietly stop showing up. Whether to buy a gift card for the person who's impossible to shop for.

The businesses that show up in their inbox this week are the ones that stay on the list. The businesses that wait until December are competing for the same attention as everyone else's holiday campaigns, Black Friday follow-ups, and December urgency emails.

You have a list of people who've already spent money with you. Some of them haven't been back since summer. They're not gone — they're just not thinking about you because nobody reminded them you exist.

This week is the last clean window before holiday noise takes over. Send the message.
