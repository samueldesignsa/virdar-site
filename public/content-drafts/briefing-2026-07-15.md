## One Insight

Denton is the only DFW suburb Beverly has walked that has a university at the center of the economy. Every other territory — Keller, Southlake, Frisco, Mansfield — is a bedroom suburb that serves commuters and their families. Denton serves those same demographics but also has 65,000 students, hundreds of graduate programs, and a year-round academic calendar that shapes when local businesses are busy and slow.

The seasonal pattern for Denton businesses is different: the revenue dip isn't the holiday slowdown or summer slow season (both of which affect suburban practices) — it's the May–August period when UNT and TWU are in reduced-enrollment summer sessions. The small businesses around the Square and along University Ave fill August with the returning student wave. That's a different conversation than the back-to-school conversation Beverly is having in Keller and Frisco. It's worth noticing which Denton businesses are primarily student-facing versus primarily family-facing, because the automation hook is different: student-facing businesses need pre-return booking; family-facing businesses need the same back-to-school recall that works everywhere else.
