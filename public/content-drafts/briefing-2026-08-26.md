## One Insight

**The Addison angle is worth naming explicitly when you walk in.**

Addison is one of the most concentrated service corridors in North Texas — it packs an enormous number of healthcare, beauty, and professional services into a small geography with a high-income resident base and strong commercial traffic from Belt Line Road and Addison Circle. Business owners in Addison know they're in a premium market. They often have higher-than-average revenue per patient/client and are actively trying to grow.

This changes how you open. Instead of "most businesses have a problem where they don't follow up" — which sounds like a problem they might not have — try "the businesses in Addison that are winning the competitive part are doing one thing different from the ones that aren't: they have a system for staying in front of the right clients at the right time, automatically." Addison owners want to believe they're already doing well (and often they are) but they're also competitive enough to be interested in what the leaders in their category are doing differently.

The med spa angle is particularly strong: fall injectable season is real, the lapsed client list is a known asset, and the ROI on a fall campaign is immediately calculable from the menu. If the owner knows their average treatment ticket ($350–$600), you can do the math for them on the spot: "If you have 80 lapsed clients and 15% respond, that's 12 treatments at $450 — $5,400 in revenue from one message."
