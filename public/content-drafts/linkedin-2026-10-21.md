A $2,750 FSA balance expires December 31.

The carryover maximum is $660. Anything above that disappears.

So a patient with $1,400 in their FSA account right now has $740 to spend or lose. Before the end of the year. On dental work, eye exams, frames, PT sessions, or medical aesthetics they've been putting off.

That's the conversation your October outreach can start.

The numbers for HSA are different — $4,300 self-only limit in 2026, and HSA funds technically roll over, but the same psychology applies: patients with a high balance are looking for a reason to use it before year-end. October gives you an honest reason to reach out. November gives you the same reason, but the calendar is filling.

Dental, optometry, PT, and med spa practices that send a specific message in October — "here's what your FSA covers here, and here's what we have open" — don't have a slow November.

The ones that wait find out in November why October mattered.
