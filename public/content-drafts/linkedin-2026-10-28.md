# LinkedIn Post — Wednesday, October 28, 2026

**Topic:** The Thanksgiving dead week — service businesses that fill it intentionally vs. those that accept half a schedule
**Target length:** ~175 words
**Post time:** 8–9am CT

---

Thanksgiving week is five weeks out.

Most service businesses will accept whatever the schedule hands them that week — cancellations, light booking, a Wednesday that looks empty by Monday. Some will fill it intentionally.

Here's what intentional looks like: pull your client list, filter for people who haven't booked in 60+ days, and send one specific message in the next two weeks. Not a generic "we miss you" — something with a reason to act now. "We have three openings the week of November 24th and we're holding them for returning clients until November 10th." The clients who've been meaning to get back in will book. The ones who weren't going to come back regardless won't.

A dental office that does this fills four to six hygiene appointments they would have lost. A salon fills six to eight slots. A personal trainer fills a week that would have run at 60 percent capacity.

The window to run this outreach is the next two weeks. After that, you're reacting to cancellations instead of filling a calendar.
