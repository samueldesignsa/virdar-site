## Area: Irving / Las Colinas / Coppell

Today's focus is the Irving/Las Colinas/Coppell corridor — dense commercial and service business strip along MacArthur Blvd, Story Road, and Denton Tap Road. ## The Businesses

### 1. All American Flooring
**Address:** 3021 W. Airport Freeway, Irving, TX 75062
**What they do:** Residential and commercial flooring installation — hardwood, LVP, tile, carpet. Been at this location about 8 years.
**What I noticed:** Google listing has 34 reviews, most from 2022-2023. No reviews in 2026. Response time on the two recent 3-star reviews was zero — neither answered. No online booking or estimate request form on their website; it just says "call us."

### 10. Coppell Eye Care
**Address:** S. MacArthur Blvd or W. Sandy Lake Rd, Coppell, TX 75019 (verify address)
**What they do:** Optometry — eye exams, glasses, contacts. Single-location private practice in an affluent suburb.
**What I noticed:** September/October is peak season for optometry — back-to-school wave is winding down but FSA/HSA spend deadlines in Q4 drive a second rush. Private practices that don't actively communicate with patients about year-end benefits lose those patients to LensCrafters and Walmart Vision. **Best hook:** Year-end benefits/recall outreach. Pitch the Q4 benefits window directly: "Your patients have FSA/HSA dollars they need to spend by December 31 — if you're not reminding them, you're sending them to your competitors." Ask how many patients are overdue for their annual exam.

## Today's Route

**Suggested order:** Start in Coppell (Denton Tap Rd corridor) → move south into Las Colinas (MacArthur Blvd) → finish on Irving's Story Road / Rochelle Road strip.

Coppell is the highest-income area and sets a good tone for the day. Las Colinas is corporate-dense with strong aesthetics/med spa presence. Irving's older commercial corridors have more independent operators.

Park once on MacArthur Blvd in Las Colinas and walk to 2-3 businesses — the corridor is walkable in a way that Irving's arterials generally aren't.

**Estimated drive time:** 25 minutes from Addison or 35 minutes from Frisco/McKinney.
