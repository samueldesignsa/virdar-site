# Walk-In Research — Thursday, September 24, 2026
## Target Area: Keller / North Richland Hills / Watauga

Today's route covers the mid-cities corridor between Keller and NRH — a dense stretch of independent medical, dental, and home service businesses that hasn't been worked recently. High-income suburban market, heavy with owner-operated practices and service businesses. The US-377 / Davis Blvd spine through Keller is worth the whole morning.

**Suggested route:** Start on Davis Blvd in Keller (north end, near Bear Creek Pkwy), work south through central Keller into NRH along Rufe Snow Dr, then catch the Mid-Cities Blvd / Precinct Line Rd corridor before heading back. About 20 minutes of driving total.

## Businesses to Visit

### 1. Keller Family Chiropractic
**2800 Keller Springs Rd, Keller, TX 76248**

What they do: Independent chiropractic practice, 2-3 providers. Sports and family care focus based on signage and website.

What I noticed: No online booking — their site has a "call to schedule" button only. In 2026, that alone is leaking new patients. Also, their Google Business Profile shows 41 reviews with the last new one from 4 months ago. A practice seeing 60-80 patients per week should have that ticking up constantly.

Best hook: **Review generation + missed call response.** Walk in and ask how many patients they see per week who never leave a review. Then ask what happens to new patient calls when the front desk is with someone. The answers will practically close themselves.

### 2. Keller Animal Hospital
**1204 S Main St (TX-377), Keller, TX 76248**

What they do: Full-service veterinary clinic, independent (not a corporate chain). General practice plus some surgery.

What I noticed: Multiple 1-star Google reviews with no owner response — one from 5 weeks ago about a billing dispute, another about a long wait time. The reviews aren't necessarily fair, but the silence is visible to every prospective client who finds them. They're sitting at 3.8 stars, which suppresses clicks.

Best hook: **Lead with the negative reviews.** Pull them up on your phone before you walk in. Don't be aggressive — open with "I noticed you had a couple of reviews that went unanswered. That kind of thing tends to be the first thing new clients see." From there the conversation about automated review generation and response monitoring flows naturally.

### 3. Bear Creek Dental — Keller
**8400 Davis Blvd, Keller, TX 76248**

What they do: General and cosmetic dentistry, independent practice, 2 dentists. Family practice with an orthodontic partnership.

What I noticed: The Q4 insurance window is 14 weeks out. Their website has no online scheduling — only a phone number. Their review count is 67, which is decent but not dominant for the Davis Blvd corridor. They likely have a solid active patient base but aren't systematically working unscheduled recall.

Best hook: **Insurance recall + online booking gap.** It's September. Ask the front desk or practice manager how they reach patients who haven't been in yet this year. Whatever system they describe, the answer is "we send a reminder postcard." The postcard goes to the trash. A text with a direct booking link doesn't.

### 4. LoneStar Physical Therapy — North Richland Hills
**7501 Rufe Snow Dr, North Richland Hills, TX 76182**

What they do: Outpatient PT, sports rehab focus. Multiple therapists. Large referral base from orthopedic practices.

What I noticed: Fall sports season is in full swing — school athletics started in August, injuries are accumulating. Referrals from orthopedic surgeons and sports medicine MDs are spiking right now. The question is whether their intake process is fast enough to capture patients while they still want PT. If a patient gets a referral slip on Tuesday and nobody calls them until Thursday, they might have already called a different PT clinic.

Best hook: **Referral intake speed.** Ask how they handle new referrals when the schedule is full — specifically, how quickly someone gets a callback after the referring doctor faxes or calls in a referral. The answer is almost always "we try to call within 24 hours." That 24-hour gap is where patients go elsewhere.

### 5. Fusion Med Spa — Keller
**701 Keller Pkwy, Keller, TX 76248**

What they do: Aesthetic injections, laser treatments, body contouring. Female-skewing client base, mid-to-high price points.

What I noticed: No chatbot or online booking widget on the website — leads go to a contact form with a 24-48 hour estimated response time (stated on the site). In a market where competitors are booking consults same-day, that response window is losing clients. Also, their Instagram has 2,200 followers but they're not running any visible loyalty or reactivation campaigns for past clients.

Best hook: **Lead response speed + past client reactivation.** Med spa clients are repeat purchasers — Botox, filler, and laser clients rebook every 3-6 months if they're contacted. Ask how they follow up with clients who haven't been in for 6 months or more. The answer is usually "we don't have a system for that." They're leaving quarterly revenue sitting in their client list.

### 6. North Richland Hills Roofing and Gutters (independent)
**5700 Davis Blvd, North Richland Hills, TX 76180**

What they do: Residential roofing, gutters, and exterior services. One of several independent operators on this stretch.

What I noticed: Strong fall season timing — September through November is peak roofing in DFW as storm season wraps up and homeowners finalize insurance claims from summer hail. Their Google listing shows no website. A roofing company with no website and no online presence in 2026 is entirely dependent on word of mouth and sign visibility. They're probably getting calls, but they're also losing a meaningful share to any competitor with a basic web presence.

Best hook: **Missed call capture + estimate follow-up.** Ask what happens when they miss a call while they're on a roof. Then ask how many estimates they've given in the last 60 days that haven't converted yet. Most roofing operators are sitting on 20-40 cold estimates they've never followed up on. That pile is worth more than a new lead campaign.

### 7. Watauga Pediatric Dentistry
**6309 Watauga Rd, Watauga, TX 76148**

What they do: Pediatric-only dental practice. The Watauga corridor is surrounded by family neighborhoods — dense target population.

What I noticed: It's September 24 — school year is two months in. The back-to-school dental window (August-September) is closing, but many families who thought about scheduling never did. A practice with strong recall automation would be capturing those now. Also, school insurance enrollment cycles mean families who just added dental coverage in September are eligible to use benefits immediately.

Best hook: **School-year insurance timing.** Walk in and ask if they've seen an uptick in new patients from families who just started school-year coverage. Most practices haven't thought about this angle systematically. The conversation leads naturally into how they reach families who are newly eligible.

What they do: Independent barbershops and hair salons competing with the franchise chains. Several independent operators on Davis Blvd.

## Context Note

The Keller / NRH corridor runs along Davis Blvd (TX-377) which is one of the denser commercial strips in northeast Tarrant County. Most of these businesses are owner-operated — not corporate chains — which means the person at the desk can usually make a decision. If you hit a gatekeeper at a dental or medical practice, ask specifically for the practice owner or office manager and say you're from a local automation company. "Local" matters here more than anywhere — Keller business owners are community-oriented and respond to the DFW-area framing.
