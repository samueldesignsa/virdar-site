## Black Friday Walk-In Strategy

**Area: Downtown Plano Arts District + Preston Road Corridor**

Downtown Plano on Black Friday is the right call. The Arts District has the highest concentration of independent businesses in Collin County — coffee shops, restaurants, boutiques, salons — all within a 10-minute walk. It draws a deliberately local crowd on Black Friday, which means the owners are there, it's busy but not chaotic, and the customers around you are exactly the kind of people who recommend local businesses.

**Top 3 opens for tomorrow:**

1. **Gift card angle** — "With the holidays here, are you actively selling gift cards? A lot of shops in the area have really underutilized that revenue stream." Every business in the district has this gap.

2. **Catering inquiry speed** — "Corporate holiday party catering bookings are happening in the next two weeks — do you have a process for responding to those inquiries fast enough to close them?" Applies to every restaurant and coffee shop.

3. **January cliff** — "What does January look like on your calendar? A lot of businesses have a great holiday season and then walk into January empty." The timing makes this question land — they're in the middle of the holiday rush and January is visibly coming.

**Today's blog post** (coffee shops) is a direct credential at 1418 Coffee, Local Good Coffee, and Mudleaf Coffee. Worth mentioning: "We just published a breakdown of exactly what this looks like for coffee shops — I can send you the link."

## One Insight

**Black Friday is the best day of the year to make the independent-vs.-chain argument in real time.**

Walk into 1418 Coffee or Bibliobar tomorrow and you'll see it: people who specifically drove to Downtown Plano instead of going to a Starbucks or a Barnes & Noble. They made an active choice to support a local business. That choice is emotional, deliberate, and something those customers are proud of.

That's the opening. When you talk to the owner of an independent shop on Black Friday, they know viscerally what their customers value and why they chose them. The gap they feel most acutely on Black Friday is not "how do I get more customers" — it's "how do I keep the ones who showed up today coming back in January?" The gift card conversation, the lapsed customer reactivation, the follow-up system — all of it speaks directly to what they're experiencing right now. 

The owners you'll meet tomorrow are already in the mindset. You just have to show up with the right question.
