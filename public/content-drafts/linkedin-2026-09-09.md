A window cleaning company in Keller does excellent work. Every job gets a solid review when you ask the customer face to face.

The problem: nobody asks.

The crew packs up, drives to the next job, and the customer who would have given five stars is back at their desk by the time they think about it. The moment passes.

That same company has 17 Google reviews. Their competitor across town — same quality, maybe less — has 94.

The one with 94 reviews shows up first when someone in Keller searches "window cleaning" on a Saturday morning. They get the call without making a single additional sale.

The difference isn't the work. It's a text sent within an hour of job completion with a link and two sentences.

That's it. One automated message, triggered when the job closes.

The businesses winning in local search aren't outspending anyone. They're just asking — at the right moment, consistently, for every job.

If your review count hasn't moved in six months, that's the fix.
