## Territory: Grapevine / Coppell / Trophy Club

---

**Why this corridor today:**

Grapevine, Coppell, and Trophy Club haven't been covered this month. This is a distinct NW DFW corridor — different character from the Frisco/Prosper growth corridor Beverly walked last week. Grapevine has a genuine historic downtown (Main Street) with walkable independent businesses. Coppell has a dense professional and medical park base along Sandy Lake and MacArthur. Trophy Club / Roanoke is a newer growth edge with the same "built fast, never set up systems" profile Beverly has found productive all month.

**Key timing today:**
- **Two-a-days START today (July 28).** Grapevine ISD athletes are on the field this morning. Carroll ISD (Southlake/Grapevine overlap) two-a-days also start today. Every medical practice in this corridor serving athletes is either organized or reactive right now.
- **School starts August 18** for Grapevine-Colleyville ISD and Coppell ISD — 3 weeks from today. This is slightly later than Frisco/Prosper (Aug 12), which means the back-to-school window is still open here. Beverly can walk in and say: "You have until August 18. The ISDs that started earlier already had this conversation last week."
- **Carroll ISD football** is a nationally-ranked program. Southlake Carroll practices and many of the families who support that program live and spend in Grapevine. Any practice or business that serves families in this area has a highly motivated athletic family base.

**Routing suggestion:**
- Morning (9–11:30am): Coppell medical/professional corridor (Sandy Lake Rd, MacArthur Blvd) — primary care, PT, chiro, optometry, urgent care, dental

### 8. HVAC / Home Services — Coppell or Trophy Club commercial corridor
**What they do:** Independent HVAC, plumbing, or electrical contractor serving Grapevine, Coppell, Trophy Club, and surrounding suburbs
**What to notice:** August is peak season for HVAC in North Texas — 105°F outdoor temperatures mean AC calls hit their annual maximum. The problem for independent HVAC contractors in summer is not lack of demand; it's that they're overwhelmed with service calls and letting estimates and follow-ups fall through the cracks. The high-revenue decision isn't whether to answer the service call — it's whether to follow up on the equipment replacement estimate given to a homeowner whose 14-year-old unit is still running but barely.

"Estimates given in June and July on aging equipment — the homeowner is going to make that decision before fall. ## Beverly's Angle Today

Today is the first day of two-a-days. The urgency that's been building since July 21 is now live. Beverly can walk into any medical or health practice in this corridor and say: "Two-a-days started this morning. Did you send your outreach?" That question opens every conversation, and the answer is almost always no.

For the medical corridor (Coppell) — lead with optometry, PT, and chiro because those practices have the highest density of school-age and athletic patients.

For Grapevine Main Street — the conversation is different. These businesses have local-identity customer relationships, not foot-traffic customer relationships. The Grapevine restaurant or salon owner knows their customers personally. The pitch isn't "get more customers" — it's "talk to the ones who already love you before they fall out of their routine."

For Trophy Club / Roanoke — these are newer businesses with the "built fast, never organized" profile. They're often 2–4 years old, have 300–800 clients in their system, and have never done anything systematic with that list. The urgency hook is the same (two-a-days, school starts August 18), but the baseline conversation is "you have a list — have you ever used it?"

**Grapevine-Colleyville ISD and Coppell ISD start August 18 — 21 days from today.** Beverly has the 3-week window argument for this territory that Frisco/Prosper didn't have last week (Frisco started Aug 12 — now 15 days away). The practices here have slightly more runway. The message is: "You have 3 weeks. The Frisco practices that waited last week now have 2 weeks. Don't use your extra week to wait."
