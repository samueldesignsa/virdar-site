**Target Area:** Park Cities / Highland Park / University Park

---

## Why This Area Today

Park Cities is one of the highest-income zip codes in Texas. Business owners here have revenue and spending authority. They're also the most likely to say "I already have people handling that" — meaning the hook has to be specific and outcome-focused, not technology-led. Lead with the December/January timing angle (every business here sells to customers making holiday and New Year decisions right now) and the past-customer angle (they almost certainly have a customer list they're not working).
