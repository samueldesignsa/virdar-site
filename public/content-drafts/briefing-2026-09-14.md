## One Insight

**The southern Dallas County market is one of the best untapped walk-in territories in DFW — and it has a different buyer psychology than north Dallas.**

Every time the walk-in rotation has gone north — Frisco, McKinney, Plano, Prosper — the businesses are more sophisticated, more vendor-saturated, and more likely to have already heard some version of the AI pitch. That's fine, and those markets are worth working. But there's a real advantage in the Cedar Hill / DeSoto / Duncanville corridor that's easy to miss.

Business owners in southern Dallas County are running their businesses at full tilt. They're not going to pitch conferences or reading marketing newsletters. They rely on word of mouth, Google reviews, and repeat customers — and almost none of them have a system working their customer list. When you walk in and tell a pest control owner in DeSoto that their spring customers haven't been reached since June, they don't say "we already have a vendor for that." They say "yeah, I know — we just haven't gotten around to it."

That's the opening. The problem is obvious, the timing is perfect, and the solution is specific. Southern Dallas County isn't a secondary market. It's a primary one that just hasn't been prioritized yet.
