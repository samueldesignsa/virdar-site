# LinkedIn Post — Wednesday, October 7, 2026

**Topic:** October estimate-backlog window for construction trades — the follow-up call nobody makes

**Target length:** ~170 words
**Post time:** 8–9am CT

---

Most contractors are leaving Q4 money on the table right now.

Between June and September, you gave quotes on jobs that never closed. The homeowner asked for a number, got it, and went quiet. You assumed they went with someone else.

Some of them did. But a real percentage of those customers are still undecided. They pushed the timeline back. They were waiting for school to start, a vacation to end, or financing to come through. October is when those decisions happen — because the holidays are coming, and people who want something done before Thanksgiving are making calls this week.

The follow-up most contractors don't make: a single message to every open estimate from summer. No hard sell. Just: "We're heading into our fall scheduling window. If your project is back on your radar, we still have availability in October and November."

In roofing, remodeling, or painting, one closed estimate from that list is worth $8,000–$25,000.

How many open summer estimates are sitting in your system right now?
