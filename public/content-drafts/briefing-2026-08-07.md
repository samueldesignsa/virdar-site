### 1. Blog Post #73 — AI Automation for Independent Bookkeeping Businesses
**File:** `public/blog/ai-automation-for-independent-bookkeeping-businesses.html`

This was the first priority industry on the September backlog. It fills a meaningful gap: the blog library has a CPA/accounting firm post, but bookkeeping businesses operate on an entirely different model — recurring monthly revenue, 15–50 client base, revenue math driven by close rate and churn rather than tax engagement fees. Different problems, different automations, different math.

The post covers five revenue problems:

1. **Inquiry Response Speed:** Firm receiving 4 inquiries/month, responding same-day to half and 3+ days to the other half. Close rate difference (65% same-day vs. 18% delayed) produces 0.7 additional clients/month closed. At $9,000 lifetime value: **$75,600 in additional annual client revenue from response speed alone.**

2. **Client Onboarding Friction:** Average 22 days from signing to first reconciliation due to document and access back-and-forth. Automated onboarding portal drops this to 6 days. First-90-day churn drops from 18% to 7%. **1 additional retained client per year × $8,000 remaining lifetime value = $8,000 from onboarding speed.**

3. **Monthly Deliverable Follow-Up:** 35% of clients never acknowledge monthly reports; churn rate for unengaged clients is roughly double engaged clients. Automated 3-day "did you review your numbers?" follow-up lifts acknowledgment to 65%. **4 additional retained clients per year × $7,000 average remaining lifetime value = $28,000 from monthly follow-up.**

4. **Year-End Document Collection:** January is paralyzed by chasing clients for December docs. Automated December 20 / January 8 / January 15 reminder sequence lifts receipt-by-Jan-10 rate from 40% to 75%. **10 recovered productive hours in January plus capacity to serve 2 additional year-end clients.**

5. **Former Client Reactivation:** 35 former clients in database. Semi-annual check-in at 4% response rate = 1.4 re-engagements per year. **$12,600 in recovered annual revenue from two messages per year.**
