## One Insight

West Plano is underworked for a simple reason: it doesn't feel like a walk-in area. The businesses are in office parks and professional strips, not visible storefronts on a main road. The result is that most vendor outreach in this corridor is appointment-based, and walk-ins feel unusually personal. That's an advantage. A walk-in to a dental office in a Preston Road office park doesn't feel like a cold call — it feels like someone specifically sought them out. The dentists and optometrists in this corridor are also serving the highest-income patient base in the DFW suburbs, which means they have more revenue to protect from lapsing benefits and more to gain from proactive outreach. The FSA pitch lands harder here because the average patient benefit balance is higher. Worth treating this corridor as a priority area, not a secondary stop.
