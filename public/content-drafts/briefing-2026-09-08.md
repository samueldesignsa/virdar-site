## One Insight

**Handyman services are worth a direct outreach push in parallel with walk-ins.**

Here's the thing about handymen: they're almost never in an office. The owner of a two-person operation in Mansfield or Grand Prairie is in a truck or on a job site. Walk-in works better for sedentary businesses — dental, chiro, salon, fitness, medspa. Handymen are better reached through direct outreach: Facebook business pages, Nextdoor pro profiles, Google Business Profile "message" features, or a phone call in the early morning before they're on the first job.

If you find a handyman business on today's walk (they sometimes have small storefronts near hardware stores or in light industrial strips), use the pitch from the blog: "Your past customers are your best source of repeat work. The ones you did jobs for last fall — is anything reaching out to them before October, or does repeat business just come in when people remember to call?" That line works.

But for proactive outreach to this category, consider building a list of Mansfield/Arlington handymen from Google Business Profile, contacting them via the business phone or Google message feature Tuesday through Thursday morning (before jobs start). The blog post we published today gives you a reason to reach out: "I just wrote a breakdown of how handyman businesses are using automation to work their customer lists — if you want me to send it, I can."

Content-as-outreach is the cleanest cold opener for this category.
