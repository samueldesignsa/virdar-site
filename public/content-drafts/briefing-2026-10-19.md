## One Insight

The HEB corridor is worth a second visit. The AA/Bell employee demographic is unusually benefit-rich for a market that doesn't get treated as a premium territory — these are working families who have employer plans that rival what you'd find in Southlake or Westlake, but without the competition for those clients that characterizes the Southlake market. The pitch lands harder here than in a market where the business owner has heard the "year-end benefits" angle before — because in HEB, many of them haven't.
