# Walk-In Research — Wednesday, July 8, 2026

**Territory: Rowlett / Rockwall, TX**

New territory. Rowlett and Rockwall sit east of Dallas along Lake Ray Hubbard — a stretch of eastern DFW that agency outreach almost never reaches because it requires a deliberate drive east on I-30 or SH-66. These cities have 75,000 and 50,000 residents respectively, a mix of longtime families and recent transplants, and a business corridor that has been growing steadily without being oversaturated by tech vendors or agency canvassing.

## Target Businesses

### 1. Rowlett Family Dentistry area (SH-66 / Merritt Road corridor, Rowlett)
**What they do:** Independent family dental practice serving Garland ISD and Rowlett families
**The observation:** Garland ISD starts August 11 — that's 34 days. Pediatric back-to-school appointments need to happen in July. Any independent dental practice that hasn't sent a recall campaign to overdue pediatric patients this week is going to face August compression when everyone calls at once. The practices that distributed appointments across July and August already have their schedule protected. The ones that haven't sent anything yet still have two weeks to act.
**Best hook:** "Garland ISD starts August 11. That's 34 days. The families booking pediatric checkups right now are booking July — not August when your schedule compresses. What does your current July calendar look like?"

### 2. Speech therapy or occupational therapy practice (Rockwall / Summer Lee Drive area)
**What they do:** Independent pediatric therapy practice (speech-language pathology, OT, or both)
**The observation:** This is the sharpest timing in the year for therapy practices — back-to-school referrals from pediatricians and school screenings are incoming right now, and families who got referrals in May or June haven't booked yet. The practices filling September are reaching that waitlist in July, not September. Referrals that don't convert in 72 hours go cold at a 40% rate.

## What's Different About Rowlett / Rockwall

Eastern DFW is the part of the metro that every agency ignores because it's not on the way to anything. Plano, Frisco, McKinney, Southlake — those are the default destinations for outreach, which means the competition in those cities is measurably higher. A business owner in Rockwall who's been running for 12 years has been pitched by software vendors and marketing agencies, but rarely by anyone walking in with specific revenue math for their industry and a clear answer to "what does it actually do."

Rockwall has a tight, community-oriented business environment — owners know each other and talk. One good conversation with an owner who connects Beverly to two others is worth three cold walk-ins in Plano.

The RISD back-to-school date (August 18) is one week later than MISD and Garland ISD. That gives Rockwall family-serving businesses one extra week of July compared to Mansfield and Rowlett — but they need to start outreach now regardless, because the fall schedule builds from the first week of August whether the owner is ready or not.
