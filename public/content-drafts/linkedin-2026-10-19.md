**Topic:** Holiday booking window is open right now — most businesses are treating it like it's weeks away
**Target length:** ~170 words
**Post time:** 8–9am CT

Thanksgiving is 5 weeks out. Christmas is 9.

The businesses that fill their holiday calendar are reaching their past clients right now. Not in November. Now.

This applies everywhere — boarding facilities, med spas, photography studios, salons, dentists, HVAC contractors who want planned pre-winter visits before emergency season hits.

The logic is simple: your past clients are making holiday plans this week. They'll book whoever reaches them first with a clear ask. A message that says "our holiday schedule is filling — here are the dates we have" beats waiting for them to remember you exist.

The businesses that wait until November find out in November that the calendar is already half-filled — with the clients of whoever acted in October.

You don't need a campaign. You need a list of past clients and one specific message about the holiday dates you have available.

The window is open right now. Most of your competitors aren't using it.
