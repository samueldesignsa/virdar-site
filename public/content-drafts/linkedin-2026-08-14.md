# LinkedIn Post — Friday, August 14, 2026

Q4 starts in 7 weeks.

Most practices won't touch their patient recall campaigns until mid-October — two weeks into Q4 — and the response rates will be mediocre. Not because the campaigns are bad, but because they're late.

The patients who have unused insurance benefits and haven't been in since spring made their decisions about fall appointments in August. They decided in the back-to-school chaos whether they were going to deal with their dental cleaning, their eye exam, their chiropractic follow-up. If you're in their inbox in September — when they've just gotten kids settled into school and are thinking about their own schedules again — you catch them in the decision window.

If you're in their inbox in October, they've either already booked somewhere else or decided to wait until next year.

The practices I see get the most out of Q4 aren't the ones with the best campaigns. They're the ones that run them three weeks earlier than everyone else.

If your patient database has people who are 12+ months overdue and still haven't heard from you, the window to catch them before Q4 is right now. Not in two months when Q4 is already underway.
