## Targets

### 1. Independent Dermatology Practice — Southlake area
**Where to look:** E Southlake Blvd / N Carroll Ave corridor, or Village Center / Shops of Southlake area. Several independent derm and med spa practices cluster here.
**What they do:** Medical and cosmetic dermatology — acne, rosacea, Botox/filler, laser, skin cancer screening.
**Specific observation:** Dermatology practices in affluent areas have high cosmetic patient volume but almost none have a systematic 90-day follow-up for Botox/filler patients. The cosmetic patient who lapses here isn't going to a cheaper provider — she's just drifting. A re-engagement message would pull her back immediately.
**Hook:** "Your Botox patients from March and April are at 90 to 105 days right now. How many of them have rebooked? The ones who don't get a message at day 90 usually don't rebook until they notice the lines are back — which could be month five or six. What does your cosmetic follow-up look like?"

### 2. Boutique Fitness Studio — Southlake / Colleyville
**Where to look:** Village Center Pkwy, Southlake Blvd near Kimball Ave, or near Central Market on E Southlake Blvd. Barry's-style or yoga/Pilates studio.
**What they do:** Group fitness classes, memberships, possibly personal training.
**Specific observation:** July is the re-engagement window after post-July 4 lapse (covered in the July 7 LinkedIn). Studios in high-income areas often have members who travel extensively in summer. The members who skipped two weeks for July 4 are in the "will they come back or won't they" window right now.
**Hook:** "What does your attendance look like this week compared to the first week of June? The members who've missed two weeks in a row since July 4 — what does your re-engagement sequence look like for them?"
**Revenue frame:** Members who lapse in July and aren't re-engaged by end of July cancel in August. In a boutique studio, each lost member is $150–$250/month. 10 recovered members = $1,500–$2,500/month in retained revenue.

### 3. Independent Orthodontic Practice — Southlake / Trophy Club
**Where to look:** N Carroll Ave or E Southlake Blvd near dental/medical cluster. May share space in a professional building with dental.
**What they do:** Braces, Invisalign, retainers. School-year oriented — most new starts happen before or just after school starts.
**Specific observation:** Carroll ISD families are heavily invested in their kids' appearance and academic/extracurricular performance. Orthodontic care here is near-universal in the middle school age group. The window for pre-school-year starts closes fast once school is in session and families' schedules compress.
**Hook:** "You've probably got a list of consult patients from spring who haven't started yet. The families on that list are making decisions in July — this month. They're not going to start in September when their kid is in full-swing fall schedule. What does your outreach to unconverted consults look like right now?"
**Revenue frame:** An unconverted ortho consult from March that starts in August = $5,500–$7,500 in treatment revenue. For a practice with 15 unconverted spring consults, converting 8 of them with a July outreach = $44,000–$60,000.

### 5. Private Academic Enrichment or Tutoring Center — Southlake / Westlake
**Where to look:** Near Carroll High School, or in Westlake/Dove Road corridor. Kumon-style or private tutoring, or a coding/STEM enrichment program.
**What they do:** Academic enrichment, test prep, subject-specific tutoring, STEM programs.
**Specific observation:** Southlake Carroll is one of the highest-achieving school districts in Texas. Parents here invest heavily in academic enrichment and start preparing early. Fall enrollment decisions happen in July, not September. An enrichment center that doesn't reach its past-student families in July loses those families to fall sports and other commitments before the school year starts.
**Hook:** "Your families from last school year are deciding right now — this month — what their kids are doing in the fall. Are you reaching them specifically, with a returning-family priority message, before you open to new students? The ones you reach in July commit. The ones you reach in September are already overcommitted."
**Revenue frame:** Tutoring in Southlake averages $80–$120/hour, with most students on weekly recurring sessions. One additional student = $320–$480/month × 9 months = $2,880–$4,320/year.

### 6. Med Spa — Southlake / Colleyville
**Where to look:** Village Center or Southlake Town Square area. Many independent med spas cluster near retail corridors in affluent suburbs.
**What they do:** Botox, filler, laser, body contouring, chemical peels, HydraFacials.
**Specific observation:** Summer in DFW is a mixed season for med spas — some treatments (laser, chemical peels) are limited by sun exposure, so the late-summer pipeline depends on treatments that don't have sun restrictions. The clients who did laser treatments in spring are now 90–120 days out. They're due for follow-up. Nobody is calling them.
**Hook:** "Your spring laser and peel clients are at 90 to 120 days. Have they rebooked? The clients who don't get a message at that window usually come back in November when they finally notice the result has faded — or they go somewhere else who did reach out."
**Revenue frame:** A med spa with 60 spring treatment clients, 40% of whom haven't rebooked: 24 lapsed clients × $350 average treatment = $8,400 waiting in the lapsed list. A reactivation message gets 50–60% of them back.

### 7. Independent Optometry — Colleyville / Grapevine
**Where to look:** Near GCISD school corridors or Colleyville shopping center. Independent O.D. (not LensCrafters or Pearle).
**What they do:** Eye exams, pediatric vision, glasses, contact lens fittings.
**Specific observation:** GCISD starts August 10. The optometrist who reaches GCISD families in July fills August with pediatric exams. The one who doesn't is taking walk-ins in September.
**Hook:** "GCISD starts August 10. A child who needs glasses for the first time doesn't know they need them — they've always seen the world that way. ### 8. Youth Fine Arts or STEM Academy — Southlake
**Where to look:** Near Carroll ISD schools or in retail/office strip near Kimball Ave. Music, visual arts, coding, or debate programs.
**What they do:** Enrichment classes for school-age children — recurring weekly sessions, fall/spring enrollment cycles.
**Specific observation:** These programs compete directly with fall sports for Saturday morning and weekday afternoon time slots. The families that commit in July have committed before soccer and football schedules are distributed (which happens August 1–2 at most schools). The programs that reach families in July get the commitment before the conflict exists.
**Hook:** "Fall sports schedules come out August 1. After that, families know their Saturday mornings are locked. If you're reaching your returning families now, in July, you're getting the commitment before the conflict. If you're waiting until August, you're competing with an already-distributed schedule."
**Revenue frame:** Fall session for a STEM or arts program: $200–$400/child for 12 weeks. 10 additional returning enrollments × $300 = $3,000 in fall revenue from families who already know the program.
