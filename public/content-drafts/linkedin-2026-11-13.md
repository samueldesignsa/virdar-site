The gift card is the highest-margin product most service businesses have.

A lash studio charges $175 for a full set. The gift card for that session has the same price, costs nothing to deliver, and 15–20% are never redeemed — pure margin.

A day spa charges $95 for a massage. The gift card gets bought for Christmas, used in January, and brings a new regular client into the schedule.

Most service businesses sell gift cards because someone asks. The lash studio has a "gift cards available" note at checkout. The massage therapist has a PayPal link somewhere on her website.

None of them have sent a message this month that says: "Looking for a gift for someone who's hard to shop for? Here's what we offer."

That email, sent twice — once in November, once the first week of December — is worth $5,000 to $12,000 in gift card revenue for the average service business that hasn't done it before.

The window opens now. It closes around December 20.

The list is there. Use it.
