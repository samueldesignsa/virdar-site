Fall HVAC season doesn't start in September. It starts in August.

The HVAC companies that are fully booked through October — no scrambling, no gaps between jobs, no slow weeks in late September — aren't booking those appointments in October. They're filling them now.

What they're doing: pulling their maintenance agreement list, identifying every customer whose system hasn't had a fall check, and sending a simple message. "Your system is due for its fall tune-up before the first cold front. Here's a link to book." That's it. No sales pitch. No discount. Just a timely message to a customer who already trusts them.

What the other companies are doing: waiting for inbounds. Hoping people remember them. Watching the calendar flip to September and wondering why the phone isn't ringing.

The difference isn't marketing. It's not a new website. It's a list they already have and a message they could send today.

The first cold front in DFW comes in October. The service calls come fast when it does. Which side of that do you want to be on?
