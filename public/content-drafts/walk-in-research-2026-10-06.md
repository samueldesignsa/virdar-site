# Walk-In Research — Tuesday, October 6, 2026

Southlake, Grapevine, and Colleyville are among the highest-income suburbs in the Dallas-Fort Worth metro. Average household income in Southlake exceeds $230,000. The business landscape here skews toward owner-operated practices and service companies that have grown organically with the residential base. They are not under-resourced; they are under-systematized. The walk-in conversation here isn't "can you afford this" — it's "your revenue is growing faster than your processes." Last covered in this territory rotation: not recently. Good Tuesday territory — Monday walk-ins have already happened, and owners are more settled mid-week.
