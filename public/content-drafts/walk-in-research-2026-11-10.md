# Walk-In Research — Tuesday, November 10, 2026
## Area: Addison / Carrollton / Farmers Branch (North Dallas Inner Suburbs)

Addison is one of the densest commercial corridors in North Texas relative to its size — more restaurants per capita than any city in the country, a substantial base of independent medical and dental practices, and a large daytime population from the surrounding corporate office campuses. Carrollton and Farmers Branch border it and share the same commercial density without as much competition from other vendors. This area has not been covered recently.

**Suggested route**: Start on Addison Pkwy / Belt Line Rd in Addison, work west into Carrollton's Old Town corridor (Beltline/Josey area), then north on Marsh Lane into Farmers Branch. 2–3 hours, 5–7 stops.

**Tuesday note**: Tuesday mid-morning is one of the best times for walk-in outreach. The Monday rush has cleared, decision-makers are on-site and not in weekend recovery mode, and it's early enough in the week that they're open to something new before the week gets full.

## Route Notes

**Addison** is compact — most of the commercial corridor is within 2 miles of Belt Line Rd and Addison Pkwy. Walk-ins are easy here because the businesses are dense and parking is not a problem outside of restaurant lunch hours (11am–1pm).

**Carrollton** Old Town is a walkable historic commercial district — concentrated, easy to cover on foot, and the businesses there tend to be owner-operated (not chain managers). This is a good walk-in environment.

**Farmers Branch** runs along Marsh Lane / Valley View — a strip-center corridor that's less concentrated but has lower competition from other vendors compared to Addison.

*Verify addresses with Google Maps first. The Addison/Carrollton corridor is well-covered by Yelp and Google, so checking recent reviews and online booking status before each walk-in takes 2 minutes and gives you a specific observation to open with.*
