# LinkedIn Post — Friday, September 4, 2026

**Topic:** The businesses filling their October schedules right now all did the same thing.

**Post:**

---

A carpet cleaning company in north Texas has 160 customers in their contact list. Some came in once. Some came in twice. A handful are regulars.

They've never sent a follow-up message to any of them.

Last September, a national franchise opened six miles away. The franchise has a corporate system that texts every customer 11 months after their last cleaning. That one text — automated, impersonal, sent by software — books 20–30 appointments before October starts.

The independent still has better trucks, better technicians, and a better reputation among the people who know them. They just never ask.

September is the one month where the calendar for Q4 gets set. The families that host Thanksgiving and Christmas are thinking about their floors right now. The businesses that fill October in September are the ones that reached out. The ones scrambling in November are the ones that waited.

This isn't a marketing problem. It's a timing problem. The list exists. The customers want the service. The only question is whether someone sends the message before they search Google and find someone who did.
