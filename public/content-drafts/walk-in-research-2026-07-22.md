# Walk-In Research — Wednesday, July 22, 2026
## Territory: Frisco / Little Elm (Preston Road North / FM 423 Corridor), TX

**Why Frisco / Little Elm today:**
Frisco is one of the fastest-growing cities in the United States — population crossed 260,000 in 2025, adding roughly 12,000 residents per year. Little Elm and the Frisco ETJ to the west are developing at the same pace. This growth produces a specific independent business profile: practices opened in the last 3 to 5 years, owners who built their patient base from scratch in a new market, and zero inherited systems from a predecessor. These are the owners most likely to have never set up automated patient communication — not because they don't want it, but because they built their practice around working by hand during growth and haven't stopped to build systems because growth masked the inefficiency.

Two-a-days start July 28 — **6 days.** School starts for Frisco ISD: **August 12** — 21 days. (Frisco ISD starts earlier than RISD, Plano ISD, and Allen ISD.) This is critical: Beverly has 6 days fewer to the back-to-school window in Frisco than in the territories covered this week. The sports physical conversation here is more urgent today than it was in Addison yesterday.
