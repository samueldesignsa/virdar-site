# Walk-In Research — Tuesday, October 13, 2026
## Territory: Flower Mound / Lewisville / Highland Village

Flower Mound and Highland Village are among the wealthiest municipalities in Texas by median household income. The residential base is predominantly dual-income professional families, many of whom commute to corporate campuses along the Lewisville/Grapevine corridor — Fidelity Investments' Westlake campus, Nokia's US headquarters, and multiple DFW Airport-adjacent employers draw a workforce that is heavily benefits-loaded and financially organized. Flower Mound's housing stock is primarily 1995–2015 construction, which means the water heater and HVAC replacement windows are live right now in a very high concentration of homes. Highland Village sits on the eastern edge of Flower Mound, along Turbeville Rd and FM 2499 — a commercial spine dense with owner-operated healthcare and service businesses.

October hooks: year-end FSA/HSA/dental/vision benefits (very strong in this benefits-saturated market), pre-winter HVAC and plumbing maintenance, fall aesthetic booking season (Botox/filler timelines for Thanksgiving/Christmas), Q4 estimate backlog follow-up for contractors.

**Tuesday note:** Tuesday morning is a strong walk-in day. Decision-makers who were in planning mode Monday are settled in by Tuesday — schedule is running, but they haven't hit Wednesday crunch yet. 8:30–10:30am is ideal for healthcare practices. Contractors are reachable at their office/yard before 9am.

## 1. Flower Mound Family Dentistry (or comparable practice on FM 2499 / Long Prairie Rd)
**Address:** Around 3800–4200 Long Prairie Rd, Flower Mound TX 75022 (Long Prairie Rd / FM 2499 is the main commercial spine; several owner-operated dental practices cluster near the Long Prairie / Cross Timbers intersection)
**What they do:** General and cosmetic dentistry serving Flower Mound's residential base — predominantly employer-insured families with Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, or United Concordia plans through Fidelity, Nokia, and other major area employers.
**What I noticed:** October is the live recall window for dental practices in a market with this benefit profile. Flower Mound families are benefit-aware and financially organized — the patients who haven't used their annual cleaning benefit are not forgetting because they don't care about dental health. They're forgetting because the practice hasn't reached out. A dental practice with 800 active patients in this market likely has 150–200 patients who have a fall cleaning due and zero outreach from the practice. Most of those patients have $1,000–$1,500 in available dental benefits expiring December 31. The practice that reaches them in October fills its November and converts some of those cleanings to restorative work.
**Best hook:** "October is the window when dental recall outreach actually converts here — your patients have employer Delta Dental and Cigna plans expiring December 31 and a lot of them are benefit-aware and motivated. Are you running proactive outreach to patients who haven't scheduled a fall cleaning, or is it mostly self-driven?"

**Best hook:** "Fall is the live booking season for aesthetic practices — patients who want results by Thanksgiving need to start treatments this month. The patients in your database who came in once and haven't been back are your best October outreach target. They already trust the practice. Are there 60–80 patients you haven't reached out to who came in at some point this year but haven't rebooked?"
