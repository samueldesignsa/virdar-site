## One Insight

Richardson is the most underworked corporate-employed territory in the entire walk-in research series. The Telecom Corridor — Ericsson, Samsung Electronics America, Nokia, Fujitsu, all within a few miles of each other on Telecom Parkway and Greenville Avenue — employs thousands of engineers and corporate managers who live in Richardson and north Plano, carry employer HDHP plans, and use the independent dental, chiropractic, and optometry practices on Campbell Road and Belt Line Road. These patients understand insurance math, respond to specific numbers, and will book when the argument is quantified — not when they're given a generic benefit expiration notice. The distinctive pitch here is the HDHP deductible reset, not the FSA: "You've paid $1,800 toward your $2,500 deductible this year. That resets January 1. October is when addressing the thing you've been managing costs least." The practices in Richardson who hear that framing — and who Beverly can help reach those patients with that exact message — have a cleaner conversion path than the FSA pitch, because the audience already understands what a deductible is. Worth a second visit to this corridor in November if today produces any warm contacts.
