Prosper alone permitted over 4,000 new homes in 2025. The new-mover capture angle, which applies in every Prosper and Celina conversation, is something Beverly has not pitched yet and represents a meaningfully different value proposition than the benefit-expiration message used everywhere else. If today produces warm contacts in Prosper or Celina, those are likely to be higher-conviction leads because the owners already understand they need infrastructure — they just haven't built it yet.
