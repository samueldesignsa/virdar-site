## One Insight

**Today is the best day of the year to get honest answers from business owners about their revenue gaps.**

Thanksgiving Eve is unique: owners are mentally stepping back from the daily grind, taking stock of the year, and doing the natural accounting that the holiday forces. "How did this year go? What do I want to change?" Those aren't questions they ask in October or February. They ask them in the last week of November.

That mental state makes them unusually candid in a conversation. When you walk into a Bishop Arts business today and ask "how's the holiday been treating you so far?" — you're likely to get a real answer. Not the professional deflection of "great, really busy," but something actual: "Honestly it's been slow since Halloween" or "We're booked through Christmas but I have no idea what January looks like." Either answer is a direct line to what Virdar solves.

The pitch today isn't a pitch. It's a conversation about what the next 60 days look like and whether they have a system for it. Most won't. And Thanksgiving Eve is the day they're most willing to say so.
