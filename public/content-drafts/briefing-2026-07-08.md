## One Insight

Speech therapy and OT practices are the most under-pitched category in the Virdar portfolio right now. Every other practice type in the library — dental, optometry, chiropractic, med spa, fitness — has had some version of agency or software vendor outreach. Pediatric therapy practices have had almost none. The owners in this space are clinical, not business-development oriented, which means they've heard less sales noise and are often genuinely curious about what operations tools exist for their specific situation.

The referral window problem is one every speech-language pathologist knows viscerally — they've watched families fall through that gap. Walking in with "here's what the conversion rate looks like when you respond to the referral same-day versus calling back Wednesday" is not a pitch to them. It's a description of something they've seen happen in their own practice and never had a name for. That specificity is what earns the next conversation.
