# Walk-In Research — November 3, 2026
## Area: Irving / Las Colinas / Coppell

**Why this area today:** Urban corporate corridor with high business density. Las Colinas has a concentration of independent professional services, med spas, and wellness businesses that are mid-size enough to have real revenue problems but small enough that AI automation is a new concept. Coppell skews toward affluent family-service businesses — tutoring, fitness, personal services. Both markets overlap with the North Texas SMB profile. Good mix of walk-in targets.

### 1. Vivid Med Spa — Las Colinas
**Address:** 5465 W Airport Fwy, Irving, TX 75062 (near Las Colinas Blvd)
**What they do:** Medical aesthetics — Botox, fillers, laser, skin treatments. Independent practice, not a chain.
**What I noticed:** No online booking on their website — booking requires a phone call during business hours. Google reviews show several 1-star complaints about not being able to reach anyone by phone. Review responses are slow or absent (several go unanswered for 3+ weeks).
**Best hook:** Lapsed client reactivation + online booking automation. "How many clients got their Botox in March and haven't been back since? That's your Q4 revenue sitting in your CRM." Med spa reactivation framing — seasonal hook for holiday look prep (parties, photos, New Year's).
