**Topic:** The first two weeks of November are the most valuable outreach window of the year for appointment-based businesses
**Target length:** ~175 words
**Post time:** 8–9am CT

---

Tomorrow is November 1.

The businesses that finish December strong don't get there by running ads in December. They get there by having one specific conversation with their existing clients right now, in the first two weeks of November.

Here's what that conversation looks like: pull your client list, find everyone who hasn't been in since July or earlier, and send them one message before November 14th. Not a promotion. Not a blast. One message that says: "We have three openings between now and Thanksgiving and we're holding them for people who haven't been in a while."

A dental office that does this fills eight to ten hygiene appointments it would have lost to "I'll call after the holidays." A salon fills a week. A chiropractor fills the slow spots before the December office-party season hits.

The window is the next two weeks. After November 15, you're reacting to cancellations instead of building a calendar.

November 1 is tomorrow. The window is open now.
