## One Insight

Today's generator post makes a point worth using in every walk-in today: the homeowner intent window is short. For generators it's days — a cold front moves through, the homeowner goes online, they book the first company that responds. The same pattern holds for HVAC tune-ups, dental recalls, roof estimates, chiropractic appointments. The business doesn't lose because of price or quality. It loses because it was asleep when the customer was ready. That framing — "your customer had 20 minutes of intent and nobody was there" — is concrete and uncomfortable in a productive way. It doesn't require the owner to believe in AI. It just requires them to recognize a gap they've probably already felt.
