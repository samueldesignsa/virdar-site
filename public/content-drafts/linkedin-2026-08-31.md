**Topic:** September starts tomorrow. Most businesses will wait for it to come to them.

September starts tomorrow.

For most service businesses in North Texas, that means waiting to see what comes in. Waiting for the phone to ring. Waiting for the slow weeks to pass. Waiting for the fall rush to rescue them.

The businesses that are already full in September did something different. In August, they looked at their customer list and asked: who haven't we heard from in 90 days? Then they sent those people a specific message. Not a newsletter. Not a promotion. A message with their name, a reference to the last service, and a reason to come back right now.

Most of them didn't set this up themselves. They had a system that did it while their team was focused on the work.

The difference between a busy September and a slow one isn't the economy or the competition. It's what you did in August with the list you've already built.

That list is not a marketing asset. It's your business. The question is whether it's working while you're working.
