## One Insight

The mid-cities corridor (Hurst/Euless/Bedford) is underworked relative to its business density. It's between two more glamorous markets — Southlake/Colleyville to the north and Arlington/Mansfield to the south — and doesn't have the name recognition of either. But it has a dense concentration of owner-operated medical, dental, and service businesses that have been in place for a long time and are not being targeted by automation vendors who focus on newer, flashier markets.

The airport adjacency also means there are fleet accounts and corporate clients in this area — auto detailing shops, dry cleaners, and printing/promotional companies that serve DFW's business traveler infrastructure. Those are different buyers than a solo chiropractor, but they have the same communication problem: a database of past customers they don't systematically reach.
