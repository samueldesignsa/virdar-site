# Walk-In Research — Friday, August 14, 2026
## Territory: Richardson / Garland (East Dallas County)

**Why this territory today:** Richardson and Garland are two of the largest cities in Dallas County and have been conspicuously absent from this walk-in cycle. Together they represent a population of roughly 280,000 — larger than many of the suburban combinations Beverly has hit this cycle. More importantly, they have distinct independent business ecosystems that fit the Virdar model well:

- **Richardson** is a high-income, high-education suburb (pop. ~120,000) with an unusually strong independent healthcare corridor along Campbell Road and Central Expressway. The UTD student population drives demand for medical, optometry, mental health, and sports medicine practices that have accumulated large patient panels. Many of these practices serve families who have been in the same house for 15+ years and have high insurance coverage and high LTV.
- **Garland** (pop. ~240,000) is one of the most underestimated independent business markets in DFW. The commercial corridors along Northwest Highway, Garland Road, and Belt Line Road have dense independent dental, chiropractic, veterinary, and service businesses — most of which have been operating for 10–20 years with manual systems. The competitive pressure here comes from national chains moving into the corridor, not from other independents with sophisticated automation.

**Primary angle for today:** End of week + end of summer. School has started, the busy season is setting up, and independent business owners who have been running at full pace all summer are now in the planning window for fall. Beverly's angle: "What does your fall look like — and what are you doing differently this year to prepare for it versus last year?"

**Secondary angle — Q4 insurance benefits.** August 14 means patients and clients have roughly 4.5 months of remaining benefits in 2026. Practices that haven't run their insurance benefit reminder campaigns yet are 6 weeks behind the ones that have. This is a conversation starter at every healthcare and professional services stop today.
