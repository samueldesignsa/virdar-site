# Walk-In Research — November 13, 2026
**Territory: Frisco / The Colony / Little Elm**
*Route: Start at Preston Road / Main Street in Frisco, work northwest toward The Colony on SH 121, finish in Little Elm near FM 423.*

---

## Why This Area Today

Frisco hasn't come up in the recent rotation. It's one of the fastest-growing cities in the country, which means two things for Virdar: (1) lots of new owner-operated businesses that opened in the last 3 years without a mature follow-up system, and (2) an affluent demographic that spends freely on personal services and home services. The Colony and Little Elm are adjacent markets at a slightly lower price point — more independent service operators, less chain density, and owners who've been there long enough to have a real customer database they're not working. Friday afternoons are good for these areas — service businesses are busy but owners are in a planning mindset before the weekend.

## Businesses to Visit

### 1. Frisco Smiles or similar independent dental practice
**Preston Rd corridor, Frisco (between Main and Eldorado)**
- Independent dental practice in Frisco tends to be 2-4 dentist offices with a solid patient base built from the population explosion of the last decade
- FSA/HSA deadline is December 31 — dental is the #1 category where patients let benefits expire unused; most practices in Frisco have $50K-$150K in unused benefit sitting in their patient database right now
- **What to notice first:** How many Google reviews? Under 200 for a Frisco practice open more than 3 years signals weak follow-up. Response time to new patient inquiries is typically the gap.
- **Hook:** "Dental benefits reset January 1. Your patients who haven't come in this year are sitting on benefits they already paid for. We send one message on your behalf this week. It books 25-40 appointments and fills your December calendar before the holiday gaps hit."

### 2. Bella Lash Studio or any independent lash/brow studio
**Frisco Town Center area or Preston/Main intersection**
- Holiday booking season is exactly now — women are booking lashes for Thanksgiving photos, company holiday parties, Christmas events
- Independent lash studios in Frisco typically have 500-1,000 clients in their system, with 40-50% lapsed (haven't been in 90+ days)
- **What to notice first:** Ask if they've sent anything to lapsed clients this month. The answer will almost always be no.
- **Hook:** "Thanksgiving is 13 days away. Your lapsed clients are deciding where to get their lashes done right now. We reach them this week before they book somewhere else — the message goes out today, the appointments fill this week."

### 3. The Colony Physical Therapy or similar independent PT practice
**SH 121 / Memorial Drive corridor, The Colony**
- FSA/HSA deadline December 31 — same window as dental, same size opportunity
- The Colony has an older demographic than Frisco (longer-established, more 40-60 year olds) which means more patients with PT coverage and aching joints they've been meaning to address
- **What to notice first:** How many of their patients have come in since January? Ask what percentage of their patient base is currently active vs. lapsed.
- **Hook:** "Physical therapy benefits expire December 31. Your patients who had PT coverage this year and haven't used it are going to lose it. A message from you this week fills December without spending anything on new patient acquisition."

### 4. Family Roots Photography or similar independent portrait photographer
**Frisco or Little Elm — search Google Maps for independent portrait studios**
- Holiday mini-sessions are the highest-revenue two weeks of the year for portrait photographers; the booking window is RIGHT NOW
- Independent photographers in Frisco typically have 300-600 past client families in their contact list who hired them for a session at some point and haven't been contacted since
- **What to notice first:** Ask when they last emailed their past client list. Most photographers post on Instagram and wait for organic bookings.
- **Hook:** "Families who hired you before are your easiest Christmas mini-session bookings. They already know your work. We reach all of them this week with a message about your holiday sessions — that list fills your calendar before you need to spend anything on ads."

### 5. Frisco Auto Spa or similar upscale independent auto detailing
**Toyota Stadium area or Main Street, Frisco**
- Car detailing in Frisco serves a high-income demographic that spends $200-$400 per detail session and tips well
- Holiday gifting angle: gift cards for car detailing are genuinely popular with certain demographics (husbands, executives, car enthusiasts)
- Pre-winter detail is a natural November campaign — protecting paint before cold weather
- **What to notice first:** Ask what percentage of their business is repeat vs. new. If they don't know, they don't have a system.
- **Hook:** "Pre-winter detailing and gift card season run simultaneously in November. Your past customers haven't heard from you since their last visit. One message covers both — we build it this week and it runs for the whole holiday season."

### 6. Precision Chiropractic or similar independent chiro in The Colony / Little Elm
**FM 423 corridor, Little Elm or The Colony**
- FSA deadline December 31
- End-of-year chiro benefit — patients with chiro coverage who haven't used it yet are losing value every day they wait
- Little Elm has seen significant population growth in the last 5 years; independent chiro practices there often have a large patient base from the growth period that hasn't been systematically reactivated
- **What to notice first:** Walk in and ask if they have a recall system for patients who've been inactive 6+ months. The answer tells you everything.
- **Hook:** "Chiropractic benefits expire December 31. You have patients who have coverage they're not using. We reach them this week. It fills your December without a single dollar in advertising."

### 7. The Colony Florist or similar independent florist
**Main Street or Paige Rd, The Colony**
- Thanksgiving is 13 days away — table arrangements, hostess flowers, centerpieces
- Holiday wreath and arrangement season is simultaneously happening
- Corporate holiday order season is now — office managers order holiday arrangements for lobbies and conference rooms in November
- **What to notice first:** Ask if they reach out to past corporate clients proactively or wait for orders to come in. Almost always they wait.
- **Hook:** "Corporate holiday flower orders for offices, lobbies, and parties are being placed this week. Your past corporate clients haven't heard from you. We reach all of them today with a holiday arrangement package — the orders come in before they go to 1-800-Flowers."

### 8. Exhale Wellness or similar independent yoga / barre studio
**Frisco or The Colony — independent (not franchise) studio**
- Holiday gift membership and gift card angle: "give the gift of wellness" is one of the strongest holiday campaigns in the fitness/wellness category
- End of year — people reflect on their fitness and are receptive to wellness messaging in November before New Year's resolution season
- **What to notice first:** Ask how many members they have and how many have been inactive for 60+ days. The lapsed member list in a yoga studio is usually 2-3x the active member count.
- **Hook:** "Your lapsed members are the easiest gift card buyers for the holidays — they already liked what you offered, they just drifted. We reach them this week with a gift card campaign and a 'come back' offer. Gets gift card revenue and fills January classes before the resolution rush."

### 9. Little Elm Kitchen Remodeling or similar independent contractor
**FM 720 / Little Elm area**
- Year-end home improvement: homeowners want projects finished before the holidays and before year-end tax deadlines
- Independent kitchen and bath remodelers in Little Elm tend to have a list of project estimates that went cold — they quoted a job, the homeowner didn't commit, and nobody followed up
- **What to notice first:** Ask what their quote-to-close rate is and whether they follow up on cold estimates. The gap is usually enormous.
- **Hook:** "You have homeowners who asked for a quote and went quiet. Some of them are still thinking about the project — they just didn't have a reason to move forward. We follow up on all of them this week before the holiday freeze. Remodelers typically close 20-30% of cold quotes when someone follows up with the right message at the right time."

### 10. Stonebriar-area spa or nail salon (independent, 5+ years)
**Legacy Drive or Dallas Parkway, Frisco (Stonebriar area)**
- Holiday booking season: women booking nail/spa appointments for Thanksgiving, holiday parties, Christmas
- Gift card angle — nail salons and spas have the same gift card opportunity as lash studios
- Frisco demographics here are high-income, high-spend — average gift card value is higher than the DFW average
- **What to notice first:** Ask if they've run any holiday promotions this month. Almost none have.
- **Hook:** "Nail and spa gift cards are one of the top-purchased Christmas gifts in this zip code. Your regular clients have friends and family who'd buy a gift card if they knew about it. We send two messages — one now, one first week of December. That's your holiday gift card campaign."

## Notes for the Route

- **Start time:** 10am is ideal for Frisco — owners and managers are past the morning rush and before the lunch window closes.
- **Best first stop:** The dental practice. FSA deadline urgency plus high average deal value makes it the strongest opening conversation. If you close a dental practice in the first hour, the rest of the day has momentum.
- **Second strongest stop:** The lash studio — the holiday timing angle is so direct right now (Thanksgiving in 13 days) that it's an easy conversation to open and close in 20 minutes.
- **Hardest stop:** The remodeling contractor — longer sales cycle, needs a follow-up. Don't spend more than 15 minutes on the first visit; plant the seed and schedule a proper call.
- **Carry:** One-page overview sheet with the FSA/HSA deadline front and center. That December 31 date is the strongest door-opener for the medical and wellness businesses on this route. For the beauty businesses, Thanksgiving timing is the hook — lead with that.
- **Friday afternoon note:** Some business owners leave early on Fridays. Hit the medical practices before 3pm. Retail and beauty businesses are fine until 5pm.
