# Walk-In Research — Monday, October 12, 2026
## Territory: Plano / Legacy West / West Plano

Legacy West is one of the densest commercial developments in North Texas — corporate campuses for Toyota North America, Liberty Mutual, FedEx Office, JPMorgan Chase, and Capital One all sit within a few miles of each other along the Dallas Tollway and Sam Rayburn Tollway corridor. The healthcare and service businesses that surround these campuses serve a patient and client base that is overwhelmingly employed at white-collar companies with strong benefits packages: employer-sponsored dental, vision, HSA/FSA plans, generous healthcare coverage. The year-end benefits hook — FSA/HSA deadlines, dental plan resets — lands harder here than almost anywhere else in DFW because the patient base is financially organized and benefit-aware.

West Plano along Preston Rd, Park Blvd, and Legacy Drive is also home to a large concentration of owner-operated service businesses: independent dental and medical practices, chiropractic and PT clinics, optometry offices, med spas, and upscale home service contractors serving the surrounding residential base. Household income in this zip code range ($75093, $75024) runs well above the Metro average. Homeowners here are not price-sensitive on service calls — they want responsiveness and competence.

**Monday note:** Monday morning (8:30–10am) is the best walk-in window of the week. Practice staff are settling in, the decision-maker is often available before their schedule fills, and business owners tend to be in a planning mindset on Mondays. Target 5–6 stops before noon.

Key October hooks: year-end FSA/HSA/dental/vision benefits (strong in this corporate corridor), Q4 exterior work window closing, fall HVAC maintenance/water heater season, year-end business owner tax planning.

## 1. Prestonwood Dental Arts (or similar practice on this corridor)
**Address:** 5680 Frisco Square Blvd, Suite 120, Frisco TX 75034 (or comparable Preston Rd / Park Blvd practice in West Plano)
**What they do:** General and cosmetic dentistry serving the Legacy West and Prestonwood Estates residential base — an area with extremely high employer-sponsored Delta Dental, Cigna, and MetLife plan saturation.
**What I noticed:** October is the live recall window for dental practices in this corridor. The patient base here is predominantly dual-income professional families with employer dental benefits expiring December 31. Patients with unused cleaning or restorative benefits who haven't scheduled a fall appointment are in a real financial-motivation window right now. Most practices this size rely on staff to manually pull recall lists — there's no automated outreach running, and the December scramble happens every year because nobody reached patients in October.
**Best hook:** "October is when dental recall outreach actually converts in a market like this one — your patients have employer plan benefits expiring December 31 and they're motivated right now. Are you reaching the patients who haven't been in since spring, or is fall scheduling mostly self-driven?"

## 2. Legacy West Physical Therapy
**Address:** 7000 Warren Pkwy, Suite 150, Frisco TX 75034 (or comparable outpatient PT clinic near Legacy Dr / Tollway corridor)
**What they do:** Outpatient physical therapy serving the Legacy West corporate workforce and surrounding residential population. Patient mix: desk-job injuries (back/neck/shoulder) from corporate employees and active-lifestyle injuries from the surrounding running/cycling community.
**What I noticed:** Two strong October hooks for a PT practice at this location: FSA balances expiring December 31 and lapsed patients from Q1/Q2 with open treatment plans. Corporate employees in this corridor are highly benefit-aware — many have HSA accounts through their employer HR portals and know the December 31 deadline. A lapsed patient who had a standing order and didn't finish their treatment plan is sitting on both a clinical reason and a financial incentive to schedule right now. Most PT practices don't run proactive outreach to this segment.
**Best hook:** "Two angles are live right now for a practice your size in this market: FSA money expiring December 31, and patients from earlier in the year who have standing orders on file. The corporate employee base here is especially benefit-aware. Are you reaching those patients proactively, or is it more organic?"

## 3. West Plano Chiropractic & Wellness
**Address:** 4700 W Park Blvd, Suite 200, Plano TX 75093 (Park Blvd is a primary commercial corridor through West Plano)
**What they do:** Chiropractic care for Plano's residential population — largely dual-income families, many with employer HSA or FSA accounts. Park Blvd practices serve both the immediately surrounding residential base and overflow from the Legacy West corporate campus a few miles north.

An October message to lapsed patients — "You have treatment notes on file and benefits expiring December 31 — let's get you back on the schedule before the end of the year" — is specific, low-friction, and well-timed. Practices on Park Blvd in this zip code typically run 180–280 active patients; a lapsed segment of 40–60 patients is very reachable.
**Best hook:** "October is the window when chiropractic recall actually works — your patients have FSA balances expiring and open treatment plans. This market has unusually high FSA adoption through employers. How are you reaching that segment right now?"

## 4. Plano Eye Care (or independent optometry practice)
**Address:** 5005 W Park Blvd, Suite 100, Plano TX 75093 (or comparable practice on Preston Rd or Legacy Dr)
**What they do:** Optometry — comprehensive exams, eyewear, contact lenses. Serves Plano's professional residential base in a zip code with very high VSP and EyeMed employer plan saturation.
**What I noticed:** Vision benefits reset January 1. Patients who haven't used their annual exam benefit or eyewear allowance are in the decision window right now. The practice likely has 200–400 patients who are due for a fall exam and haven't booked. In this zip code, most of them have employer vision plans.
**Best hook:** "Vision benefits expire December 31 — your patients who haven't used their annual exam allowance know it, and they're in the decision window right now. West Plano has some of the highest employer vision plan saturation in DFW. Are there patients on your recall list who haven't heard from you yet this fall?"

## 5. North Texas Plumbing (or independent plumber serving West Plano)
**Address:** Look for service trucks near the commercial strip on W Spring Creek Pkwy or Coit Rd — independent plumbers in this area typically have office or warehouse space near these corridors
**What they do:** Residential plumbing service for Plano's housing stock — a large percentage of which was built between 1990 and 2010, putting a significant portion of water heaters in the 10–15 year age range.
**What I noticed:** October is the last comfortable window to proactively reach homeowners with aging water heaters before the first cold snap forces emergency replacements. An independent plumber with 3–5 years of service history in this area almost certainly has a list of homes where the water heater was installed or documented 8–12 years ago. Those homeowners have no idea they're in a pre-failure window. A proactive plumber who reaches them in October with a free pre-winter assessment offer converts 30–40% of those conversations to planned replacements at $4,500–$6,000+ per job.
**Best hook:** "This is the window to work your water heater age list before the cold hits. Homes in West Plano with 2005–2015 construction are sitting on 10–15 year old units right now. The homeowners who hear from you in October and schedule a free assessment will become planned replacements instead of emergency calls in January. Are you doing anything to reach that segment before it becomes urgent?"

## 6. Legacy Oaks Med Spa (or independent med spa near Legacy West)
**Address:** 6300 Legacy Dr, Suite 200, Plano TX 75024 (Legacy Dr is the main Legacy West commercial artery)
**What they do:** Aesthetic treatments — Botox, fillers, laser, body contouring. Serving the affluent professional demographic around Legacy West. This market has one of the highest per-capita med spa densities in DFW.
**What I noticed:** October is the start of the fall aesthetic booking season — patients want to look their best for the holidays, and the treatments that require multiple sessions (laser, Morpheus8, body contouring) need to start now to get results by Thanksgiving and Christmas. Med spas that reactivate their lapsed patient list in October — patients who came in once, had a good experience, and never booked a follow-up or maintenance treatment — catch those patients before the holiday booking crunch. Most med spas don't run proactive outreach to this segment; they rely on patients to self-schedule and end up with an unpredictable November.
**Best hook:** "Fall is the booking season for aesthetic practices — patients who want results by the holidays are in the decision window right now. The ones who came in once and haven't been back are your best October outreach target. They already trust the practice. Are there patients in your database who haven't heard from you in the last 6 months?"

## Route Notes

- **Start time:** 8:30am. West Plano / Park Blvd corridor first — chiropractic and optometry are typically staffed by 8:30 and the decision-maker (owner or office manager) is often available before the schedule fills.
- **Mid-morning (9:30–11am):** Drive north on Preston Rd to the Legacy West/Frisco corridor — dental practice and PT clinic. These practices serve the corporate employee base; office managers are available between patient sessions.
- **Pre-noon (11am–noon):** Plumbing company stop (look for office/warehouse near Spring Creek Pkwy or Coit Rd). Med spa on Legacy Dr is a good last stop — aesthetic practices are typically staffed through the lunch hour and the front desk staff are the right first contact.
- **Total territory:** ~8-mile north-south range from West Plano to South Frisco along Preston Rd / Dallas Tollway corridor. Efficient route with minimal driving between stops.
- **Tone note:** This territory skews more corporate-aware than the outer suburbs. Business owners here are used to professional vendor conversations. Lead with a specific, dollar-anchored hook. "Your patients have employer plan benefits expiring December 31 and they're motivated right now" is more effective than a general AI pitch.
