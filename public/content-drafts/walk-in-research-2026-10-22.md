# Walk-In Research — Thursday, October 22, 2026
## Territory: Frisco / McKinney / Allen

Frisco, McKinney, and Allen sit at the northern edge of DFW's growth corridor — and together they form one of the densest concentrations of young, high-income families in the entire Sun Belt. Frisco's population has grown faster than nearly any city in the country over the past fifteen years, driven by corporate relocations (Toyota North America, Keurig Dr Pepper, Stonebriar Centre area employers), tech sector expansion, and the lifestyle draw of top-rated school districts. McKinney and Allen carry much of the same profile at a slightly more established pace.

Median household incomes: Frisco $140K+, McKinney $120K+, Allen $130K+. These are dual-income professional households — a lot of tech, finance, healthcare, and corporate management — with employer-sponsored benefits packages that include strong dental, vision, FSA, and HSA contributions. The demographic knows what an FSA is, has maxed their HSA, and will act on a specific year-end deadline.

Frisco has not been covered in recent walk-in research. McKinney and Allen have not been covered since early spring at the earliest. This territory should be a priority for October.

**The October hooks hit hard here:**
- **FSA/HSA**: This is a corporate-employed, benefits-maximizing demographic. A household with two engineers or a nurse and a finance professional may have $5,500 in combined HSA contributions sitting available. The $2,750 FSA max, $660 carryover, December 31 deadline is not an education task here — it's a trigger. These patients have been meaning to use their benefit and a specific message from their provider converts them.
- **Year-end deductible**: High-deductible health plans are the norm at the large employers in this market. A family in Frisco with a $4,000 family deductible who has had a child's sports injury or orthodontic start in Q1 may be sitting on $2,500–$3,000 of paid deductible with six weeks left to use it at reduced cost.
- **Holiday prep**: Frisco and Allen have a dense social calendar through December — corporate events (Toyota, DR Pepper, Keurig all host large holiday events), school events at Frisco ISD and Allen ISD, and the social entertaining circuit of the upper-middle-income suburb. Aesthetics, hair, and dental cosmetic work are being booked right now for November.

**Thursday note:** Frisco/McKinney/Allen medical practices are fully staffed Thursday mid-week. Owner-operators and practice managers are accessible before the noon patient block — arrive between 9am and 10:30am for medical and dental, 10am–noon for service businesses. The Legacy Drive / Preston Road / Stacy Road corridors are the primary commercial spines; parking is abundant at every stop.

The practice that sends a specific message to open-estimate patients in October — not a generic "benefits expiring" blast in November — fills November from their own list before competing for last-minute December slots.
