# Walk-In Research — Monday, August 4, 2026
## Territory: Euless / Bedford / Hurst (HEB Corridor)

**Why this corridor today:** Fresh territory not covered in recent rotation. The HEB corridor — Euless, Bedford, Hurst — sits between DFW Airport and Fort Worth along Airport Freeway (183) and Bedford Road, and has one of the highest concentrations of independent service businesses in the mid-cities. Dense with independent barbershops (this market skews male, working-class, and walk-in heavy), auto repair, dental practices, chiro, fitness studios, and personal service businesses. Today's blog (barbershops/men's grooming) is a direct leave-behind for every barbershop stop. Back-to-school timing is live — HEB ISD, Hurst-Euless-Bedford ISD, and Birdville ISD all start between August 13 and 19.

**Routing:** Start at Airport Freeway / Brown Trail corridor (Bedford) → Mid-Cities Boulevard stops (Hurst) → Euless Main / Glade Road (afternoon).

## 9 Target Businesses

### 1. Independent Barbershop — Brown Trail / Airport Freeway, Bedford
**What they do:** Walk-in men's barbershop. Mix of regulars and first-timers from Airport Freeway traffic. Cash-heavy, no booking system, no customer database.
**What to look for:** No contact list. If you ask "what do you do when a regular stops coming in?" the answer is "we don't do anything, we just hope they come back." Back-to-school week is 15 days away and they have no way to send a text to regulars.
**Hook:** "School starts in two weeks. If you had a list of your regulars, you could fill Monday and Tuesday of that week with one text message. Do you have that list?"

### 2. Men's Grooming Studio or Upscale Barbershop — Mid-Cities Boulevard, Hurst
**What they do:** Slightly higher-ticket men's grooming concept (beard trims, hot towel shaves, premium cuts in the $40–60 range). More likely to have some booking, less likely to have automated follow-up.
**What to look for:** Booking system that doesn't send reminders or lapsed-client outreach. Review count below 150 on Google — this type of shop earns reviews but rarely asks for them at the right moment.
**Hook:** "You probably have 30-40 customers who haven't been back in eight weeks. Some of them are gone. Some are just busy. A six-week follow-up text brings back the ones who are just busy — at essentially zero cost."

### 3. Independent Dental Practice (General or Pediatric) — Precinct Line Road / Euless area
**What they do:** Family or general dental practice serving HEB corridor families. HEB ISD starts August 13 — one of the earliest start dates in DFW. That's nine days from today.

### 5. Fitness Studio or Gym — Mid-Cities Boulevard / Industrial Boulevard, Euless
**What they do:** Independent fitness studio — boutique HIIT, CrossFit, Orangetheory-type — in the mid-cities. Membership-based with high summer lapse rate as members travel and schedules break down.
**What to look for:** Members who were attending 3x/week in spring and have dropped to 1x/week or gone inactive. No system that flags reduced attendance before the membership cancels. Back-to-school is the pivot point — members returning from summer routines need a reason to re-commit.

### 8. Independent Tutoring or Learning Center — Bedford Road or Precinct Line, Hurst/Bedford
