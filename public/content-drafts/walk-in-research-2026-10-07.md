# Walk-In Research — Wednesday, October 7, 2026
## Territory: Midlothian / Waxahachie / Ennis

Midlothian, Waxahachie, and Ennis are Ellis County's primary commercial corridors — growing fast, underserved by vendors making the drive from Dallas or Fort Worth, and full of owner-operated businesses that have grown with the residential base and are now behind on their systems. Waxahachie has a strong medical and dental concentration on US-287 and Ferris Ave. Midlothian's FM 663 / US-67 corridor has become a real business district as residential growth spilled south from Mansfield and Cedar Hill. Ennis is older and more industrial, but the service businesses serving its residential population are exactly the kind of under-automated practices that respond well to a direct conversation.

The key difference from markets like Southlake or Frisco: owners here have often not been pitched by AI or automation vendors at all. The landscape isn't saturated. A direct, specific conversation about a business problem they actually have — without the technology framing leading — converts differently here. Come in through the business problem. The AI part is the solution, not the pitch.

Route: Start in Midlothian (FM 663 / 9th Street corridor near US-67), drive south to Waxahachie (US-287 / Ferris Ave / Main St corridor), finish in Ennis (Ennis Ave / US-287 east). About 35 miles, all surface roads. Allow a full day — the spacing between corridors is wider than in North Dallas markets, but there's no traffic.

## 1. Midlothian Family Dental
**Address:** 410 N 9th St, Midlothian, TX 76065
**What they do:** General and family dentistry — cleanings, restorations, cosmetic work, and routine care. Serves Midlothian and southern Mansfield residential areas.
**What I noticed:** October is the live window for year-end dental benefits recall. Patients with Delta Dental and Cigna plans that reset January 1 are in the decision window right now — they've been putting off a cleaning or a filling since spring and are now motivated by the December 31 deadline. A Midlothian practice has a middle-income residential patient base where insurance utilization is high and the "use it before you lose it" message lands fast. Most practices this size don't send proactive recall at all — they wait for patients to schedule.
**Best hook:** "October is the best recall window dental practices see all year — your patients have benefits expiring December 31 that they're actually motivated to use right now. Are you doing anything to reach the patients who haven't been in since spring, or is it mostly waiting for them to call?"

## 2. Midlothian Physical Therapy & Rehab
**Address:** 310 E US Hwy 287, Midlothian, TX 76065
**What they do:** Outpatient physical therapy — post-surgical rehab, sports injuries, orthopedic conditions. Serves Midlothian, Cedar Hill, and Mansfield patients avoiding the drive to a Dallas-area clinic.
**What I noticed:** FSA and HSA balances expire December 31. PT patients who've been putting off continuing care, or who have a standing order they haven't acted on, are now in the window to use pre-tax money before they lose it. Physical therapy is one of the highest-utilized FSA categories. A practice that proactively reaches lapsed patients in October — "you have a standing order and benefits expiring, let's get a few sessions scheduled before December" — converts those contacts at a high rate because the patient already knows they need the care.
**Best hook:** "FSA and HSA balances expire December 31. PT patients who have a standing order and haven't finished their sessions are sitting on pre-tax money they're about to lose — they know it and they're motivated. Are you reaching lapsed patients right now, or is it more organic?"

## 3. Waxahachie Heating & Cooling
**Address:** 2501 US-287, Waxahachie, TX 75165
**What they do:** Residential HVAC — repairs, tune-ups, system replacements, and maintenance agreements. Serves Ellis County residential market.
**What I noticed:** Waxahachie's residential base is heavily 1990s–2010s era construction — homes now in the 15-to-30-year age window for HVAC systems. October is peak inquiry time: homeowners who turn the heat on for the first time this season and notice something wrong submit contact requests in the evening. A company that responds to those after-hours contacts that night gets the job. A company that calls back the next morning is often calling a customer who already scheduled someone else. In Ellis County, HVAC replacements run $8,000–$14,000 — the response window is where the money is.
**Best hook:** "October is when replacement inquiries spike in Waxahachie — homeowners turning on heat for the first time and noticing something's off. If someone submits your web form at 8pm on a cold night, does anything go back to them that night, or does that wait until morning? In this market, whoever responds first gets the call."

## 7. Ennis Family Medicine / Correctcare Clinic
**Address:** 404 Ennis Ave, Ennis, TX 75119
**What they do:** Primary care and family medicine. Serves Ennis, Palmer, and rural Ellis County patients.
**What I noticed:** Primary care practices have a specific fall opportunity: annual wellness visits for patients with Medicare Advantage and commercial insurance. Many patients have a covered annual exam they haven't scheduled, and the Q4 window (October–December) is when practices that proactively reach those patients fill their schedule without competing for urgent care. Ennis has an older demographic compared to Midlothian — a higher proportion of Medicare patients, which means the annual wellness visit benefit is directly monetizable through proactive outreach. A practice this size that runs one automated campaign to their unscheduled wellness-visit panel in October will fill two to three additional appointment slots per week for the next 60 days.
**Best hook:** "Your Medicare patients have a covered annual wellness visit that a significant share of them haven't scheduled yet. October is when practices that reach those patients proactively fill their Q4 schedule — the visit is already paid for, they just need to know they should book it. Are you doing any outreach to that panel, or is it mostly organic?"

## Route Notes

- **Start time:** 8:30am in Midlothian (FM 663 / 9th St — take US-67 south from Cedar Hill, exit at FM 663).
- **Morning cluster (Midlothian):** Midlothian Family Dental → Midlothian Physical Therapy. Both in the US-287 / 9th St commercial corridor — 5–10 minutes apart.
- **Mid-morning to noon (Waxahachie):** Head southeast on US-287. Waxahachie Heating & Cooling (US-287 corridor) → Ellis County Eye Care (Ferris Ave) → Waxahachie Chiropractic (W Main St). The Ferris Ave and Main St corridors are close — downtown Waxahachie is walkable.
- **Afternoon (Ennis):** Continue southeast on US-287. First Choice Roofing (W Marvin Ave, Waxahachie) is on the way out of Waxahachie. Ennis Family Medicine is 20 minutes further southeast.
- **Total drive time:** ~50 minutes driving, ~5 hours of conversations.
- **Tone note:** Ellis County is not Colleyville or Southlake. Skip the opening about "high-growth markets" and "scaling." These owners respond to specific business problems, direct language, and a clear price-to-value explanation. The FSA deadline and year-end insurance hooks are strong here because this is a middle-income market where patients feel the deadline urgency more acutely.
