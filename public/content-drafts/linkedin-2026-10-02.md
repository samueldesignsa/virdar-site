# LinkedIn Post — Friday, October 2, 2026

**Topic:** Estimate backlog — the October follow-up window

---

Roofing contractors gave 40, 50, maybe 80 estimates between June and August.

Most of those homeowners are still thinking about it.

October is when they act. School is back in a rhythm. The heat broke. The holidays are close enough that people start thinking about what needs to get done before year-end.

The companies that reach out to those unsold estimates right now — a simple "checking in on your project" message — will close jobs this month that their competitors are still waiting on.

The ones that don't will watch those homeowners book whoever followed up.

Same thing applies to remodeling contractors, generator companies, kitchen and bath shops — anyone who gave quotes over the summer and never heard back.

The window is short. Most of this demand firms up before November.

Your quote inbox from summer isn't dead. It's waiting for a nudge.

What's your process for following up on unsold estimates?
