Thanksgiving is tomorrow.

If your calendar has gaps through the holiday season, that decision point was two or three weeks ago — when you either sent something to past customers or you didn't.

The owners with full calendars didn't do anything exotic. They reached out to people who already knew them. They said: "The holidays are coming. Here's how to book." That's the whole thing.

There's good news: the same window is open right now for January.

December is when January fills. The customers who haven't heard from you in four to eight months are going into the new year thinking about whether to restart whatever habit they had with you. A message from you in December — specific, personal, with a clear path to book — hits them at the exact moment they're making that decision.

January is not too far away to start. It's exactly far enough.

What's your outreach to past customers looked like in the last 30 days?
