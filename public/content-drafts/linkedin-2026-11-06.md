Your optometry patients have two separate pots of money earmarked for their eye care right now.

The first is their FSA balance. That expires December 31. Use it or lose it.

The second is their vision insurance benefit — the annual allowance for glasses, contacts, and exams that resets January 1 whether they used it or not. The patients who don't schedule in November and December lose both.

They're not choosing to skip their appointments. They're just not thinking about it. Their calendar is filling with Thanksgiving and Christmas. Their optometrist's office hasn't reminded them that two separate deadlines are running simultaneously.

The practices that are booked solid in November and December sent one message in October: "Your FSA balance and your vision benefit both expire by year-end. If you haven't scheduled your exam, now is the time — we have appointments available through December 20."

One message. Two urgency triggers. November calendar full.

If your practice doesn't have that message scheduled, this week is the window to send it.
