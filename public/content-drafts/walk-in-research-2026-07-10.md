## Why Plano Today

Plano hasn't appeared in walk-in research despite being one of the largest cities in North Texas (285,000+ residents, Collin County). It's distinctly different from recent territories: higher household income than most DFW suburbs ($90K+ median), a professional services density along Preston Road and Legacy that rivals Frisco, and a huge base of independent practices that coexist with the corporate corridor. Plano ISD (PISD) starts August 17 — just 38 days out. That anchor date drives every back-to-school pitch in this territory. Independence Pkwy and W 15th St have a heavy independent business cluster. Legacy/US-75 has professional services. Spring Creek corridor has family-focused businesses that are ideal for the childcare/after-school angle just published this morning.

Route: Take US-75 north to Plano. Start Spring Creek Pkwy, work east to Coit/Independence, finish Preston Road corridor. 30–40 minutes from Dallas.

## Plano-Specific Notes

Plano business owners skew corporate-adjacent. Many of them have either worked in or adjacent to technology companies and are not unfamiliar with software or automation as a concept. That's a double-edged sword: they're easier to have a technical conversation with, but they've also heard pitches. The approach that works in Plano is not "have you heard of AI?" It's "here's exactly what's happening in your patient list / enrollment list / customer list right now, and here's the specific number it's costing you." Revenue math first. Product second. Story last.

PISD August 17 is the sharpest anchor date in today's territory. Use it specifically: "38 days from today" is a different conversation than "back to school is coming."
