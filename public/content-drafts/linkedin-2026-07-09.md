Fall sports registration is open right now across North Texas.

Most leagues will lose 25–35% of their returning families before the season starts — not to a competing program, but to inertia. The family means to sign up again. They just don't get around to it until the window closes or Saturday fills up with something else.

The fix isn't complicated: reach returning families in the last week of the current season, while they're still in the parking lot mentally. A message that goes out the day after the final game — "Fall registration opens for returning families this week before the public" — converts at 40% higher than the same message sent in February.

The leagues I work with send that message automatically, without a coordinator having to remember to build the list and send the email. The returning family gets the ask at the highest-motivation moment of the year. The league fills its rosters before families commit to other fall activities.

Fall registration decisions get made in July. The leagues that know that are the ones with full rosters by August 1.
