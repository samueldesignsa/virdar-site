## One Observation

The Addison / Far North Dallas corridor is a higher-credential walk-in environment than Beverly's recent east DFW territory. The practice owners here have been pitched. They're skeptical of new systems and protective of their time. The conversation that works is not "here's what AI can do" — it's "here's the specific number this is costing you." The pediatric PT owner who has never heard of IEP referral positioning doesn't need a demonstration of automation. They need the question: "Of the school districts within 5 miles of your practice, how many special education coordinators know your name?" The answer is almost always zero. The question lands because it's true and because the owner immediately understands what zero means for September.

Use specificity as the trust signal, not the pitch. Richardson business owners are analytical. Addison business owners are skeptical. Specific financial math passed the bar in Richardson. Specific pointed questions pass it here.
