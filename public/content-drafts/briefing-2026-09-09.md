## One Insight

**Window cleaning is a better walk-in target than it looks.**

Most window cleaning owners are in vans during business hours, not in offices — so the direct walk-in is hard. But several things work in Keller and NRH today:

1. Some companies run a small office or storage facility with a posted address. Google "window cleaning [city]" before heading out — if the address is commercial (not residential), it's worth a stop.
2. If you see a window cleaning van in a parking lot during a job, that's the owner or a crew lead. A 2-minute conversation in a parking lot is a legitimate cold outreach.
3. The blog post published today gives you a content-as-outreach angle for any window cleaning company you can't reach in person. "I just wrote a breakdown of how window cleaning companies are losing customers between spring and fall seasons — if you want me to send it over, here's the link." That's a clean reason to send a text or DM to any window cleaning business you find on Google.

Content-as-outreach works best in categories where the owner is hard to pin down in person. Window cleaning, landscaping, pressure washing — these are all better reached by phone or message than a walk-in.
