## Field Notes

- Waxahachie has a compact downtown with foot traffic, especially on weekday mornings. Parking is free and easy on Jefferson St and the surrounding blocks.
- Midlothian is more spread out along the US-287 corridor — expect more strip mall settings, less foot traffic, and a different energy. Owners will be in their businesses but the atmosphere is more transactional than relational. Lead with the problem, not the pitch.
- Ellis County is growing fast (new residential development along the Midlothian/Waxahachie corridor) but the existing small business community is still small-town. First name, firm handshake, and "I'm local too" will go further than a polished deck.
- Best stops first: Waxahachie Family Dentistry (review gap is visible and undeniable), Midlothian Eye Care (FSA/HSA hook is timely), Midlothian Heating & Air (October tune-up window is closing).
