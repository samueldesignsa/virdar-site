## Context Notes

Cedar Hill, Duncanville, and DeSoto are majority-minority communities with a high proportion of long-established, independently owned businesses. The owners here are not early adopters. They've heard pitches before. The frame that lands is **the cost frame**: specific revenue they're leaving on the table, specific dollar amount, specific scenario. "Here's what you lost last month" lands better than "here's a system you could build." Don't lead with AI. Lead with money.

The back-to-school angle is sharper here than almost anywhere else in DFW — Cedar Hill ISD, CFBISD, and DeSoto ISD all have student populations with high athletic participation and significant pediatric healthcare demand. These schools start August 11. The window to reach families before they need appointments is this week and next.
