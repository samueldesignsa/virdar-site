## One Insight

The deductible-reset pitch is the single most underutilized argument in every health services conversation between now and December 31. Every dental, chiropractic, PT, and optometry practice in DFW has patients who paid into their deductible this year and then stopped coming in. Most practice owners know this abstractly — they know patients drop off, they know the calendar resets, they know the last quarter is when benefit-aware patients return. What they don't realize is how many patients in their database are in this exact position right now, in October, and how specific the message can be. "You paid $800 toward your deductible and haven't been in since April — come back before January and it costs you $0" is not a general wellness reminder. It's a financial argument with a specific number and a specific deadline. The practices that send it convert lapsed patients at 2–3x the rate of general recall messages. The ones that send it in October (rather than November or December when the calendar is already compressed) have the best chance of actually booking and completing the appointments before the reset. This is the argument to put in front of every health services business Beverly walks into today.
