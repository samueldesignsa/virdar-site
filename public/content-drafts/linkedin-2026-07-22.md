Some of your counseling clients held it together all summer.

They managed the kids being home. The change in routine. The heat. The travel that wasn't relaxing. The slow erosion of every schedule that normally keeps them functional.

And then school starts August 18 and the house is quiet for the first time since May and they realize they're not okay.

September is the highest-volume new client inquiry month for most counseling practices. It's also when lapsed clients — the ones who attended January through May and took a "summer break" — come back to the same need they had in spring.

The practices that fill September started their reactivation campaign in August. A message to lapsed clients who stopped in June: "School is starting, a lot of people find this a natural time to reconnect." Sent August 10. Takes 20 minutes to set up.

The practices that don't send it get the same calls in September — they just don't get to prepare for them.
