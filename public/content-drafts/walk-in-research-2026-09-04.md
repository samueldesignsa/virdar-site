# Walk-In Research — Friday, September 4, 2026
## Territory: Hurst / Euless / Bedford (HEB Corridor), TX

**Why this territory today:**
Hurst, Euless, and Bedford sit at the geographic center of the DFW metro — wedged between Fort Worth and Dallas, directly adjacent to DFW International Airport, and served by the Trinity Railway Express. The HEB corridor is working-class to upper-middle-class: teachers, municipal workers, airline employees, healthcare workers, and a dense small-business population that serves those households. Pipeline Road, Bedford Road, Airport Freeway (183), and Precinct Line Road are the main commercial corridors — all with strong concentrations of independent service businesses.

This territory hasn't been in the recent walk-in cycle. Businesses here serve customers who value consistency and personal relationships. The owners are typically working the business themselves — not absentee operators. They know every customer's name. The pitch lands differently here than in a corporate corridor: lead with "you already have the relationships, you're just not staying in front of them."

**Drive time from central DFW:** 20–25 minutes via TX-121 or I-820.

**Best timing:** Start in Bedford (Airport Freeway / L. Don Dodson Dr area) at 9:30–10am. Move to Hurst (Precinct Line Road / Pipeline Road) at 10:30–11:30am. Euless (W. Euless Blvd / Airport Freeway) works 11am–1pm.

**Market framing for this corridor:** HEB business owners are pragmatic. They've heard about AI and assume it's not for them — too expensive, too complicated, built for bigger companies. The reframe: "This isn't software you learn. It's a system that reaches your customers so you don't have to think about it." September urgency: "Q4 is the most important quarter for most of the businesses on this street. The ones with a full October are reaching their past customers this week."

## 10 Targets

### 1. Bedford or Hurst Dental Practice (any independent general or family dentist)
**What they do:** General and family dentistry serving the dense residential population of the HEB corridor. Mid-income patient base with a mix of insurance and self-pay. High value of an active patient relationship — families here stay in the community and refer neighbors.

**Specific observation:** The six-month recall window is open right now for every patient who came in during February and March. Independent dental practices in HEB compete with DSO chains (Aspen Dental is on Airport Freeway) that run automated recall every day. The independent that doesn't have a September outreach sequence is giving patients a reason to book wherever texts them first. December insurance benefits expire in 16 weeks — the practices with full October hygiene schedules sent that message this week.

**Hook:** "How many patients who came in January through March haven't been back yet? Those are your September recalls. The practices with a full October sent that message this week — has yours gone out?"

### 3. Euless or Bedford Auto Repair Shop (any independent)
**What they do:** General automotive repair — oil changes, brakes, tires, engine work. HEB's proximity to DFW Airport means a lot of two-car households and commuters who depend on vehicle reliability. Independent shops here build strong word-of-mouth with airline employees and shift workers who need fast, trustworthy service.

**Specific observation:** Independent auto repair shops with a solid customer base lose repeat visits to the simple problem of invisibility. Customers come in for an oil change, leave satisfied, and don't come back for 9 months because nobody reminded them. The chains (Firestone, Jiffy Lube) send automated service reminders to every customer in their system. The independent shop with 200 past customers has the relationships — but no system for staying in front of them between visits.

**Hook:** "How many customers have been in the last two years who haven't come back for maintenance? That's the list you're sitting on. The shops that stay full between word-of-mouth referrals send service reminders at the right intervals."

### 4. Hurst Chiropractic or Physical Therapy Practice (any independent)
**What they do:** Musculoskeletal care — acute injury, wellness maintenance, sports recovery, post-surgical PT. The HEB corridor has active adults, weekend athletes, and an above-average rate of work-related injuries from airport jobs, construction, and service industries.

**Specific observation:** September is one of the two strongest reactivation months in chiro and PT. An independent practice with 80–100 past patients has 15–20 people right now who are at the exact moment where a personal check-in converts to a booked appointment. Most practices don't send that message and wait for patients to self-refer — which happens at half the rate of direct outreach.

**Hook:** "Patients you discharged in April and May — when did they last hear from you? That's a September recall list. The practices with full fall schedules reached out to those patients this week."
