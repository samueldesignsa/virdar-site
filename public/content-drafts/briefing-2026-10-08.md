## One Insight

Home inspectors are an underserved segment for Virdar walk-ins. Most of the recent walk-in research has focused on healthcare and trades — dental, HVAC, roofing, PT, chiropractic. Home inspection is worth adding to the walk-in list as a specific target category, not just when you happen to pass one. Why: inspectors are small, owner-operated businesses with a simple revenue problem (response speed and agent relationship maintenance), they have a clear year-round need rather than a seasonal hook, and the blog post published today gives you immediate context for the conversation. The Warren Baird Inspection model in North Texas is one of many solo operators who have grown to 15–20 inspections a week purely on agent referrals but have never systematized that relationship. They're not being pitched by AI vendors. A direct walk-in conversation starting with "October is a busy real estate month — do you have a system for staying in front of your referring agents, or is it mostly whoever sends you clients?" is a low-resistance opener for a segment that hasn't been worked.
