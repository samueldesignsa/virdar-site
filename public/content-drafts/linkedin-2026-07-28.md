# LinkedIn Post — Monday, July 28, 2026

**Topic:** Two-a-days start today. The preparation window just closed.

---

Two-a-days start today in most of DFW.

The athletic trainers and sports medicine practices that sent their pre-season outreach last week have organized schedules right now. Families booked. Vision clearances in the queue. Sports physicals on the calendar. They went into today's demand with a plan.

The practices that didn't send anything are about to find out.

August is when independent health practices lose patients they shouldn't lose — not to worse care, but to whoever was first to show up in the inbox. The chain that ran the back-to-school mailer. The urgent care that confirmed same-day availability. The OD that sent a vision clearance reminder while the athletic physical was still being scheduled.

The window to reach those families proactively closed yesterday. After today, the conversation shifts from preparation to management.

If you run a practice that serves school-age athletes and you haven't done outreach yet — there's still this week. Most ISDs in Collin County start August 12. The two-week window before school starts is the last preparation window you have.
