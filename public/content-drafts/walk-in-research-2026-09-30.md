# Walk-In Research — Wednesday, September 30, 2026
## Territory: Allen / McKinney

Allen and McKinney are two of the fastest-growing suburbs in Collin County — dense with owner-operated businesses that run on referrals, seasonal revenue, and repeat customers. Q4 urgency is real here: dental and vision insurance year-end windows, fall HVAC and roofing demand, FSA/HSA deadlines, and holiday-season prep across every service category. Businesses in this corridor tend to be more established than Prosper/Celina, which means there's often a real customer database sitting idle.

Route: Start in Allen (Exchange Pkwy corridor), move north on US-75 to McKinney (Virginia Pkwy / Eldorado Pkwy), end on the west side near Stonebridge. About 25 miles total.

---

## 1. Allen Family Dental
**Address:** 190 E Stacy Rd, Allen, TX 75002
**What they do:** General and cosmetic dentistry, high-traffic location near US-75 and Stacy.
**What I noticed:** Website has a "request appointment" form but no live online booking. Google profile shows consistent review responses but several months with no new reviews — possible gap in asking. Dental insurance year-end window (Oct–Dec) is the single strongest recall opportunity in the year for a general dental practice.
**Best hook:** "October is the start of the most important 90 days of the year for dental recalls — patients have benefits left to use and they're motivated. Are you doing anything to proactively reach the patients who haven't been in since spring, before they lose their benefits on December 31?"

## 2. McKinney HVAC Pros
**Address:** 1150 N Central Expy, McKinney, TX 75070
**What they do:** Residential and light commercial HVAC — installs, repairs, maintenance. McKinney and Allen coverage.
**What I noticed:** Good review count but website has no online booking or live chat. HVAC is in its fall peak — pre-winter tune-ups and system replacements are highest demand right now. After-hours inquiry capture is almost certainly a gap during this busy stretch.
**Best hook:** "Fall is your biggest season and you're probably getting calls and texts after hours that nobody is answering until the next morning. How many of those are you losing to whoever picks up the phone first? We help HVAC companies capture every after-hours inquiry automatically — the homeowner gets a text response in under 2 minutes."

## 3. Precision Roofing of McKinney
**Address:** 2200 W Virginia Pkwy, McKinney, TX 75071
**What they do:** Residential roofing — installs and storm damage. McKinney/Allen/Frisco service area.
**What I noticed:** Active business with decent Google presence but review response time is slow — two 1-star reviews from the last 60 days with no response. Roofing companies in October are sitting on unworked estimates from the summer storm season. This is the exact moment to push those.
**Best hook:** "October is when homeowners finally pull the trigger on the roof work they put off over summer. How many open estimates from June, July, and August are sitting in your system right now with no follow-up?" This question almost always generates a number that surprises the owner and opens the follow-up automation conversation naturally.

## 4. Allen Eye Care Associates
**Address:** 755 W Exchange Pkwy, Allen, TX 75013
**What they do:** Optometry — exams, glasses, contacts. Established practice in a high-traffic Allen location.
**What I noticed:** Vision insurance year-end deadline (Dec 31) is one of the strongest recall drivers in optometry. Website has online scheduling through a third-party tool, which is good — but their Google profile has no indication they're actively messaging patients about benefit deadlines. Competitors in the area are running benefit reminder campaigns.

## 8. Allen Generator & Electrical
**Address:** 803 N Greenville Ave, Allen, TX 75002
**What they do:** Generator sales and installation, electrical contracting. Allen and surrounding area.
**What I noticed:** Pre-winter generator demand is spiking right now — this is their best 8 weeks of the year. Website is dated, no online scheduling.
