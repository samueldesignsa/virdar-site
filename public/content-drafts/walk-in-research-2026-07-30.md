# Walk-In Research — Thursday, July 30, 2026
## Territory: Flower Mound / Highland Village / Lewisville

**Why this corridor today:** Fresh territory not covered in recent weeks. Flower Mound and Highland Village are high-household-income suburbs with an exceptionally high concentration of independent health, fitness, and service businesses. This is a personal trainer's market — active families, dual-income households, people who have money and are trying to stay healthy. The blog published this morning (personal training/coaching) is the most relevant leave-behind in the library for this specific corridor. Back-to-school season is 2–3 weeks away across Lewisville ISD and Flower Mound's schools.

**Routing suggestion:** Start at the Highlands/FM 2499 corridor → cross to Long Prairie Road businesses → finish on Main Street Lewisville for the afternoon foot traffic.

## 8 Target Businesses

### 1. Lifetime Fitness / Independent Personal Training Studios — FM 2499 / Lakeside Pkwy Area, Flower Mound
**What they do:** Flower Mound has multiple independent personal trainers operating out of private studios or shared gym spaces along the FM 2499 corridor near Lakeside Business District.
**What to look for:** No automated package tracking, clients who "disappear" over summer and haven't been followed up, no formal referral process. Ask: "Do you have a way to reach clients who paused for summer before school starts?"
**Hook:** Summer-to-fall re-engagement (August message timing is right now — schools start August 19). Package expiration recovery. Referral capture at goal milestones.

### 2. Crossfit Flower Mound / Boutique Fitness Studio — Long Prairie Road, Flower Mound
**What they do:** Boutique fitness studio (CrossFit, Orangetheory, F45, or independent HIIT/strength concept) serving the Long Prairie corridor. These businesses typically run on membership models with attendance tracking.
**What to look for:** No system for flagging members whose attendance dropped from 4x/week to 1x/week in the last 30 days. Those members are 3–4 weeks from canceling and nobody has reached out.
**Hook:** Attendance-drop early warning sequence — reach members before they cancel, not after. Back-to-school member re-engagement for summer lapsers.

### 3. Flower Mound Family Dentistry or Pediatric Dental Practice — FM 1171 / Cross Timbers Road
**What they do:** Independent general or pediatric dental practice serving Flower Mound families. Back-to-school dental exams are the single biggest revenue window of the second half of the year for pediatric-heavy practices.
**What to look for:** No proactive outreach to overdue pediatric patients before school starts. Lewisville ISD starts August 19 — any family that hasn't scheduled a back-to-school exam has roughly 2.5 weeks of urgency left before the window closes.
**Hook:** "You have families in your system whose kids are overdue for a dental exam. School starts August 19. Those appointments need to happen before then, and the families don't know to call. Do you have a message going out this week?"

### 4. Chiropractic or Physical Therapy Practice — Highland Village / Lewisville Corridor
**What they do:** Independent chiropractic or PT practice serving the Highland Village and Lewisville area. Marcus High School (Flower Mound) and Lewisville High School both run two-a-days right now — Day 3.
**What to look for:** No relationship with school athletic trainers yet. Practices that haven't positioned themselves for the sports season injury referral pipeline are losing those referrals to the chiropractic chains that already reached out.
**Hook (today specifically):** "Two-a-days are in Day 3 right now at Marcus and Lewisville. The first wave of injuries happened Monday. Does the athletic trainer at Marcus know to send referrals here, or is that going to a practice that called them in June?"

### 5. Med Spa or Aesthetic Clinic — Lakeside Business District, Flower Mound
**What they do:** Independent med spa or aesthetic clinic in the Lakeside area. This demographic — Flower Mound women 35–55, dual-income households — is the core med spa clientele.
**What to look for:** No summer lapsed-client reactivation message. Aesthetic clients pause in summer (travel, heat, no personal time). The practices that fill September reach those clients in August, before the fall books itself.
**Hook:** "The clients who were coming in every 6–8 weeks in spring and stopped for summer are about to set their fall routines. Does anyone know who those clients are and is there a message going out this week?"

### 6. Dog Grooming Business — FM 1171 / Flower Mound Road
**What they do:** Independent dog grooming studio in Flower Mound. High pet-ownership area. Grooming businesses have strong rebooking behavior if prompted — clients who don't get a reminder at 6 or 8 weeks drift to whoever has an opening.
**What to look for:** No automated rebooking reminders. No lapsed client outreach for pets that haven't been in for 90+ days. The simple question: "When a client doesn't rebook before they leave, how do you follow up?"

### 7. Independent Auto Repair — Main Street / Stemmons Freeway Corridor, Lewisville
**What they do:** Independent auto repair shop in Lewisville. Higher traffic area, more transactional clientele than north Flower Mound, but declined service follow-up is a consistent revenue leak across all shop types.
**What to look for:** No system for following up declined services. August is a strong window for pre-fall maintenance — families getting cars ready for school-year driving schedules.

### 8. Tutoring Center or Learning Studio — FM 3040 / Long Prairie Road, Flower Mound
**What they do:** Independent tutoring or learning center in the FM 3040 corridor. Flower Mound families are high-education-priority — tutoring center density here is above average. Back-to-school enrollment season is the single most important revenue window of the year.
**What to look for:** No proactive outreach to returning families from last year. Lewisville ISD starts August 19 — the families who enrolled last fall haven't been contacted yet about fall enrollment. Competitors have.

## Key Hooks for Today

**Universal across every stop:**
- "School starts August 19 for Lewisville ISD — what are you doing in the next two weeks to reach customers/clients/patients before that routine sets?"
- "Who in your database came in this spring and hasn't been back? Are they hearing from you before they set their fall schedule with someone else?"

**Health practice-specific:**
- Two-a-days are live at Marcus and Lewisville High. Day 3. The sports injury referral pipeline is already moving.
- Insurance benefit window: Q3 is open. Patients who came in Q1 and Q2 have unused coverage through December. None of them have been told.

**Fitness/coaching-specific:**
- Summer client re-engagement window closes when school starts. The August message goes out now or it doesn't go out.
