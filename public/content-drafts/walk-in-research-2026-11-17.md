# Walk-In Research — Monday, November 17, 2026
## Territory: Grapevine / Colleyville, TX

Today's route covers the Grapevine and Colleyville corridor — Main Street Grapevine, the Northwest Highway / Hwy 26 strip, and the Colleyville Town Center / Boulevard 26 area. Thanksgiving is 9 days out. Grapevine bills itself as the Christmas Capital of Texas, and that's not marketing fiction — the independent business culture there is strong, the owner demographics skew toward people who've been in place 10+ years, and they're in the middle of their most important revenue stretch of the year. The pitch practically writes itself.

Colleyville is next door and even higher income — smaller commercial footprint, but the businesses that are there have been there forever and their clients have money to spend. Neither market has been saturated by AI automation pitches yet.

**Best hook for this territory today:** Thanksgiving pre-order urgency + holiday booking window. For food businesses: "pre-orders close this week." For beauty/service: "every open slot from now through December 20 is a slot that fills or stays empty." For medical/dental: FSA and year-end benefits.
