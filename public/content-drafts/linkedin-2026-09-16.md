# LinkedIn Post — Wednesday, September 16, 2026

---

An irrigation contractor in Frisco had 89 active customers who needed their systems blown out before winter.

He'd done their spring startups. He had every phone number. He knew exactly when the work needed to happen.

In previous years, he'd get calls in late October when the first cold front hit — customers panicking, schedule already slammed, some of them going to whoever could come this week instead of him.

This year he sent a message in mid-September: "Fall is coming and our blowout schedule is filling up. We typically get booked out 3–4 weeks in October — if you want to lock in your preferred week, reply and we'll hold the slot."

72% of his customers scheduled within two weeks.

The year before, 34% had scheduled by the same date.

Same customers. Same service. The only difference was a message that went out six weeks earlier, before the panic started.

The customers were always going to need the service. The question was whether they'd call him or call whoever they found when they finally remembered.
