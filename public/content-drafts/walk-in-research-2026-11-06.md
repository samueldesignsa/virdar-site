# Walk-In Research — Friday, November 6, 2026
## Area: Hurst / Euless / Bedford (Mid-Cities, Tarrant County)

The mid-cities corridor runs along Highway 183 and Pipeline Road between DFW Airport and Fort Worth. Dense with independent medical, dental, and service businesses that have been in the same locations for 10–20 years. Strong base of established owners who don't typically hear from automation vendors. Northeast Tarrant County has not been worked yet this month.

**Suggested route**: Start at Bedford Rd/183 corridor in Bedford, work west through Euless toward SH-121, then north to Hurst and the North East Mall area. 2–3 hours with 5–7 stops.

---

### 1. Bedford Optometry / Eye Care Clinic
**Area**: Bedford Rd / L Don Dodson Dr corridor, Bedford  
**What they do**: Independent optometry practice, 1–2 doctors, mix of eye exams and retail optical (frames, contacts)

**What to notice**: This is the highest-urgency prospect in the area right now. FSA balances expire December 31. Vision insurance benefits reset January 1. Most patients have both — two separate expiration deadlines on the same calendar. Practices in this corridor almost certainly have not sent the double-deadline message yet. If you can see any signage or a website with no "schedule your year-end exam" prompt, the gap is confirmed.

**Hook**: "Your patients have two deadlines running right now — FSA and their vision benefit. One message, sent this week, would fill your November. I can show you what it looks like."

---

### 2. Euless Family Dental
**Area**: W Euless Blvd / Westpark Way corridor, Euless  
**What they do**: General dentistry, family practice, 2–3 chairs, insurance-heavy

**What to notice**: Year-end dental benefits are the strongest seasonal hook in dentistry. Most patients have $1,000–$2,000 in annual dental benefits that expire December 31. Practices that don't actively remind patients of this lose the revenue. With 7–8 weeks left, there's still time to fill the schedule — but the window is closing. Check if their Google listing shows any "year-end" messaging or availability notes.

**Hook**: "Every dentist in this corridor has patients sitting on unused dental benefits that expire in 55 days. The ones who send a reminder this week will be fully booked in November. I can tell you exactly what to send."

---

### 3. HEB Physical Therapy (Hurst-Euless-Bedford area)
**Area**: Precinct Line Rd / Brown Trail area, Hurst  
**What they do**: Independent physical therapy practice, workers comp and cash-pay mix

**What to notice**: PT practices lose patients between discharge and re-injury. Patients who completed a treatment plan 6–12 months ago often need PT again but don't come back — they go wherever is most convenient at the time of their new injury or referral. No one contacts them between episodes. FSA is also relevant here — PT is an FSA-eligible expense, and patients with remaining balances might prepay or schedule preventive sessions.

**Hook**: "You have former patients who are going to need PT again in the next year. Right now they don't have a reason to come back to you specifically. We can fix that — and use the FSA deadline to bring them in this month."

---

### 4. Bedford Chiropractic & Wellness
**Area**: Harwood Rd / Airport Fwy area, Bedford  
**What they do**: Independent chiropractic, cash-pay and insurance mix, possibly adding massage or wellness services

**What to notice**: Chiropractic has a strong FSA angle — adjustments are FSA-eligible. Year-end is when patients realize they have $200–$400 sitting in their FSA that expires in 55 days. Most chiropractic practices do not run any outreach around this window. If the practice has a Google listing with 50+ reviews and no recent posts mentioning FSA, they haven't activated this.

**Hook**: "Chiropractic is one of the top FSA spend categories in November and December. Your patients have money earmarked for exactly what you do, and it expires December 31. Have you sent them anything about it?"

### 5. Mid-Cities Medical Spa or Aesthetics Clinic
**Area**: Precinct Line Rd / 820 corridor, North Richland Hills / Hurst  
**What they do**: Aesthetic treatments — Botox, filler, laser, body contouring — independent or franchise location

**What to notice**: Three revenue windows are open simultaneously right now for med spas: (1) FSA balance spending — cosmetic treatments ARE eligible for FSA in certain categories, (2) holiday social prep — Thanksgiving and holiday parties drive treatment demand in November, (3) gift cards — the highest-margin product a med spa sells. Most med spas don't have an automated campaign running any of these simultaneously.

**Hook**: "You have three reasons to reach your patient list this month: FSA, holiday prep, and gift cards. Most med spas are running maybe one of these. I can show you what running all three looks like — it's one campaign, three angles."

---

### 6. Euless Hair Salon or Blowout Bar
**Area**: Main Street / Euless area or W Euless Blvd  
**What they do**: Independent hair salon or blowout bar, appointment-based, local clientele

**What to notice**: Hair salons are in their peak season right now — Thanksgiving and holiday events drive appointment demand through December. Salons that don't have a reactivation campaign running for clients who haven't been in since summer are leaving November/December appointments unfilled. Check if their booking system is visible online and whether they're showing availability in the next two weeks.

**Hook**: "Every client who came in for summer highlights and hasn't been back — they're thinking about their hair for Thanksgiving and Christmas parties right now. Have you reached out to them? That's a November full of appointments sitting in your contact list."

---

### 7. Bedford or Hurst Auto Detailing
**Area**: Airport Fwy / 183 service road, Bedford or Euless  
**What they do**: Independent auto detailing shop, mobile or fixed location, mix of individual and fleet customers

**What to notice**: Year-end is a strong booking period for auto detailing — people want their vehicles clean before holiday travel and family visits. Corporate and fleet customers sometimes have year-end budgets to spend before December 31. Detailing shops in this corridor rarely have any outreach system beyond word-of-mouth and Google.

**Hook**: "November and December are when people want their car clean for the holidays. The shop that reminds their past customers this week is going to be booked. Do you have a way to reach everyone who's been in before?"
