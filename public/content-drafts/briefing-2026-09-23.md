This is a high-income suburban market — households spending $120K+ per year — where independent medical and dental practices are the dominant business type. Every dental, ortho, PT, and chiropractic practice in this corridor has the same three revenue gaps: recall patients who lapsed without follow-up, new leads that went cold, and a review profile that hasn't grown in months. The pitch practically writes itself.

More importantly: affluent suburban practices have the margins to pay for automation, and they have enough patient volume to see the ROI fast. A dental practice doing 80 appointments per week and converting even 10 extra patients per month from reactivation is adding $6,000 to $9,000 per month in revenue. That math makes the conversation easy to close. If the Flower Mound walk-ins go well today, this corridor is worth a return visit focused specifically on the dental and medical cluster on Justin Rd.
