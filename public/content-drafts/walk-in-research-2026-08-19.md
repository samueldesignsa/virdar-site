**Territory: Frisco / Prosper**

## Why This Territory Today

Frisco and Prosper are among the highest-income, fastest-growing corridors in DFW — and they haven't been in the walk-in cycle recently. Frisco is mature and dense, with a wide range of independent healthcare, wellness, and professional service businesses competing against each other for the same high-income patient base. Prosper is newer and still building its provider mix, which means many businesses are less than 5 years old and still figuring out how to retain the patients they're acquiring.

**Two different pitches:**
- **Frisco pitch (competitive, retention focus):** "Frisco has 20 options for every service. The practices that hold onto patients are the ones that stay in front of them between appointments. The ones that let the relationship go quiet lose patients to whoever shows up in their Instagram feed first."
- **Prosper pitch (growth, acquisition focus):** "Prosper is adding families every month. Those families are actively choosing a dentist, a pediatrician, a PT clinic right now — often for the first time in this area. The practices that respond to new patient inquiries within the hour get those relationships. The ones that call back the next day compete against whoever already scheduled them."

**Fall angle (applies everywhere today):** School started last week. Q4 is six weeks out. Insurance benefits — dental, vision, FSAs — are in the active burn window: families who haven't used what they've paid for are starting to notice. Any healthcare practice Beverly walks into today can be pitched on the same urgency: "Your patients have 4.5 months of remaining 2026 benefits. The most valuable message a practice can send right now is 'you have coverage you haven't used and haven't been in since spring.' Do you have a process for identifying and reaching those patients?"

### 2. Frisco Orthodontics (independent, Frisco, TX — Preston Rd or Main St area)

**What they do:** Independent orthodontic practice, traditional braces and Invisalign, competitive Frisco market.

**What I noticed:** Back-to-school is peak consult season for orthodontics — parents who waited until summer is over are ready to move now. Independent orthodontic offices in Frisco face heavy competition from Smile Doctors, Orthodontic Associates, and other multi-location chains. The chains have intake systems; the independent offices mostly rely on the front desk and hope. A new consult inquiry that arrives at noon on a Tuesday and doesn't get called back until 4pm has already toured two other offices online by then.

**Hook:** Speed-to-response for new consult inquiries. "When a parent fills out your new patient form at 1pm on a school day, what happens? Walk me through the process. The answer tells you whether you're getting the consultation or your competitor is."
