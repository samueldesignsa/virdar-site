## Key Hooks for Today

**Universal across every stop:**
- "Plano ISD starts August 12. That's next Tuesday. Every practice and business in this corridor with a patient or client list should have a message going out right now. What does yours look like?"
- "Fall routine sets this week. The clients who hear from you first are the ones who reschedule with you. The ones who don't hear from you are the ones who look for alternatives."

**Back-to-school angle (dental and pediatric medical):**
- Plano ISD is one of the largest school districts in Texas. The volume of families needing sports physicals and back-to-school checkups in the next ten days is significant. Practices that haven't proactively reached their own patient panel are competing on availability against practices that did.

**Sports injury angle (orthopedic, PT, chiro):**
- Plano ISD fall sports begin next week. Baseball, tennis, cross country, volleyball — high-volume sports injury categories. The independent practice that has a referral relationship with school athletic trainers before the season starts captures those patients. The one that waits until after the first injury is competing with whoever the trainer already knows.

**Legacy corporate demographic:**
- The Legacy / DNT corridor has a uniquely high density of dual-income professionals who will maintain long-term relationships with service providers who stay in contact. The fitness studio, med spa, salon, and financial advisor pitches all land harder here than in most suburban territories.
