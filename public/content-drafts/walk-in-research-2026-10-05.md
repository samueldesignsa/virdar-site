# Walk-In Research — Monday, October 5, 2026
## Territory: Celina / Anna / Melissa

Celina, Anna, and Melissa are the fastest-growing cities in Collin County right now — all three have added significant commercial density in the last three years as the residential build-out has pulled retail, medical, and service businesses north from Frisco and McKinney. The business landscape is newer, which means owners are less entrenched with existing vendors and more open to conversations about growth infrastructure. Vendor saturation is significantly lower than Plano, Frisco, or McKinney — a walk-in here gets more time with the owner than almost anywhere else in the Metroplex. Businesses in this corridor tend to be in the $600K–$2M revenue range, owner-operated, and still actively building their systems rather than defending the status quo.

Route: Take the Dallas North Tollway north through Frisco and into Celina (Preston Rd/Hwy 289). Morning cluster along Preston Rd in Celina. Mid-morning cut east on Hwy 380 to Anna. Afternoon south on US-75 to Melissa. About 40 miles total, straightforward surface roads.

## 1. Celina Family Dentistry
**Address:** 2591 S Preston Rd, Celina, TX 75009
**What they do:** General dentistry — cleanings, restorations, cosmetics, and family care for the growing Celina residential community.
**What I noticed:** October is the highest-leverage recall window for dental practices — year-end insurance benefits expire December 31 and patients who've been putting off care are now motivated to book before they lose coverage. Celina practices like this one are seeing rapid patient growth from residential expansion, which means a large segment of their patient base is relatively new and has no established recall habit. New patients who came in once and haven't been back are sitting in the database unused.
**Best hook:** "October is the start of the strongest recall window dental practices see all year — patients with benefits expiring December 31 are actually motivated right now. Are you doing anything proactive to reach patients who haven't been in since spring, or is that mostly inbound from here? The practices that reach out first fill their November before the last-minute crunch starts."

## 2. Celina Air Conditioning & Heating
**Address:** 317 W Walnut St, Celina, TX 75009
**What they do:** Residential HVAC service, installation, and repair. Covers Celina, Gunter, and Van Alstyne.
**What I noticed:** Fall is the busiest tune-up and early replacement window in North Texas. Homeowners in Celina's newer developments are in homes that are 3–7 years old — approaching the first major HVAC decision point for a lot of systems. After-hours inquiry capture is the live gap for any HVAC company right now. A homeowner who turns the furnace on for the first time in October and notices something wrong is calling at 8pm, not 9am.
**Best hook:** "This is your peak inquiry window for fall tune-ups and first system replacements for newer homes. How are you handling after-hours contact right now? If someone fills your form at 9pm because their furnace just kicked on weird — does something go back to them that night, or does that wait until morning? In October, whoever responds first gets the job."

## 3. Prosper-Celina Chiropractic
**Address:** 1800 W Outer Loop, Celina, TX 75009
**What they do:** Chiropractic adjustments, sports injury treatment, and wellness care. Serves Celina and northern Prosper.
**What I noticed:** FSA and HSA balances expire December 31 — patients sitting on pre-tax health funds are motivated to use them in Q4. Fall sports injuries (high school football, soccer, cross-country) are also peaking in October. Celina is a high-school-sports-density area — Celina ISD has a well-known football program, and the fall injury volume is real. Chiropractic practices in growing suburbs often under-invest in recall because new patient volume masks the retention gap.
**Best hook:** "Two things are running in your favor right now: FSA money patients need to spend before year-end, and fall sports injuries from Celina ISD and the surrounding schools peaking this month. The practices that reach lapsed patients now — 'your FSA expires soon, here's how to use it' — fill their October and November without waiting for the phones to ring. Are you doing outreach to past patients right now, or more inbound from here?"

## 6. North Texas Roofing (Anna/Melissa area)
**Address:** 511 E White St, Anna, TX 75409
**What they do:** Residential roofing, gutters, and storm damage. Covers Collin County north — Anna, Melissa, Van Alstyne, Sherman.
**What I noticed:** October is the follow-up window for summer estimates — roofing companies that gave quotes in June through August are sitting on a pipeline of homeowners who didn't pull the trigger during the summer heat. Those homeowners are now in the mindset to act before year-end. Smaller roofing companies in Anna and Melissa also tend to have thin Google profiles — a review push to past customers would have high impact in a market this size.

The companies following up right now are closing jobs their competitors don't know are still available. If those estimates are sitting somewhere — a spreadsheet, a CRM, even a stack of printed forms — there's a straightforward way to reach all of them in one push this week."

## 7. Melissa Family Medicine
