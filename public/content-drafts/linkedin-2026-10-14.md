# LinkedIn Post — Wednesday, October 14, 2026

**Topic:** Your reputation earns the click. Your response time determines whether you get the job.
**Target length:** ~165 words
**Post time:** 8–9am CT

---

A business owner I talked to recently has a 4.8-star rating and 140 genuine reviews. Earned over years. Every one of them deserved.

When someone finds his business through Google at 2pm on a Tuesday and fills out the contact form, his typical response time is 3–4 hours. Sometimes the end of the day.

Two miles away, a competitor with 3.6 stars and 40 reviews texts back in under 10 minutes. Every time.

The customer who submitted that form called the faster one at 3pm and booked the job.

Your reputation earns the click. Your response time determines whether you get the job.

This is one of the most consistent patterns in service businesses. The highest-quality operator is not always the one who wins. The operator who responds first wins — most of the time, regardless of reviews, regardless of price.

Most business owners have no idea what their average response time actually is.

What's yours?
