Thanksgiving is Thursday.

The business owners with full holiday calendars didn't do anything magical. They reached out to their past customers two to three weeks ago — when there was still room on the calendar — and said: "The holidays are coming. Here's what we have available. Here's how to book."

That's it. One message to the people who already trust them.

The owners scrambling this week are trying to book Thanksgiving-week slots on a 72-hour window. That's not a marketing problem. It's a timing problem. And timing is the one thing you can't fix with a last-minute push.

Here's what this week tells you about January: the same math applies. The businesses with full January calendars will reach out to past customers in December, while they're still in the glow of the holidays, before the gap in the calendar has time to feel urgent.

If you have customers who bought from you this year and haven't heard from you in a while — that's your most underworked asset going into the holiday season.

What's your outreach to past customers looked like in the last 30 days?
