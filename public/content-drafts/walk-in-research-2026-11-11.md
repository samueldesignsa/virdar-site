# Walk-In Research — Wednesday, November 11, 2026
## Area: Plano — Preston Road / Legacy Drive / Parker Road Corridor (West Plano)

West Plano is one of the highest-income suburban markets in Texas. The Preston Road corridor from Parker Road north to Headquarters Drive runs through zip codes 75093, 75024, and 75025 — a concentration of independent medical, dental, and specialty service businesses serving a population with strong employer benefits packages and significant disposable income. This corridor has not been covered recently and is worth a full day.

Today's angle: 15 days to Thanksgiving, 50 days to December 31. The FSA/benefits deadline is the primary hook for medical and dental businesses. The Thanksgiving travel window is the primary hook for pet care businesses. Both are extremely timely today.

**Suggested route**: Start at Preston/Parker intersection, work north on Preston to Legacy Drive, then east on Headquarters Drive. 2–3 hours, 5–7 stops. Most of the commercial development here is in professional office parks and strip centers — parking is easy and businesses are walkable within each node.

### 1. Preston Park Village Dental (or similar independent dental practice on Preston Rd)
**Area**: Preston Rd / Parker Rd area, Plano 75093  
**What they do**: General and family dentistry, 2–4 chairs, mix of PPO insurance and fee-for-service patients

**What to notice**: West Plano patients are disproportionately employed by large companies (Toyota, Fidelity, JPMorgan, Liberty Mutual, and dozens of regional employers nearby). Employer dental benefits — typically $1,000–$2,000/year — reset January 1. Many of these patients have a remaining balance they haven't used. 50 days left. Check their Google listing: do they have any year-end messaging? Open appointment slots? If you can see daytime availability in the next two weeks on their online booking, that's direct evidence the reminder hasn't gone out.

**Hook**: "Your patients in this area are largely corporate employees with comprehensive dental plans — $1,000 to $2,000 that resets January 1. The ones who have benefits left aren't lazy, they just haven't been reminded. One message this week fills your November. The practices that send it are booked. The ones that don't are calling patients in December."

## Route Notes

**Preston/Parker node** (75093): Dense with independent medical, dental, and professional service businesses. This is a mature commercial corridor — businesses here tend to be owner-operated and have been there 5–15 years. High-income clientele.

**Legacy/Headquarters node** (75024): Corporate-adjacent. Businesses here serve a mix of local residents and daytime corporate employees. Med spas, restaurants, and specialty retail skew premium. The holiday party and FSA pitches land particularly well because the clientele has the budget and the deadlines.

**Plano note**: Wednesday is a strong walk-in day — decision-makers are in-office mid-week, and businesses are past the Monday urgency and not yet thinking about Friday logistics. Mid-morning (9:30–11:30am) before lunch traffic picks up is the sweet spot for this corridor.

*Verify addresses with Google Maps before heading out. The Legacy/Headquarters area has significant construction and parking lot access changes from the past year. Plan an extra 10 minutes for navigation.*
