## Why This Territory Today

Burleson, Crowley, and Joshua sit in Johnson County, directly south of Fort Worth. They haven't been in the walk-in cycle yet. This corridor is distinct from the DFW suburbs covered in recent weeks — it's more blue-collar and working-class than Frisco or Southlake, with a strong owner-operated business culture, practices that have been in the same location for 10–20 years, and zero DSO or franchise consolidation on most streets.

The demographic is a mix of longtime Texas families, trades workers, and young families who moved south for affordable homes. Business owners in Burleson tend to be hands-on and price-sensitive — they respond better to ROI specifics than to "efficiency" framing. Lead with the dollar number, then explain the system.

Burleson has a solid commercial corridor along NW John Jones Dr and SW Wilshire Blvd. Crowley is tight but has a clear Business 1187 strip. Joshua is small-town — a handful of independent shops that have been there for decades.

**Drive time from central DFW:** 30–40 minutes south on I-35W.

**For healthcare stops (all of them):**

**For Joshua Hearing Center specifically (the standout stop):**
"I wrote a post this morning specifically about audiology practices and the five places they lose revenue between visits. The device aging section — patients with five-year-old aids who haven't been told what's changed — is probably your biggest untapped opportunity right now. Can I show you what that looks like on virdar.co?"

**For home services (Comfort Zone HVAC, Lone Star Pest):**
"Fall service season is three weeks out. The contractors who are fully booked in October are reaching their existing customers right now — before the rush — with a specific offer. What does your August outreach to existing customers look like?"

**For auto repair (Crowley Auto):**
"You serviced customers all spring and summer. You have their car, their mileage, and their phone number. The customers due for service right now don't know they're due unless you tell them. Does your system send that message, or are you waiting for them to remember?"

## Burleson Market Notes

- Business owners here are practical and direct. Skip the AI buzzwords entirely. Talk in dollars, time, and customer names.
- Price sensitivity is higher than in Plano or Frisco — but they're not opposed to spending money if the ROI is clear. "This pays for itself in the first two months" is the close.
- Many practices are bilingual or serve bilingual families. If you get into a conversation about after-hours inquiries, the Spanish-language response angle is relevant — a lot of Johnson County is bilingual.
- Fall is a strong natural selling season for home services, healthcare (benefits expiring), and auto repair (back-to-routine). The season alignment is on your side today.
