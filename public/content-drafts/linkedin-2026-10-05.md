Healthcare savings accounts expire December 31.

Most patients with FSA or HSA balances are sitting on $800–$2,000 they're about to lose. They know it. They just haven't done anything about it yet.

The practices filling their November and December schedules right now are the ones that already reached those patients with a simple message: "You have benefits expiring soon. Here's how to use them before year-end."

The practices that wait for patients to remember on their own will see a flurry of calls in late November — frantic, schedule-scrambling, last-minute. Some will get in. A lot won't.

This applies to chiropractic, physical therapy, optometry, audiology, and any other practice where people pay for care using pre-tax benefits.

You don't need to run a campaign. You need to send one message to your patient list this week.

The ones who engage book in October. The ones who ignore it would have ignored you in November too.

Is your practice reaching FSA patients proactively right now?
